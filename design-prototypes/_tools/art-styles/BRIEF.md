## How the page is built

Read `_tools/build-art-styles.mjs` (short) first. The generator takes 3b's markup (`_tools/hex-src/index.html` homepage, `_tools/hex-src/tokens-sheet.html` token sheet) unchanged, 3b's whole stylesheet (`option-3b-hex-soft/styles.css`, about 2600 lines: read it, your overlay targets its selectors), and appends your `style.css`. Tokens: your `tokens.json` is a partial DTCG file merged over `option-3b-hex-soft/tokens.json` (colour groups need `"$type": "color"` on `color`; hex strings are fine, the generator normalizes them; override light AND dark; you may add colour keys, and add `$extensions["com.hummlist"].contrastPairs` for new fg/bg pairs you rely on). Fonts are declared in `tokens.json` `font.family.*` (already filled in) and become `@font-face` automatically. Type sizes/weights/tracking live in `type.*` tokens: override them there, not only in CSS, so the token sheet stays true.

3b's bee mascot is gone: every `url("assets/bee.svg")` now points at your `assets/motif.svg`, and the header/footer logo uses your `assets/mark.svg`. Both are required and currently missing or placeholders. **No bee mascot, no insect, no wings, antennae or striped bug body.** Hexagons, the letterform H, stripes as pattern, list lines and checkmarks are fine. `mark.svg` is the Hummlist brand mark (48 px square box in the header, scales to 64+): a soft-cornered hexagon is the core; make it carry your art style. `motif.svg` fills the spots where the bee was (hero corner `.bee-flight .bee`, `.flight-divider .bee`, `.route-bee`, `.privacy-bee`, `.cta-bee`, `.store-shot-bee`): e.g. a small hex cluster, a hex with a check, a stack of list hexes, in your style. You may also restyle or hide those elements and the dotted `.flight` paths (they were the bee's flight lines) if your style wants something else. You can override `assets/comb-light.svg` / `comb-dark.svg` (3b's honeycomb backdrop) by putting your own files with those names in your `assets/`.

CSS rules (the checker enforces them): no `data:` URLs, no external URLs, no `@import`, no inline styles or scripts in HTML (you can't touch HTML anyway). Textures go in SVG files in `assets/` (an SVG with `feTurbulence` used as `background-image` works; CSS gradients and patterns work). The site CSP is `default-src 'self'`.

## Keep 3b's identity and quality bar

- Same structure, sections and screens as 3b (header, hero with app phone mock and stickers, the three features, voice section, privacy checklist, CTA, footer; token sheet with swatches, type, hex form and depth, measures, motion, and the four "Anwendungen": app, email, store image, Alexa card). Mobile 390 px and desktop 1440 px, light and dark (`prefers-color-scheme`).
- The soft-cornered hexagon stays the brand shape (buttons, chips, check boxes, avatars, badges are generated hex masks; keep them hexagons). Tactile `.hex` buttons: the face must still sink by exactly its edge `--d` on press (you may change `depth.*` tokens, e.g. a flatter 4 px edge, but not to 0). 48 px minimum targets. A visible focus ring on every tab stop. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` nothing animates (3b already handles this; keep any animation you add inside a `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` query).
- WCAG AA: 4.5:1 text, 3:1 UI lines and large text; axe-core zero violations. Text over textures, glass or images must stay readable (`contrast-review.mjs` measures pixels).
- Copy: German, du-form. The brand is always "Hummlist", never "Humm" alone. `style.json` holds the token sheet prose you must write: `lead` (one or two sentences on the style), `colorNote` (how colours are used), `colorNotes` (optional map colour key -> short German description shown under the swatch), `shadows` (3 labels for the shadow samples, e.g. "sticker-s · 2/2/0 Tinte"), `shadowNote`, `captions.app|email|store` (figure captions that describe your style). Write plain, short, no marketing fluff.
- It must look like a deliberate, polished, professional design in that art style, clearly different from 3b and from the other styles, not "3b with a filter". Change type scale, spacing, surfaces, borders, shadows, textures, layout emphasis as your style needs. Do it with craft: consistent rhythm, nothing broken, overlapping or clipped at either width.
