# Help and contact

Snacklist is a shared shopping list for one household. Whatever one person adds, everyone else in the household sees right away. Snacklist sorts each item into its category. It runs on iPhone and Android.

## How to get help

- **In the app:** tap **More**, then **Send feedback**. On your phone you can also shake it on the screen where the problem happens. Describe what happened or what you are missing. The app version, device, language and the screen you are on are sent along, but no list items. This is the fastest way.
- **By email:** [support@snacklist.app](mailto:support@snacklist.app). Please say which device you use (iPhone, Android or browser).

Who is responsible for Snacklist is in the [imprint](/en/imprint). How Snacklist handles your data is in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy).

## Common questions

### What happens with my email address?

We use it for the waitlist and the beta invite, and, if you ticked the box, for one email when the app launches. For the invite it goes to Apple or Google. Details are in the [privacy policy](/en/privacy).

### How do I install the beta on iPhone?

Your invite comes by email from Apple (TestFlight), sent to the address you used for the waitlist. First, get Apple's free TestFlight app from the App Store. Then open the invite email on your iPhone, tap the button in it, and install Snacklist in TestFlight. There's no public link.

### How do I install the beta on Android?

Your invite email has a "Join the beta" button. Open it on your phone, with the Play Store signed in to the same Google account as the email address the invite went to. Accept there, then install Snacklist from the Play Store. After that, updates arrive as normal Play Store updates.

### I signed up with the wrong email. What now?

The invite only works with the email of your Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android). Just sign up again on [snacklist.app/en](/en) with the right address.

### My invite hasn't arrived. What can I do?

Check your spam folder, and in Gmail the Promotions tab too. We send invites in waves. On iPhone, the TestFlight email comes from Apple, not from Snacklist. If it's still missing, write to [support@snacklist.app](mailto:support@snacklist.app).

### How do I invite my partner?

Tap **More**, then **Invite your partner**. Share the link or the code. An invite is valid for one person. You can revoke open invites there too.

### How do I join a household with a code?

Open the invite link you received, or tap your household's name at the top of the list, then **Join with a code**, and enter the code. Snacklist shows you which household you are joining first. If you are brand new, choose **Join household** right after signing in.

### How do I export my data?

Tap **More**, then **Account** and **Export my data**. You get everything stored about you as a JSON file.

### How do I delete my account?

Tap **More**, then **Account** and **Delete account**, and confirm by typing DELETE. Your account, your profile, your access tokens and connected apps are deleted right away. If others stay in the household, they keep the list; your items stay there without your name. If you are the last member, the whole household is deleted, Snacklist Lifetime included; the purchase can't be moved to another household. Whether you can get your money back is under "Can I get a refund for Snacklist Lifetime?". Households you archived are deleted too. If others stay in the household, Snacklist Lifetime stays with them. Deleted data stays in our encrypted backups for up to 90 more days, then it is gone there too.

Without the app: write to [support@snacklist.app](mailto:support@snacklist.app).

### What happens to Snacklist Lifetime if I leave a household as its last member?

Snacklist Lifetime belongs to the household and does not move with you. If you leave a household with Snacklist Lifetime as its last member, it is deleted. The app warns you first: Snacklist Lifetime ends with the household. For refunds, see "Can I get a refund for Snacklist Lifetime?".

### Can I get a refund for Snacklist Lifetime?

You buy Snacklist Lifetime from Apple or Google, not from us. So:

- **iPhone (App Store):** only Apple can refund. Request it at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) ("I'd like to" › "Request a refund"); how it works: [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223). We can't refund App Store purchases ourselves.
- **Android (Google Play):** within 48 hours of buying, request the refund from Google directly; Google decides ([Google Play refund policies](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/15574908?hl=en)). After that, email us at [support@snacklist.app](mailto:support@snacklist.app) with the order number from Google's purchase confirmation (it starts with "GPA.") or the email address of your Google account. We look at each request individually and, where we agree, refund through Google Play.

If a purchase is refunded, the unlock ends for the household it was bought for, for all its members, unless the household bought Snacklist Lifetime more than once.

Our refunds after the 48 hours are voluntary. They don't affect your statutory rights, also after the 48 hours, for example if Snacklist Lifetime doesn't work as described or if you can withdraw from the purchase. You have these rights against Apple or Google, who sell you Snacklist Lifetime; details are in [Apple's terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/) or [Google Play's terms](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/). Feel free to write to us in that case too; we'll help.

### How do I turn off usage data?

Tap **More**, then **Usage data**, and turn off **Send usage data and error reports**. From then on the app sends nothing to PostHog. You can turn it back on at any time.

### Can I add items with Siri?

::: until native-siri
Yes, with a Shortcut you set up once yourself in the Shortcuts app. Right now this only works in German; the guide [Snacklist with Siri](/en/guides/siri-shortcut) explains why and links to the German setup steps. Apple turns your speech into text; we only receive the text, not a recording. Section 7 of the [privacy policy](/en/privacy) has the details. Using Siri with Snacklist without a Shortcut is planned but not available yet.
:::

::: once native-siri
Yes. On iPhone with iOS 17 or later you can do it directly with the Snacklist app, with no shortcut of your own, as soon as you're signed in and in a household: say for example "Add to Snacklist", or ask Siri to read out your list. It works in English and German. Apple turns your speech into text; we only receive the text, not a recording. Section 7 of the [privacy policy](/en/privacy) has the details. You can still use the older way with your own shortcut, but you no longer need it.
:::

### Can I add items with Alexa?

Yes, with the Snacklist skill for Amazon Alexa. Enable it in the Alexa app and link it to your Snacklist account. Then say, for example, "Alexa, ask Snacklist to add milk and eggs" or "Alexa, ask Snacklist what's on the list". The skill understands English and German, depending on the language your Alexa device is set to. To end the connection, disable the skill in the Alexa app or disconnect **Amazon Alexa** in Snacklist under **More** → **Access**. The skill is still in a beta test and not yet available to everyone; Peter sends you the invitation link. The guide [Snacklist with Alexa](/en/guides/alexa) explains more.

### Can I use Snacklist with an AI assistant?

Yes, with AI assistants that can add their own MCP servers, through Snacklist's MCP server. Whether your assistant can, and on which plan, is up to its provider. There's a step-by-step guide for [Claude](/en/guides/claude). Tap **More**, then **Access**. The MCP server's address is shown there. You connect the assistant by signing in or with an access token. Under **Connected apps** and **Access tokens** you see every connection and can revoke it at any time.

### I test Snacklist through TestFlight or Google Play. What should I know?

Snacklist is still in a closed test for family and friends. On iPhone the app comes through TestFlight, on Android through Google Play's internal test; updates arrive there by themselves. Peter sends you the invite link to the test. Bugs and ideas are best sent straight from the app with **Send feedback**.
