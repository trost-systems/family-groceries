# Privacy: buying Hummlist Lifetime

Draft. Once Peter approves it, this text becomes section "5a. Buying Hummlist Lifetime" of the [privacy policy](/en/privacy) and this page goes away. Until then Hummlist sells nothing.

## 5a. Buying Hummlist Lifetime

In the iPhone and Android apps you can buy Hummlist Lifetime, once, for your whole household. You buy it in Apple's App Store or on Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment and process your payment and account data as independent controllers under their own privacy policies; Apple acts as commissionaire, Google as the merchant of record. We receive no payment data and no name or email address of your Apple or Google account.

After the purchase the app sends the receipt the store signed to our server (Supabase, Frankfurt). We check it, where needed directly with Apple (App Store Server API) or Google (Google Play Developer API), and then store: the store, the product, the store's transaction number, whether it was a test purchase, when it was bought and, if so, refunded, the price, currency and country, which account confirmed the purchase first and which household it unlocks. All members of your household see that the household is unlocked. If Apple or Google refund the purchase, we learn this through their notifications or a daily query, and the unlock ends.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract, the unlock you bought); for the transaction number also Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our interest that a purchase is not used twice or in other households).

Retention: the unlock stays with the household, also when you leave it or delete your account. When you delete your account, we remove the link between the purchase and your account; the transaction itself stays stored without reference to you for as long as Hummlist runs, so refunds can be matched and the same purchase cannot unlock a second time. When the household is deleted, its unlock ends. Tax record-keeping for the sale to you is Apple's and Google's; we keep no data about you for it.

Your purchases are part of "Export my data". Usage data about purchases (section 9) is sent only with your consent.
