## 2. Hummlist and Hummlist Premium

What exactly is included is shown on the "Hummlist Premium" page in the app before you buy. Siri and the AI assistants are other providers' services with their own terms. You may need your own account there.

## 3. For the whole household

Hummlist Premium belongs to the household it was bought for. It applies to everyone who is currently a member. If you leave the household, Premium does not go with you. Premium can't be moved to another household. If the household is deleted, Premium ends. Our [help page](/en/support) explains how to archive a household so that Premium is kept. Apple Family Sharing is not supported; sharing works through the Hummlist household.

## 5. Yearly plan

The yearly plan costs the price shown to you in the store before you buy. It renews automatically for one year at a time unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before the end of the current year.

Only the person who took out the plan can cancel it, and only with Apple or Google. The app shows the way under More › Hummlist Premium › Manage or cancel subscription, and so does our [help page](/en/support). After you cancel, Premium stays until the end of the paid year. Deleting the app or your Hummlist account, or leaving the household, does not cancel the plan. Further cancellation rights follow Apple's or Google's terms and the law.

A new price applies to a running plan no earlier than the next renewal, and only in the way Apple or Google announce it in advance under their rules and, where those rules require it, ask for your consent. You can cancel at any time before the renewal.

## 6. One-time purchase ("Lifetime")

The stores call the one-time purchase "Lifetime". It is paid once and does not renew.

"Lifetime" means: Premium applies to the household for as long as the household exists and as long as we run Hummlist. It does not mean that Premium belongs to one person or lasts beyond the end of Hummlist. Section 10 explains what happens if we shut Hummlist down.

We do not terminate the one-time purchase with notice. Both sides keep the right to terminate for good cause.

If the household still has a running yearly plan, that plan keeps running alongside the one-time purchase until it is cancelled.

If we ever shut Hummlist down, we will announce it at least three months in advance by e-mail to the address of your Hummlist account and in the app. From the announcement we stop selling Hummlist Premium. Until the end you can download your data under Account › Export my data.

- **Yearly plan:** as far as the store allows, we make sure running plans no longer renew, and we tell you how to cancel. For paid time after the end, we refund the pro-rata amount.

To get a refund, write to [support@hummlist.com](mailto:support@hummlist.com) with proof of purchase, for example the order number from Apple's or Google's receipt. Because we can't issue refunds for Apple purchases through Apple ourselves, we pay by bank transfer. Any further statutory claims are not affected.
