# Hummlist on your Home Screen: the widget

The Hummlist widget shows on your Home Screen how many items are still open and what's next, with the store next to it. Tap the widget to open your list; tap **Add** to open it with the input field ready.

First sign in to the Hummlist app and open your list once. The widget always shows what the app saw last: what you add or tick off in the app shows in the widget right away. What someone else in your household adds shows up there after Hummlist was open on your phone again. Without internet the widget shows the last list it got.

## On iPhone

1. Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
2. Tap **Edit** in the top left, then **Add Widget**.
3. Search for **Hummlist** and tap it.
4. Swipe to the size you want: small (the count and what's next) or medium (with stores and the **Add** button).
5. Tap **Add Widget** and move it into place. Tap **Done** in the top right.

## On Android

1. Touch and hold an empty spot on the Home Screen and tap **Widgets**.
2. Find **Hummlist** in the list.
3. Touch and hold the widget and drag it onto the Home Screen. On some phones you tap **Add** instead.
4. Drag its edge to make it bigger: from four columns wide it shows the stores and the **Add** button.

## Ticking off

You tick items off in the app: tap the widget and your list is open.
