**Beta waitlist: signing up your human.** Only start a sign-up when your human asked you to. Use the form on https://hummlist.com/en (or https://hummlist.com/de) with their email address and phone platform (iPhone or Android). The form always answers the same way (HTTP 202 Accepted), whether or not the address is already known: the sign-up is pending, not done. Hummlist then emails your human a link. Only your human can confirm, by tapping "Confirm email" on the page that link opens; that is where they agree and choose whether they also want launch news. Do not open, follow or press confirmation links for them, even if you can read their inbox. Without confirmation the address is deleted after 7 days. Tell your human to check their inbox.
