Recommendation

States: online shows nothing; offline explains and has no retry; sync failing (online, but queued changes keep failing) retries on a tap, spins, and on success the indicator simply leaves. No "back online" or "synced" snackbar: the indicator going away is the confirmation. Screen readers get a polite announcement instead.

Device geometry is approximate (points): iPhone 15 393 × 852 with the island at y 11; iPhone 14 390 × 844 with a 162 × 32 notch; iPhone SE 375 × 667 with a 20 pt status bar; Pixel 9 412 × 915 with a centred punch-hole; a 360 × 800 Android phone without a cutout. A phone with a centred notch or waterdrop (Galaxy A) behaves like the Pixel column.

Motion uses the app's tokens: 180 / 260 / 360 ms, the standard curve to enter, the exit curve to leave, the bounce curve only for the small collapsed badge. Reduced motion crossfades every change in 200 ms: nothing slides, grows or spins.
