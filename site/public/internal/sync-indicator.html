Offline and sync indicator prototypes

Internal, not for merge. Switch the state to watch every phone react.

390 × 844 pt phone with the Dynamic Island and a 34 pt home-indicator inset, the list screen as it is after the floating top bar, the floating add field and the tab pill. Same list in every frame.

Online shows nothing in S1 to S5. Going from offline or failing back to online flashes "Wieder online" for 1.6 s, then the indicator leaves; drop the flash if online must stay silent. Failing means: online, but the queued changes keep failing (server or gateway errors for a while, or a session that won't refresh). For an expired session the action would read "Neu anmelden" instead of "Erneut versuchen" (copy only, no frame shows it).

Long copy never sits inside a hex pill: the pills carry an icon and a one-word state, the sentence sits beside or below them. Reduced motion: no slide, grow or width change; the indicator fades in and out in 120 ms.
