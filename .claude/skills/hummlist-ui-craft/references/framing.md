# Framing

How the screen sits in its space decides whether it reads as a product shot or a screenshot in
a box. Decide the frame before building the screen: the crop sets what must exist past it and
how large the type must be.

## Rules

1. **One hero, one or two satellites with a job.** A satellite is a smaller second object (a
   member's phone, a store chip, a confirmation card). Two satellites differ in size and kind.
2. **Keep what makes it Hummlist:** the list title, real items, the hex tick, the store chips.
   Logo and navigation are not required; crop around the story.
3. **The medium picks the first layout** (table below). If it does not hold, try the next one.
4. **Crop only filler.** The payoff, the action, the touched control and every real word stay
   whole. A list may run one row off a bled bottom edge. If the story does not fit, change the
   stage size or layout, never slice a button.
5. **Bleed 10 to 40 % of the hero** off at least one edge; it fills two thirds of the width.
   Shapes that read when cut (a list, a card, a row of avatars) run off by a third or more.
6. **One gutter**, measured in the final crop. Every other edge distance matches it or is well
   past it. Satellites straddling the hero's edge are the exception.
7. **Satellites straddle the hero's edge, about half outside**, over margin or chrome, never
   over content. Fully apart, tie it back with a 1 px leader ending in a small dot.
8. **Depth is distinct:** the light theme's hard navy sticker shadow on the hero, a smaller one
   on a satellite; in dark, a lighter raised surface and the night shadow. One shadow language
   per page.
9. **Flat and frontal.** A tilt or perspective has to beat the straight version at final size.
10. **What opens stays in.** A sheet, menu or toast stays a gutter inside every edge on every
    frame; place its trigger so it has room. Anchored UI sits where the app puts it.
11. **No dead corners** at rest or mid-loop. Fix the crop or layout first; add a satellite only
    if it has a role.
12. **On a colour block** (cobalt, yellow or mint section ground) inset the hero 8 to 20 % from
    the sides and run it off the bottom, so the block frames it on three sides.
13. **A small illustration may need more air** than the app itself.

## Layouts

| Layout | Hero | Satellite | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Corner bleed | Inset ~10 % top and left, 15 to 35 % past right and bottom | Straddling left or bottom edge | Hero beside copy |
| Pinned bottom | Inset 8 to 12 % left, right, top; 15 to 35 % past the bottom | Optional, straddling a side | Feature cards, bento tiles |
| Block with gutters | Inset 8 to 20 % each side on a colour block, off the bottom | One side | Section moments in cobalt, yellow, mint |
| Satellite focus | Hero cropped hard, mostly context | 30 to 40 % of hero width, carries the story | One small thing: a store chip, a sync toast |
| Phone pair / trio | Lead phone inset top, off the bottom | Second phone straddling about a third outside, present from frame one | Household sync, several members |
| Close-up | One control at 1.3 to 1.6×, its screen around it, off the bottom | None | A single control: the hex tick, the add field |
| Fade at the foot | Screen inset top and sides, last third masked out over filler | Optional | When any hard crop line would be arbitrary |

A whole phone with even margins is valid only when the brief asks for it unframed; then the
margin is at least as deep as its shadow.

## Small formats and the page

- **The page is the ground.** On a white or navy page the scene's ground is transparent: no
  plate behind the device.
- **The stage is the shot.** A tile is cut to the stage's shape; never a stage boxed inside
  another tinted ground.
- **Size for the slot.** A lone card in a 680 px column wants 500 to 560 px wide so its text
  shows at 15 to 20 px.
- **Small formats crop harder:** a 400 px card holds a third of a screen at full size, not the
  whole screen at a third.
- **A cropped phone** loses status bar and app bar together; the crop falls inside the content.
- **Moving keeps the bleed.** After any move or resize, recheck what the crop cuts, re-film.

## Check

Is the hero's top-left visible? Is every bleed deliberate, not a sliver? Does each satellite
connect to its hero with clear depth? Is the compact design composed, not shrunk?
