# Snacklist Premium (held: not on sale)

Premium launch is on hold: nothing is sold and nothing is gated. Siri, Alexa and AI assistants work for every household without Premium; Siri and Alexa stay free even after the launch (jobs 184 and 72). Mehr does not list it before the launch (job 64): the row shows only once Premium may be offered (server switch `premium_offer`, and in the store builds the purchases define) or for a household that has Premium. Its page (`/more/lifetime`, PW-P4 „Haushalt“ since job 87) opens with four hexes (three members and a plus), the household sentence and the benefit lines (the slogan „Einer kauft. Alle haben es.“ only once sales are on, Legal Review N-68), and nothing else before sales start: no „bald verfügbar“, no „in der App kaufen“ note on the web (Legal Review's final texts, job 87). There is no price, no buy button and no restore button anywhere. A household that has been given Premium by the team (testers, reviewers, partners: a RevenueCat promotional grant) sees a honey status card under the hero, „In der Beta kostenlos freigeschaltet, bis Snacklist Premium startet“ (a tester's grant: „Kostenlos freigeschaltet (Test und App-Prüfung)“) with „Du zahlst nichts, und nichts verlängert sich automatisch. Bevor die kostenlose Freischaltung endet, sagen wir dir mindestens 4 Wochen vorher Bescheid.“ and the link „Mehr dazu in der Datenschutzerklärung“, and the Mehr row appears (one line, no hint since job 85).

## Sub-features

- `prem-row` the Mehr row „Snacklist Premium“ (below „Konto“, no hint since job 85): today only for a household that has Premium (a free beta or tester unlock). With several households the row and the page follow the household shown, so switching can show or hide them. Once offered, every household sees it.
- `prem-page-web` on the web (app.snacklist.app, app.hummlist.com, the preview), once sales start (`premium_offer` on), the page adds „Snacklist Premium kaufst du in der iPhone- oder Android-App. Danach ist es auch hier für deinen ganzen Haushalt freigeschaltet.“ Not shown today, also not for an unlocked household. Nothing to buy on web, ever.
- `prem-page-soon` in the iOS and Android store builds and on the web today (purchases build define off, `premium_offer` off) the page shows the hero only (no slogan, no refund link): no „bald verfügbar“, no note on where to buy, no cards, no prices, no buy, no restore.
- `prem-refund-link` the link „Doppelt gekauft? So bekommst du dein Geld zurück“ (opens „Kauf zurückfordern“ help with the Apple and Google refund steps) ends the page only once sales are on, or for a household that bought Lifetime twice (Legal Review N-69). Not visible to anyone else today.
- `prem-granted` household with a free unlock (a RevenueCat promotional grant: testers, beta households, app review): honey status card; the grant decides the title (Legal Review N-70): a grant that ends before 2100 is a beta grant, „In der Beta kostenlos freigeschaltet, bis Snacklist Premium startet“, also after the launch; a grant without end or to 2100-01-01 (`scripts/iap/promotional.ts grant`, testers and app review) reads „Kostenlos freigeschaltet (Test und App-Prüfung)“. Then „Du zahlst nichts, und nichts verlängert sich automatisch. Bevor die kostenlose Freischaltung endet, sagen wir dir mindestens 4 Wochen vorher Bescheid.“ and the link „Mehr dazu in der Datenschutzerklärung“ (snacklist.app/datenschutz#premium-beta). Legal Review's final texts, approved 2026-11-03. Every member sees it live.
- `prem-no-paywall` no paywall is shown in production: no plan cards, so no `paywall_viewed` event is ever sent; the Zugänge page shows no Premium card (the integration lock is off).
- `prem-held-unreachable` (not visible today, shipped in the code, needs the purchases define on a store build and the server switch `premium_offer`): PW-P4 plan tiles side by side, „Jährlich“ (picked when the page opens) and „Lifetime“, each with the store's price large and „pro Jahr inkl. MwSt.“ / „einmalig inkl. MwSt.“ under it, the line „Premium gehört dann zum Haushalt „<Name>“ und bleibt dort, auch wenn du den Haushalt später verlässt.“, one button for the picked plan („Jährlich abschließen“ / „Lifetime kaufen“) with that plan's terms under it, „Kauf wiederherstellen“, terms, manage-subscription, billing warnings. Do not describe as available. Offline (switch unreadable) the page says „Snacklist Premium ist gerade nicht verfügbar.“, not „bald verfügbar“; a restore that finds only a lapsed plan says nothing to restore; a failed renewal keeps Premium through the store's grace period (job 67). The order of the switches that make this reachable is `docs/PAYWALL_RELEASE.md`.

- `prem-per-user` (job 185, dark like `prem-held-unreachable`): a purchase belongs to the person who bought it. Store builds use the Snacklist user id as RevenueCat's App User ID; the buyer sees Premium (the Mehr row and the status card) in every household they are in, live; other members see nothing of it. When a yearly plan ends, the row and status go at once; a renewal brings them back. A household's promotional grant still covers the whole household. Driven with `drive.sh premium` (`journeys/premium.mjs`: Mia's yearly plan through the webhook with her user id, her second household, Noah's view, EXPIRATION, an item added and ticked after the lapse, RENEWAL). The page's household copy („… für euren ganzen Haushalt …“) is unchanged and reads wrong for this case (NEEDS_PETER „Job 185“).

- Seeded household „Familie Brandt“, signed in. - Specs: `e2e/tests/purchases.spec.ts`, `e2e/tests/premium-yearly.spec.ts`, `e2e/tests/integration-lock.spec.ts`; run with `scripts/verify.sh --only e2e -- tests/purchases.spec.ts`.

- **No row before the launch.** `await openTab(page, 'Mehr')`; `page.getByRole('button', { name: /^Snacklist Premium/ })` has count 0 for a household without Premium (`premium_offer` is off on the local stack).
- **Open the page.** `openPremium(page)` (`e2e/tests/helpers.ts`, goes to `/more/lifetime`). The hero: `page.getByText('Snacklist Premium gilt für euren ganzen Haushalt, auch für alle, die später dazukommen.')`; the slogan „Einer kauft. Alle haben es.“ has count 0 on the e2e stack. On web before sales start: `page.getByText(/^Snacklist Premium kaufst du in der iPhone- oder Android-App\./)` has count 0; `page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Kauf wiederherstellen' })` has count 0; no button „Jährlich abschließen“ / „Lifetime kaufen“ (count 0).

- **Granted household (testers).** Grant the household through the webhook helper, then the row appears (`getByRole('button', { name: /^Snacklist Premium/ })` visible, live, without reload), and the page shows `page.getByText(/^Snacklist Premium (Lifetime|aktiv|über)/)` for a purchase. A beta grant (`p_expires_at` 60 days ahead) shows „In der Beta kostenlos freigeschaltet, bis Snacklist Premium startet“, the subtitle, and `getByRole('link', { name: 'Mehr dazu in der Datenschutzerklärung' })` with href `https://snacklist.app/datenschutz#premium-beta` (e2e purchases.spec.ts, job 87); a grant to 2100-01-01 or without end shows „Kostenlos freigeschaltet (Test und App-Prüfung)“ instead (no e2e spec asserts it; driven 2026-11-06 with `select public.promotional_premium_sync('<household>', true, '2100-01-01T00:00:00Z')` as postgres: that title, no „geschenkt“, no buy note; the row went away again with `(…, false, null)`). - **Nothing gated.** A household without Premium can still add via Siri/MCP/Alexa (see `siri`, `ai-assistants`, `alexa` maps); Zugänge shows no Premium card.

## Gotchas

- Production builds do not set the purchases define, the integration-lock define or the fake store (`app/config/auth.json`), and the server switches `premium_offer` and `integration_lock` are off. Anything about plans, prices, cards or the locked state is unreachable for a normal user.
- The e2e build (`app/config/e2e.json`) turns `INTEGRATION_LOCK` on; with the server switch also on, Zugänge shows the Premium card, tokens show „Pausiert: braucht Snacklist Premium“ and MCP and the Snacklist API (held itself) answer that the household needs Premium; Siri and Alexa keep adding, and the Siri credential's and Alexa's tiles are never paused (jobs 184 and 72). The card lists only „KI-Assistenten, die MCP unterstützen“. A Shortcut's access token still shows as paused, though Siri accepts it. That is a test of shipped-but-dormant code, not today's product. The e2e web build still never sells (web is `PurchasesUnsupported`).
- The Mehr row has no hint since job 85; the old placeholder „Siri und KI-Assistenten“ is gone from the ARB, and since job 184 so are `premiumBenefitSiri` and `integrationPremiumSiri`.
- Testers get Premium by a RevenueCat promotional grant for their household (made by the team in the RevenueCat dashboard or with `scripts/iap/promotional.ts grant --household <uuid> --apply`, which grants until 2100-01-01); the old tester list is gone. The page shows the tester line „Kostenlos freigeschaltet (Test und App-Prüfung)“ (job 87, N-70), not Lifetime. The grant takes effect through RevenueCat's webhook or `scripts/iap/promotional.ts reconcile --household <uuid> --apply`; RevenueCat unreachable means a retry later. Who gets this free unlock and what RevenueCat receives is in the privacy policy, section „Snacklist Premium in der Beta“ (snacklist.app/datenschutz#premium-beta, #216): testers and Apple's and Google's app review; it lasts until the team ends it and never turns into a paid purchase.
- Words in this feature for copy: „Snacklist Premium“ is the name; yearly and Lifetime are the planned plans but are not on sale. Do not announce a price.
- `paywall_viewed` (entry points `more`, `integrations`) fires only when a plan with a store price is shown, so never in production today; it is also subject to analytics consent. unverified in a live run (read from code only).
- Real store purchases cannot be driven on the box (no store). unverified (no store sandbox). StoreKit-local Lifetime cases (purchase, restore, refund, Ask to Buy against `Snacklist.storekit`) run in the ios CI gate on an iOS 26.2 simulator (job 127, `scripts/gates/ios.sh`); the yearly plan has no StoreKit test.
- The last member deleting the account ends any Premium, a free unlock included: see account-deletion `acct-premium`.
