# Onboarding: the guided start, from the first screen to the list

Signed out, Snacklist opens on a start screen (`/start`) with „Los geht's“ / "Get started" and „Schon ein Konto? Anmelden“ / "Already have an account? Log in", and below them „Einladung einfügen“ / "Paste invite" (job 169b W2). „Los geht's“ starts the guided start (job 168b): the usage-data question (only if this browser or phone has not answered it), „Mit wem kaufst du ein?“, the household name, the stores, „Was fehlt gerade?“, then the account, as late as possible. Everything typed before the account stays on the device and survives a reload or an app kill. Once signed in, the app makes the household from the answers (name, stores, kind, the items) and offers invites, several at once, each marked live when someone joins. A signed-in user without a household still lands on „Willkommen bei Snacklist“ / "Welcome to Snacklist" (`/welcome`); its „Haushalt erstellen“ runs the same steps from „Mit wem“, without the usage question and the account step. An invite link skips all of this and opens the join screen after sign-in (on the web `/join/<code>` is the static join page, whose „Im Browser beitreten“ opens the app at `/?invite=<code>`; household-invites `join-page`).

## Sub-features

- `start-screen` signed out, `/` opens `/start` (key `start-screen`): the Snacklist icon over the wordmark, the line „Eure Einkaufsliste. Live auf jedem Handy.“ / "Your shopping list. Live on every phone.", the text „Willkommen! In zwei Minuten steht eure gemeinsame Liste, mit allem, was gerade fehlt.“ / "Welcome! In two minutes your shared list is ready, with everything you need right now.", the filled button „Los geht's“ / "Get started" (`start-go`), the text button „Schon ein Konto? Anmelden“ / "Already have an account? Log in" (`start-log-in`, opens `/login`), „Einladung einfügen“ (`invite-paste`, household-invites `paste-invite`), and the links Impressum and Datenschutz at the foot. With a guided start under way, `/` and `/start` resume at its step instead.
- `steps` the steps live at `/start/<step>`: `usage`, `who`, `name`, `stores`, `need`, `account`, `setup`, `invite`. Each has a title bar whose title is the counter „Schritt N von 7“ / "Step N of 7" (a heading on the web), with the X „Einrichtung verlassen“ / "Leave setup" (`onboarding-exit`; the invite step has none) and a back arrow „Zurück“ to the step before (none on the usage question, „Mit wem“, the setup and the invite step). Under the bar a progress bar, then the step's question as its heading; the seven counted steps are usage, who, name, stores, need, account and invite. A step's list has the key `onboarding-<step>`. „Weiter“ / "Continue" is `onboarding-next`, „Überspringen“ / "Skip" `onboarding-skip`, an outlined button of the same size below it.
- `usage-step` no question heading of its own (the bar says „Schritt 1 von 7“): the consent question with the exact words of privacy-consent (`ConsentQuestion`), „Nicht erlauben“ and „Erlauben“. Shown only when this install has not answered; the answer moves on to „Mit wem“.
- `who` „Mit wem kaufst du ein?“ / "Who do you shop with?", text „Wähl alles, was passt. Danach richten sich Name, Einladung und deine ersten Tipps.“ / "Pick all that apply. This sets the name, the invite and your first tips." Six chips, any number: „Partner·in“ / "Partner", „Familie“ / "Family", „WG“ / "Flatmates", „Verein oder Team“ / "Club or team", „Nur ich“ / "Just me", „Andere“ / "Other" (keys `onboarding-kind-partner|family|flat|club|solo|other`). „Andere“ adds the field „Mit wem noch?“ / "Who else?" (hint „z. B. Nachbarn“, key `onboarding-kind-other-text`). With two or more picked: „Wir starten mit „<first>“. Die anderen merken wir uns für später.“ / "We'll start with “<first>” and keep the others for later." (`onboarding-kinds-several`). „Weiter“ is off until something is picked; „Überspringen“ goes on without a kind. The first pick becomes the household's kind (`households.kind`); the others are only kept on the device for later (a user can have several households since job 169b; the guided start still makes one).
- `name` „Wie soll euer Haushalt heißen?“ / "What should your household be called?", text „Den Namen sehen alle, die du einlädst. Du kannst ihn später ändern.“ / "Everyone you invite sees this name. You can change it later." Field „Name des Haushalts“ / "Household name" (`household-name`), prefilled from „Mit wem“: „Unsere Familie“, „Unsere WG“, „Unser Verein“, „Meine Liste“ (Nur ich), the „Andere“ words when they are a valid name, otherwise „Unser Haushalt“ / "Our household" (EN: "Our family", "Our flat", "Our club", "My list"). 1 to 80 characters, else „Gib einen Namen mit 1 bis 80 Zeichen ein.“ / "Enter a name of 1 to 80 characters." No „Überspringen“ here.
- `stores` „Wo kauft ihr ein?“ / "Where do you shop?", text „Wähl die Läden, in denen ihr einkauft. In dieser Reihenfolge stehen sie in der Liste.“ / "Pick the stores you shop at. Your list shows them in this order." Eleven chips, none picked (`onboarding-store-<name>`: EDEKA, REWE, Lidl, Aldi Süd, Aldi Nord, Kaufland, Netto, Penny, dm, Rossmann, Baumarkt; „Baumarkt“ is a store type that brings the Baumarkt categories, feature categories `cat-baumarkt`), „Eigenen Laden hinzufügen“ / "Add your own store" (`onboarding-store-own`, an inline field „Name des Ladens“ / "Store name"; a new name joins the chips picked, a typed name of an offered chain in any case or spacing, „edeka“, ticks that chip instead, and a name already picked stays picked once), the store-name note „Ladennamen wie dm, Lidl oder EDEKA verwenden wir nur, …“ (`stores-disclaimer`), „Weiter“, „Überspringen“ (no stores) and below it „Ohne Läden starten. Du kannst sie später unter Liste → Anpassen anlegen.“ / "Start without stores. You can add them later under List → Edit." The household gets the stores in the order picked.
- `need` „Was fehlt gerade?“ / "What do you need right now?", text „Tipp an, was auf die Liste soll, oder schreib es unten hin.“ / "Tap what should go on the list, or type it below." Twelve chips (Milch, Brot, Eier, Butter, Käse, Bananen, Äpfel, Tomaten, Nudeln, Kaffee, Joghurt, Klopapier; EN Milk, Bread, Eggs, Butter, Cheese, Bananas, Apples, Tomatoes, Pasta, Coffee, Yogurt, Toilet paper; keys `onboarding-staple-<name>`), the field „Noch etwas?“ / "Anything else?" (hint „z. B. 2 Gurken“, `onboarding-need-field`) with the plus „Hinzufügen“ / "Add" (`onboarding-need-add`) or Enter. Under „Auf deiner Liste (N)“ / "On your list (N)" each item is a chip with an X „<name> entfernen“ (`onboarding-need-item-<text>`). An item is added once, whatever its case or spacing. The button reads „Weiter“, „Weiter mit 1 Artikel“ or „Weiter mit N Artikeln“ / "Continue with N items". Typed text is split as the list does („2 Gurken“ becomes Gurken with the amount 2) and the server picks the category.
- `account-step` signed out only: the bar says „Schritt 6 von 7“, the heading „Konto erstellen“ / "Create your account", „Fast geschafft. Mit einem Konto speichern wir eure Liste, und du kannst jemanden einladen.“ / "Almost done. With an account we save your list, and you can invite someone.", then every sign-in method of the sign-in screen (Google, Apple, „Weiter mit E-Mail“, sign-up, password, sign-in link), the legal links, back to „Was fehlt gerade?“ and the X.
- `setup` once signed in, „Wir richten euren Haushalt ein …“ / "Setting up your household …" (`onboarding-setup`) with a spinner: the household is created with the name, stores and kind (a „Verein oder Team“ household starts with „Beitritte bestätigen“ on) and gets the items. Offline or on an error it shows the reason (`onboarding-setup-problem`, for example „Keine Internetverbindung. Versuche es erneut, sobald du online bist.“) and „Erneut versuchen“ / "Try again" (`onboarding-setup-retry`). A retry or an app kill in between makes one household and adds each item once.
- `invite-step` title and first button by kind: „Partner·in einladen“, „Familie einladen“, „Mitbewohner einladen“, „Mitglieder einladen“, otherwise „Jemanden einladen“ (EN "Invite your partner", "Invite your family", "Invite your flatmates", "Invite members", "Invite someone"). Text „Teile den Link mit der Person, mit der du einkaufst. Jede Einladung gilt 7 Tage und für eine Person.“ / "Share the link with the person you shop with. Each invite is valid for 7 days and for one person." (for a club „Schick jedem Mitglied einen eigenen Link. Alle sehen dieselbe Liste.“), and with items „N Artikel warten schon auf der Liste.“ / "N items are already waiting on the list." (`invite-step-waiting`). The first button (`invite-partner`) makes an invite; after it „Weitere Einladung“ / "Another invite" (`invite-another`) makes one more, each its own card (code, link, „gültig bis …“, „Teilen“ where there is a share sheet, „Link kopieren“, „Code kopieren“; no „Widerrufen“ here). The phone apps open the share sheet at once; the web shows the card.
- `invite-joined` while the step is open, an invite that someone accepts turns into „<Name> ist beigetreten“ / "<Name> joined" (or „Beigetreten“ / "Joined" without a display name) with a check mark, in place of the card's buttons (`invite-joined`, a live region). Realtime on `invites`; an invite accepted before the step subscribed shows too. For a club (join approval on, every „Verein oder Team“ household from the guided start), an accept is a join request: the card stays as it is, and the request shows on the household page (Mehr → the household) until an owner or admin lets the person in (job 168c).
- `invite-leave` „Später“ / "Later" (`invite-later`), after an invite „Weiter zur Liste“ / "Go to the list", ends the guided start and opens the list with the items on it. „Nur ich“ as the only pick skips the invite step and goes straight to the list.
- `exit` the X opens „Einrichtung verlassen?“ / "Leave setup?" with „Was du bisher eingegeben hast, bleibt auf diesem Gerät gespeichert.“ / "What you've entered so far stays saved on this device." and three buttons: „Fortschritt verwerfen“ / "Discard progress" (red, `onboarding-exit-discard`), signed out „Stattdessen anmelden“ / "Log in instead" (`onboarding-exit-sign-in`, opens the sign-in, the answers stay) or signed in „Abmelden“ / "Sign out" (`onboarding-exit-sign-out`), and „Weiter einrichten“ / "Continue setup" (`onboarding-exit-stay`). Discarding asks again: „Fortschritt verwerfen?“ / "Discard progress?", „Deine Antworten und N Artikel werden von diesem Gerät gelöscht.“ / "Your answers and N items will be deleted from this device.", „Abbrechen“ or „Verwerfen“ / "Discard" (`onboarding-discard-confirm`); then the start screen (signed out) or „Willkommen bei Snacklist“ (signed in).
- `resume` the answers are saved on every change (`DeviceCache` key `onboarding.draft`, one per device). A reload or an app kill resumes at the step left; signed out past the questions, at the account step. Typing another step's address goes back to the draft's step. A draft that cannot be read starts over.
- `existing-account` someone who signs in (account step or „Stattdessen anmelden“) to an account that already has a household gets the typed items on that household's list; its name, stores and kind stay as they were, and there is no invite step. Joining a household by invite with a draft under way does the same in the joined household. An account without a household continues the questions where it left them, without the account step.
- `welcome` signed in without a household (after the usage question on builds with analytics): „Willkommen bei Snacklist“ / "Welcome to Snacklist" with „Snacklist teilt eine Einkaufsliste mit allen in deinem Haushalt. Erstelle einen neuen Haushalt oder nimm eine Einladung an.“ and two cards: „Haushalt erstellen“ (hint „Eine neue Liste, zu der du danach deinen Partner einlädst.“, key `onboarding-create`) opens `/start/who`; „Einladung annehmen“ (hint „Du hast einen Link oder einen Code bekommen.“, `onboarding-join`) opens the join screen; under them „Einladung einfügen“ (`invite-paste`). Below: „Konto löschen“ (red); in the app bar a logout icon „Abmelden“.
- `own-while-waiting` (job 168c) on the join-request wait, „Eigenen Haushalt anlegen“ runs the guided start from „Mit wem“. If the request is approved during the questions, they go on and the user ends with both: the joined household and a new one of their own with the typed name, stores and items (the joined list gets none of them). The joined household stays the default for Siri, Alexa & KI.
- `join` the join screen, title „Einladung annehmen“ / "Join with an invite": field „Einladungscode“, „Weiter“ previews „Einladung in den Haushalt <Name>“, then „Haushalt beitreten“ or „Abbrechen“. Details in household-invites.
- `invite-link` an invite (`/?invite=<code>` on the web, `/join/<code>` in the apps) survives sign-in, the usage question and a guided start under way (kept 7 days on the device); signed out it opens the sign-in (`/login?invite=<code>`), not the start screen, and after sign-in the join screen with the code previewed.
- `new-household` (job 169b) „Neuer Haushalt“ in the switcher („Deine Haushalte“) does not run the guided start: it opens the create screen at `/households/new` (field „Name des Haushalts“, empty with a hint; the eleven store chips, „Eigenen Laden hinzufügen“, „Haushalt erstellen“, „Überspringen“ without stores; the creator becomes the owner; a double tap or a retry makes one household), then its invite screen „Partner einladen“ / "Invite your partner" at `/households/new/invite` (key `invite-partner-screen`): one invite card (no „Widerrufen“), „Später“ or „Weiter zur Liste“ to the new household's list.
- `events` (builds with analytics, after a yes) `onboarding_completed` when the invite step is left, or at the end of the setup for „Nur ich“: `kind` (the first pick or `skipped`), `kind_count`, `item_count`, `store_count`, `invited`. Never a name, the „Andere“ words, stores or items. `household_created` and `invite_sent` (`context` onboarding) as before. A yes given on the usage step sends `first_open` at the sign-in.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open Snacklist signed out: the start screen. „Los geht's“, the usage question if asked, „Mit wem“, name, stores, „Was fehlt gerade?“, the account, the setup, the invite step, the list.
- On the sign-in screen: „Neu bei Snacklist? Los geht's“ / "New to Snacklist? Get started" goes to the start screen, or back into a guided start under way.
- Signed in without a household: „Willkommen bei Snacklist“, „Haushalt erstellen“, then „Mit wem“ to „Was fehlt gerade?“, the setup and the invite step.
- An invite link: the sign-in, then the join screen.

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- Local stack, the verify-snacklist or e2e web build, a new browser context per run (the draft and the usage answer live in the browser's storage), a fresh email per run (`newEmail(...)`) because the seeded users already have a household. Viewport 390x844 or 480x900.
- Both builds carry a stand-in analytics key, so a new context sees the usage step after „Los geht's“ and the consent screen right after a plain sign-up.
- Helpers in `e2e/tests/helpers.ts`. `signUp(page, email, path = '/', { consent })` opens `/login` for `/` (signed out, `/` is the start screen) or `path` otherwise, signs up with „Weiter mit E-Mail“, „Registrieren“, and answers the consent screen (`answerConsent`, decline by default); without a link it ends on `/welcome`. `createHousehold(page, { name, stores, kinds, items, lang })` starts on `/welcome`: „Haushalt erstellen“, picks `kinds` on „Mit wem“ (or „Überspringen“ without), types `name` (or keeps the suggestion), picks `stores` (offered ones as chips, others typed as own stores; none: „Überspringen“), adds `items` on „Was fehlt gerade?“ (`addNeed`), clicks the „Weiter …“ button and waits for `/start/invite`. `skipInvite(page)` clicks „Später“ or „Weiter zur Liste“ and waits for `/` with `addField(page)` focused. `signUpWithHousehold(page, email, { stores })` is the three in a row. `onboardingChip(page, name)` finds a chip (Flutter web names a FilterChip a checkbox).

- **Start screen.** New context, `page.goto('/')`. Observable result: URL `/start`, `page.getByRole('button', { name: "Los geht's", exact: true })` and `page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Schon ein Konto? Anmelden' })` visible, the links „Impressum“ and „Datenschutz“.
- **The signed-out guided start.** On the start screen click „Los geht's“. URL `/start/usage`, heading „Schritt 1 von 7“ (the bar) and the question „Darf Snacklist Nutzungsdaten erfassen?“; click `page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Nicht erlauben', exact: true })` (wheel down on a short screen). URL `/start/who`: `onboardingChip(page, 'Familie').click()`, „Weiter“. URL `/start/name`: the textbox „Name des Haushalts“ (the suggestion „Unsere Familie“ is not in the DOM until edited), „Weiter“. URL `/start/stores`: `onboardingChip(page, 'REWE').click()`, „Weiter“. URL `/start/need`: `onboardingChip(page, 'Milch').click()`, `addNeed(page, '2 Gurken')`, then `page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Weiter mit 2 Artikeln' })`. URL `/start/account`, heading „Konto erstellen“: `openEmailSignIn(page)`, `typeInto` „E-Mail“ and „Passwort“, „Registrieren“. Observable result: no consent screen (answered on the first step), URL `/start/invite` after the setup, title and button „Familie einladen“, text „2 Artikel warten schon auf der Liste.“; via the API second view: one household „Unsere Familie“ with `kind = 'family'`, the store REWE, the items Milch and Gurken (`quantity = '2'`). `skipInvite(page)` ends on the list with both items. The progress is one labelled node on the web: `page.getByLabel('Schritt 2 von 7', { exact: true })`. `e2e/tests/guided-start.spec.ts` walks this path (its local `letsGo(page)` does the start screen and declines the usage question).
- **From Willkommen.** `signUp(page, email)`, then `createHousehold(page, { stores: ['REWE', 'Wochenmarkt'] })`. Observable result: URL `/start/invite`, one household „Unser Haushalt“ (no kind picked) with the stores REWE, Wochenmarkt in that order. `createHousehold(page)` alone skips „Mit wem“ and the stores and adds no items: a household without stores, 11 categories.
- **Type an offered chain.** On `/start/stores` click „Eigenen Laden hinzufügen“, `typeInto(page.getByRole('textbox', { name: 'Name des Ladens' }), 'edeka')`, Enter. Observable result: `onboardingChip(page, 'EDEKA')` checked, no chip „edeka“; after the setup the household has one store EDEKA (seed_key edeka).
- **Several invites, live.** On the invite step click the first button (for example „Partner·in einladen“), then „Weitere Einladung“. Observable result: two cards (`shownInviteCodes(page)` returns two codes). In a second browser context sign up with `signUp(lea, email, invitePath(firstCode))` (`/join/<code>` is the static join page on the web), `confirmJoin(page2)`. Observable result in the first page, without a reload: the first card shows „<display name> ist beigetreten“ and no buttons, the second card still has its buttons. Then „Weiter zur Liste“.
- **Resume after a reload.** On `/start/stores` with REWE picked, `page.reload()`. Observable result: URL `/start/stores`, REWE still checked. `page.goto('/start/need')` before „Weiter“ goes back to `/start/stores`.
- **Leave and come back.** On any step click `page.getByRole('button', { name: 'Einrichtung verlassen' })`, then „Stattdessen anmelden“. Observable result: URL `/login`, with „Neu bei Snacklist? Los geht's“; that button returns to the step left. „Fortschritt verwerfen“, then „Verwerfen“: URL `/start`, a new „Los geht's“ starts at „Mit wem“ (the usage question is answered).
- **Existing account.** With a draft holding items, „Stattdessen anmelden“ and sign in as a seeded user (or `signIn` in `helpers/lib.mjs` on the verify-snacklist build). Observable result: the list of that user's household with the draft's items added, no invite step, the household's name and stores unchanged.
- **Join with a code.** `joinWithCode(page, code, 'Familienname')` from `/welcome` (clicks „Einladung annehmen“) or the join screen. Observable result: URL `/`, `addField(page)` visible; the preview text for the household name is checked with `invitePreview`.
- **Invite link before sign-in.** `signUp(page, email, invitePath(code))` then `confirmJoin(page, household)`; through the static page: `openJoinLink(page, '/join/' + code)` (URL `/login?invite=<code>`), `signUpHere(page, email)`, `confirmJoin` (driven 2026-11-08, `invite-loop.spec.ts`, onboarding AC1).
- Covered by `e2e/tests/onboarding.spec.ts` (AC1-13: create, join, invite links through password, magic link and fake Google/Apple, offline, rate limit, English), `e2e/tests/guided-start.spec.ts` (the signed-out walk with a reload, two invites with one joined live, the X both ways, signing in to an account with a household), `e2e/tests/analytics.spec.ts` (order: sign-in, consent, onboarding), `e2e/tests/deep-links.spec.ts` (a link through onboarding), and the widget tests `app/test/onboarding/guided_start_test.dart`, `draft_test.dart` and `app/test/household/onboarding_test.dart` (typed chain names tick the chip, the skip hint). Run via `scripts/verify.sh --only e2e -- tests/onboarding.spec.ts`.

## Gotchas

- Signed out, `/` is the start screen, not the sign-in. The sign-in shows first only after a sign-out, after an account deletion, for an invite link, for a `next` link (an AI assistant's access page, an app link) and when `/login` is opened. Specs that sign in go to `/login` themselves.
- The draft is per device, not per account. A new browser context has none; one context reused across recipes resumes the last one. Discard it with the X or start a new context.
- The step order is the draft's, never the address's: `page.goto('/start/<step>')` lands on the draft's step, and without a draft on `/start` (signed out) or `/welcome` (signed in).
- The usage step and the consent screen are the same question; whichever comes first answers it for the device. A signed-out guided start therefore never shows `/analytics-consent`, and `answerConsent` (which waits for that URL) does not fit it.
- The progress always counts 7 steps. From „Willkommen“ it starts at „Schritt 2 von 7“ and skips 6 (no account step).
- The prefilled household name is not in the DOM until the field is edited (so `toHaveValue` on it fails).
- The invite step on the web shows the card and „Teilen“ only where the browser has Web Share; headless Chromium has none. The phones open the share sheet at once. „Widerrufen“ is only on Mehr → „Einladungen“ and the invite sheet „Leute einladen“.
- „<Name> ist beigetreten“ needs the realtime subscription; it shows only while the invite step is open, not on Mehr → „Einladungen“ (used person invites disappear there; who joined with a group link shows under „Wer ist beigetreten“, household-invites `joins`).
- Someone already in a household who opens an invite joins it beside their others, without a warning (job 169b); back or „Abbrechen“ on the join screen forgets a pending invite link; 10 wrong codes in an hour block joining for the hour (household-invites).
- There is no newsletter or email opt-in, no import from other apps and no referral question in the guided start (DECISIONS job 168b item 13).
- Fake-OIDC e2e specs (provider sign-in lands on the join screen) need the fake issuer reachable from the Supabase container and time out on the Hetzner boxes; trust CI for them.
