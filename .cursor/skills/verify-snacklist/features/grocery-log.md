# Grocery log (Verlauf, behind a flag)

Behind the PostHog flag `grocery-log` (rollout off, not public; job 199), a third tab „Verlauf“ / "History" sits between „Liste“ and „Mehr“. It lists the household's shopping trips: one trip is one shop at one store on one day, with the items bought. By default it is a journal by date (G1) under a month calendar whose days carry overlapping dots, one per trip in its store's colour, at most three. One button at the top switches to a timeline by store (G2) and back; the device remembers the choice. A tap on a past calendar day shows only that day's trips. Trips are logged by hand with „Einkauf eintragen“ (store, day, items with an optional amount; edit and delete later) or from a card on the list that offers to log a store's trip once its last open item is ticked off. A trip keeps the items as they were saved, never a link to the list's rows. A Free member sees the last 4 weeks (28 days, today included); older trips are kept and show with Snacklist Premium (the household's, or the member's own), behind a quiet lock row and locked calendar days. Logging stays unlimited for everyone. Nothing about prices, totals or spending is shown.

## Sub-features

- `gl-tab` the „Verlauf“ / "History" tab with the history icon, only while the flag is on; with it off the tab is gone and `/history` goes to the list.
- `gl-journal` G1: trips newest first under „Diese Woche“ / "This week", „Letzte Woche“ / "Last week" (Monday weeks), then one section per month („Oktober 2026“). A trip row: the store's dot, „Lidl · Gestern“ (store, day: „Heute“, „Gestern“ or „Mi., 11. Nov.“), the first three items („Rotwein, Oliven, Feta und 1 mehr“) and „4 Artikel“ / "4 items". A tap opens the trip in the sheet.
- `gl-calendar` G1's header: the month („November 2026“) with „Vorheriger Monat“ / „Nächster Monat“, Monday-first weekday letters, today circled. A day with trips shows overlapping dots, one per trip, newest trip first (leftmost, on top of the pile), coloured by store (the household's store order round six palette colours; no store or a deleted store: a neutral dot). 4 or more trips still show 3 dots and no „+N“; the day's screen-reader name says the real count („Donnerstag, 5. November, 5 Einkäufe“). Future days are muted and not buttons. The next-month arrow stops at the current month, the previous-month arrow at the oldest shown trip's month.
- `gl-day-filter` a tap on a past day highlights it in honey and narrows the journal to „Einkäufe am 11. November“ / "Trips on November 11" with an X („Alle Einkäufe zeigen“ / "Show all trips"); a second tap on the day or the X shows everything again. A day without trips says „Keine Einkäufe an diesem Tag“ / "No trips on this day" with „Einkauf eintragen“ for that day.
- `gl-store-timeline` G2: one block per household store in the household's order (a store without trips says „Noch kein Einkauf“ / "No trips yet"), then trips of deleted stores under the name they were saved with, then „Ohne Laden“ / "No store". Each block has a dot, the store's name as a heading and a „+“ („Einkauf bei Lidl eintragen“ / "Log a trip to Lidl") that opens the sheet with that store picked; rows show the day, the items and the count. No calendar.
- `gl-view-toggle` the icon button left of „Eintragen“: a store icon „Nach Laden anzeigen“ / "Show by store" in G1, a calendar-list icon „Nach Datum anzeigen“ / "Show by date" in G2. Each tap switches; the choice is kept on the device (also over a reload or restart) and is G1 without one.
- `gl-sheet` „Eintragen“ / "Log" in the top bar (screen-reader name „Einkauf eintragen“ / "Log a trip") opens the sheet „Einkauf eintragen“: „Laden“ / "Store" chips (the household's stores with their dots, and „Ohne Laden“; none is picked when the household has stores, „Ohne Laden“ when it has none; G2's + and the list's card preset theirs), „Wann“ / "When" („Heute“ picked, „Gestern“, „Anderer Tag“ with a date picker up to today), „Gekauft“ / "Bought" rows of „Artikel“ / "Item" and „Menge“ / "Qty" with an X each (from the second row), „Artikel hinzufügen“ / "Add item", „Speichern“ / "Save" (grey until an item has a name; without a store picked it says „Wähle einen Laden.“ / "Pick a store." under the chips and saves nothing). A tapped trip opens „Einkauf bearbeiten“ / "Edit trip" with the same fields and „Einkauf löschen“ / "Delete trip", which asks „Einkauf löschen?“ („Der Einkauf verschwindet für alle im Haushalt aus dem Verlauf.“) with „Abbrechen“ and „Löschen“. A trip of a deleted store keeps that name as its store chip with „Dieser Laden wurde gelöscht.“. A failed save says „Der Einkauf konnte nicht gespeichert werden. Versuch es noch mal.“ and keeps the sheet. With no trips at all the screen shows „Noch keine Einkäufe“ / "No trips yet", a line on what the log is for, and „Einkauf eintragen“.
- `gl-prompt` on the list, when this device ticks off the last open item of a store's section, a honey card at the top asks „Einkauf bei Lidl eintragen?“ / "Log your trip to Lidl?" („Einkauf eintragen?“ for the section without a store) with „3 Artikel abgehakt: Butter, Nudeln, Reis“, „Später“ / "Later" and „Eintragen“ / "Log". Not after the first tick, not when only the whole list empties, not on a partner's ticks. One card per store; ticking two stores empty gives two cards. Putting an item back before the section is empty takes it out. „Eintragen“ opens the sheet with the store, today and the ticked items (amounts included); „Speichern“ removes the card and shows „Einkauf eingetragen“ / "Trip logged". „Später“ removes the card without saving.
- `gl-free-window` for a member without Premium (neither the household's nor their own): trips from the last 28 days (today included) show; older ones are kept. G1 and G2 end in a lock row „Ältere Einkäufe siehst du mit Snacklist Premium“ / "See older trips with Snacklist Premium" with „1 älterer Einkauf“ / "1 older trip"; a calendar month with locked days shows the same row under it, and its days before the window are muted with a small lock, no dots, not buttons (screen-reader name „Sonntag, 4. Oktober, ältere Einkäufe mit Snacklist Premium“). The previous-month arrow stops at the window's first month (or an older shown trip's). The row is only its title and count, with no „Bald verfügbar.“ or „kaufst du in der iPhone- oder Android-App“ line. It opens the Premium page (a button with a chevron) exactly when „Mehr“ lists „Snacklist Premium“ (`premium_offer` on, and in a store build the purchases define; or Premium held); otherwise it is plain text. A member with Premium sees every trip. Logging works for any past day either way.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Only with the flag `grocery-log` on (PostHog; internal installs only, rollout off in `releases/flags.json`). A local web build turns it on with `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=grocery-log"` for `helpers/launch.sh`.
- Tab „Verlauf“ / "History" (the middle tab), route `/history`.
- „Eintragen“ / "Log" in the Verlauf top bar → sheet „Einkauf eintragen“ / "Log a trip".
- Verlauf → a trip row → „Einkauf bearbeiten“ / "Edit trip" → „Einkauf löschen“ / "Delete trip".
- Verlauf → a calendar day → the day's trips; on an empty day „Einkauf eintragen“ for that day.
- Verlauf → „Nach Laden anzeigen“ / "Show by store" → a store's „+“ → the sheet with that store.
- Tab „Liste“ / "List" → tick off a store section's last open item → card „Einkauf bei <Laden> eintragen?“ / "Log your trip to <store>?" → „Eintragen“ / "Log" or „Später“ / "Later".

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- `helpers/launch.sh` with `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=grocery-log"` (on the Hetzner runner also `FLUTTER_DIR=~/flutter-3.50.0`, the SDK main builds with), on `demo-familie`. Mia is Free.
- `drive.sh grocery-log [--lang en] [--dark]` runs everything below (`journeys/grocery-log.mjs`), `drive.sh grocery-log --compact` the 358 × 760 checks. It adds the stores Lidl and Rewe if missing and saves eleven trips through `save_trip` with fixed ids (a rerun saves the same ones): yesterday at Lidl, three on one day (Lidl twice, Rewe once), five on a day 8 days ago (under „Letzte Woche“ or, early in the week, a month heading), one 20 days ago, one 40 days ago. It deletes the household's other visible trips first and puts Butter, Nudeln and Reis back on the list under Lidl.
- Second view: `apiAs(inst, mia).rpc('trip_history', { p_household, p_today: 'YYYY-MM-DD' })` returns `{ trips, premium, since, locked }`; trips' `items` are `{ name, quantity, canonical_key }`.

- **Tab.** `page.getByRole('tablist')` holds three tabs; `openTab(page, L, 'tabHistory')`.
- **Calendar.** `page.getByRole('heading', { name: 'November 2026', exact: true })`. A day: `page.getByRole('button', { name: /^Mittwoch, 11\. November(,|$)/ })`, its name ends „, 3 Einkäufe“; the 5-trip day ends „, 5 Einkäufe“ and its cell (`locator.screenshot()`) shows three dots. Tomorrow has no button.
- **Journal.** Headings „Diese Woche“, „Letzte Woche“, „Oktober 2026“ in the ARIA snapshot; a trip row is a button whose name holds its store, day and items (`getByRole('button', { name: /Rotwein/ })`).
- **Day filter.** Click the day button → `getByRole('button', { name: 'Alle Einkäufe zeigen' })` appears, „Einkäufe am 11. November“ is in the snapshot, only that day's rows remain; click the X → all rows again.
- **Toggle.** `getByRole('button', { name: /^Nach Laden anzeigen/ })` → headings „Lidl“ and „Rewe“, no month heading; `page.reload()` → `getByRole('button', { name: /^Nach Datum anzeigen/ })` is there again; click it to go back.
- **Free window.** The snapshot ends in „Ältere Einkäufe siehst du mit Snacklist Premium“ and „1 älterer Einkauf“ in both views; the 40-day-old trip's row is absent. Previous month → `page.getByText('Sonntag, 4. Oktober, ältere Einkäufe mit Snacklist Premium', { exact: true })` exists and the day has no button; the window's first day (today − 27) is a button. `trip_history` answers `premium: false`, `since` today − 27, `locked: 1`.
- **Add.** `getByRole('button', { name: 'Einkauf eintragen', exact: true })` → `getByRole('group', { name: 'Einkauf eintragen', exact: true })`; in it click „Rewe“ and „Gestern“, `typeInto` the textboxes in order (item, amount, item, …), „Artikel hinzufügen“ adds a row, „Speichern“. `trip_history` then has the trip with `store_name: 'Rewe'`, yesterday, items `Joghurt`/`2` and `Honig`/null.
- **Edit and delete.** Click the row → group „Einkauf bearbeiten“ (its X buttons are named „Honig entfernen“) → add „Brot“ → „Speichern“: same id, three items. Row → „Einkauf löschen“ → button „Löschen“ → poll `trip_history` until the id is gone.
- **Prompt.** `openTab(page, L, 'tabList')`; `reveal` and click `itemRow(page, 'Butter')`, then Nudeln: no „Einkauf bei Lidl eintragen?“ in the snapshot; after Reis it is there. `getByRole('button', { name: 'Eintragen', exact: true })` → the group „Einkauf eintragen“ with buttons „Butter entfernen“, „Nudeln entfernen“, „Reis entfernen“; „Lidl“ and „Heute“ have `aria-current="true"`. „Speichern“ → „Einkauf eingetragen“ in the snapshot, the card gone, `trip_history` has today's Lidl trip with the three items.
- **358 px.** `--compact`: the three tabs and the top bar's title, toggle and „Eintragen“ sit inside 358 px without overlapping; a day cell is at least 40 px wide.

## Gotchas

- The flag is off everywhere by default: a plain `launch.sh` build has no Verlauf tab and no card. `VH_DEFINES` is part of the build stamp, so `doctor.sh` and `drive.sh` need the same `VH_DEFINES` exported, or they report the build as stale.
- On the Hetzner runner `env.sh` sources `env.hz`, which puts `~/flutter` (3.47.8) first; export `FLUTTER_DIR=~/flutter-3.50.0` or the build uses the old SDK and rewrites `app/pubspec.lock`.
- The Free window counts in the device's local days: `p_today` comes from the browser (Europe/Berlin in `openApp`), clamped by the server to its date ± 1. Seeds near midnight can land a day off.
- The window opens with the household's Premium (household-bound Lifetime, promotional or tester grant) or the member's own (job 185), as the integrations do: Noah does not see Mia's older trips through her own plan, but does through a household grant.
- Locked days are text nodes, not buttons: find them with `getByText`, not `getByRole`.
- A hovered icon button's tooltip doubles its accessible name on the web („Nach Datum anzeigen Nach Datum anzeigen“): match toggle and month arrows with a start-anchored regex, and move the mouse off before a screenshot.
- The sheet and its delete dialog hide the journal from the semantics tree: a row's count of 0 right after „Löschen“ proves nothing. Wait for the sheet to go, and check the deletion through `trip_history`.
- A picked chip in the sheet is `aria-current="true"` on the web, not `aria-selected` or `aria-pressed`.
- The prompt counts only this device's own ticks on the list it shows; a partner's ticks or another list start nothing. It lives on the device until logged or put off; a reload forgets it.
- Before sales start (`premium_offer` off, the local stack's default) the lock row is plain text, not a button: assert it with `getByText`, and that no „kaufst du“ or „bald verfügbar“ text shows (job 87's pre-sales rule). Its tap to the Premium page is covered by widget tests only.
- Trips are not live: another member's new trip shows when the tab is opened again, not while it is open.
- The trips are not on the public API or MCP.
