# Trip mode (behind a flag, not public)

Behind the PostHog flag `trip-mode` (rollout off, not public; job 234), ticking an item off asks „Kaufst du gerade ein?“ / "Are you shopping right now?". „Ja, Einkauf starten“ starts a trip: a dark strip above the add field shows a stopwatch, whether the trip runs, a pause button and „Beenden“. A trip can also be started by hand under Mehr. Beenden opens „Geplant und gekauft“ / "Planned and bought", a summary of the trip with one section per store: what was on the list at the start (bought or „nicht gekauft“), what came in since (with who added it), and, with the flag `prices-and-trip-total` on, what was paid against the planned prices. A trip that sees no tick for 45 minutes pauses itself; after 4 hours it ends itself at the last tick. With `grocery-log` on, the end writes one Verlauf trip per store, and those trips open the same summary. Without Snacklist Premium the summary shows only the head and a locked card. The running trip lives on the device; the ended trip is saved for the household.

## Sub-features

- `tm-ask` after every own tick while no trip runs on this device, a bottom sheet: „Äpfel abgehakt“ / "Äpfel ticked off" with the item's icon, „Kaufst du gerade ein?“ / "Are you shopping right now?", „Dann läuft eine Uhr mit, und am Ende siehst du, was geplant war und was dazukam.“, „Ja, Einkauf starten“ / "Yes, start a trip", „Nein, nur abgehakt“ / "No, just ticked off", and „Aus Versehen abgehakt? Zurücknehmen“ / "Ticked off by mistake? Put it back". „Nein“ (or dismissing the sheet) only ticks; nothing is remembered and the next tick asks again. „Zurücknehmen“ puts the item back on the list and starts nothing. No question while a trip runs, none for a partner's ticks, and none for an item of a pharmacy (Legal GL-2: a store whose name or seed key contains Apotheke, pharmacy, pharmacie, farmacia, apteka or eczane, in any case; dm and Rossmann still ask). A pharmacy's section also offers no „Einkauf eintragen?“ card, and a trip's end takes the items it logged out of those cards.
- `tm-strip` the running trip: a dark strip (dark in both themes) above „Einkaufssumme“ and the add field with a green dot, the stopwatch (`m:ss`, `h:mm:ss` from an hour), „Einkauf läuft“ / "Trip running", a hex pause button („Pause“ / „Weiter“, "Pause" / "Resume") and the honey „Beenden“ / "End". Paused: a grey dot, „Pausiert“ / "Paused", the stopwatch stands. It hides while the add sheet is open or the price panel is up, and shows on every list of the trip's household.
- `tm-long-running` 45 minutes after the last sign of life (the start, the last tick by anyone on the trip's list, the last „Weiter“) the trip pauses itself at that moment: „Pause · seit 14:01 nichts abgehakt“ / "Paused · nothing ticked since 14:01" (the last tick) and the button „Beenden um 14:01“ / "End at 14:01", which ends at the last tick. A tick ends that pause. 4 hours after the last sign of life, paused or not, the trip ends at the last tick and the list shows a mint card „Einkauf automatisch beendet“ / "Trip ended automatically", „4 Stunden lang wurde nichts abgehakt. Er endet beim letzten Abhaken um 14:01.“, „Doch weiter“ / "Keep going", „Übersicht ansehen“ / "See summary" and an X („Schließen“).
- `tm-summary` the page „Geplant und gekauft“ / "Planned and bought": the head „Sa., 14. Nov., 14:00 bis 14:25 · Lidl · Rewe“, the title „25 Min. eingekauft“ / "Shopped for 25 min", the lede „4 von 14 von der Liste gekauft · 1 zusätzlich“ / "4 of 14 from the list bought · 1 extra" (no list at the start: „Ohne Liste losgegangen · 3 gekauft“), after an automatic end „Beendet beim letzten Abhaken um 14:01, automatisch.“. Then a section per store (household order, then deleted stores, then „Ohne Laden“) with „Beim Start auf der Liste“ / "On the list at the start" and „Seit dem Start dazugekommen“ / "Added since the start"; each row a mint tick or an empty hex, the amount and name, and „nicht gekauft“ / "not bought" or „von Noah Brandt“ / "added by Noah Brandt". A bought extra has „War geplant“ / "Was planned" with the hint „„War geplant“ zählt einen Artikel zur Liste, wenn er abgesprochen war, aber nicht draufstand.“. With the prices flag: a row reads „1,80 € · geplant 1,50 € · 0,30 € mehr“ (or „wie geplant“, „… weniger“), each section ends in „Bezahlt 3,98 €“ with „Geplant 3,68 € · 0,30 € mehr · bei 2 Artikeln“, „2 ohne Preis“, „1 ohne geplanten Preis“, and the page in „Insgesamt bezahlt“ / "Paid in total" and the note „Bezahlt zählt nur Artikel mit Preis. Der Unterschied vergleicht nur Artikel mit geplantem und bezahltem Preis.“. The planned price is the item's price at the start; the paid price is its price at the end if it was set on the trip's day (a price from an earlier day left unchanged counts as „ohne Preis“). Right after Beenden the page ends in „Weiter einkaufen“ / "Keep shopping" (the same trip runs on) and „Fertig“ / "Done".
- `tm-free` without Premium (neither the household's nor the member's own) the summary shows the head, „5 Artikel abgehakt“ / "5 items ticked off" and one card: a lock, „Was war geplant, was kam dazu?“ / "What was planned, what came on top?", „Snacklist Premium zeigt nach jedem Einkauf, was auf der Liste stand und was unterwegs dazukam, auch von anderen im Haushalt.“, and „Snacklist Premium ansehen“ only where Mehr lists Snacklist Premium (not on the local stack). No rows, no amounts, no blur.
- `tm-history` with `grocery-log` on, an end writes one Verlauf trip per store of the bought items (store, today, the bought items); a tap on such a trip opens the trip's summary instead of the edit sheet. A second end of the same trip (after „Weiter einkaufen“) updates them.
- `tm-manual-start` Mehr › „Einkauf starten“ / "Start a trip" („Die Uhr läuft ab jetzt, die Liste, wie sie jetzt ist, gilt als geplant.“), only while the flag is on and no trip runs: the list with the strip, no question.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Only with the flag `trip-mode` on (PostHog; internal installs only). A local web build turns it on with `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=trip-mode,grocery-log,prices-and-trip-total"` for `helpers/launch.sh` (the other two flags add Verlauf and the amounts).
- Tab „Liste“ / "List" → tick an item → the sheet → „Ja, Einkauf starten“ / "Yes, start a trip".
- Tab „Mehr“ / "More" → „Einkauf starten“ / "Start a trip".
- The strip → „Beenden“ / "End" → „Geplant und gekauft“ (route `/trip/<id>`).
- The list's card „Einkauf automatisch beendet“ → „Übersicht ansehen“ / "See summary".
- Tab „Verlauf“ / "History" → a trip the trip mode logged → the summary (route `/history/trip/<id>`).

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- `helpers/launch.sh` with `VH_DEFINES="FLAGS_ON=trip-mode,grocery-log,prices-and-trip-total"` (on the Hetzner runner also `FLUTTER_DIR=~/flutter-3.50.0`), on `demo-familie`. Mia is Free.
- `drive.sh trip-mode [--lang en] [--dark] [--compact]` runs everything below (`journeys/trip-mode.mjs`; `--compact` is 358 × 760 and also checks the strip's controls fit). It resets the household first: the 15 seeded items open again, Lidl (Bananen, Äpfel, Brötchen, Vollmilch, Schokolade, Mineralwasser, Apfelsaft) and Rewe (the rest) added and set, prices on Bananen (0,25 €/St., yesterday), Vollmilch (1,09 €/St., today) and Nudeln (1,29 €, yesterday), every trip session and Verlauf trip of the household deleted, Kaffee deleted, Mia's promotional Premium removed. It deletes Verlauf trips, so drive grocery-log after it, not before.
- Second view: `apiAs(inst, mia).rpc('trip_session', { p_household, p_id })` returns the session with `bought`, `stores`, `premium` and `lines` (null without Premium); `trip_history` trips carry `session_id`. The database (`psql` on port `<prefix>22`) has `public.trip_sessions` with `source` (`ask`, `manual`), `end_reason` (`user`, `auto`), `ended_at`.
- Premium on the local stack: `select public.promotional_premium_sync_user('<user id>', true, null)` (RevenueCat's promotional sync, as `supabase/tests/trip_mode.test.sql` does), `false` to remove it; reload the page after.

- **Ask.** `reveal` and click `itemRow(page, 'Äpfel')` → `getByRole('button', { name: 'Ja, Einkauf starten', exact: true })` appears; the snapshot holds „Äpfel abgehakt“, the title, the body, „Aus Versehen abgehakt?“. Click „Nein, nur abgehakt“: the sheet goes, no „Beenden“ button, Äpfel `bought` in the database. Tick Brötchen: the sheet again; „Zurücknehmen“: Brötchen `active` again and on the screen, no strip.
- **Start.** Tick Bananen, „Ja, Einkauf starten“ → `getByRole('button', { name: 'Beenden', exact: true })` and `{ name: 'Pause' }`; the snapshot has the text „Einkauf läuft, seit weniger als einer Minute“. The stopwatch's digits are drawn only: compare two `page.screenshot({ clip })` of the strip's left part 2 s apart (they differ while running, match while paused).
- **Pause.** „Pause“ → a button „Weiter“ and „Pausiert“ in the snapshot; „Weiter“ → „Pause“ and „Einkauf läuft“ again.
- **During the trip.** Ticks of Vollmilch, Butter and Nudeln raise no sheet. Through the API: Bananen's price 0,30 €/St. today (Mia), and Noah inserts Kaffee at Rewe with 5,99 € (`noahApi.from('items').insert({ household_id, list_id, name, store_id, price_… })`); it appears on Mia's list live and she ticks it.
- **Free summary.** „Beenden“ → URL `/trip/<uuid>`; the snapshot has „Geplant und gekauft“, „5 Artikel abgehakt“, „Was war geplant, was kam dazu?“, „Lidl“, „Rewe“, „Weiter einkaufen“, „Fertig“, and none of „Beim Start auf der Liste“, „Insgesamt bezahlt“, Kaffee. `trip_session`: `premium: false`, `lines: null`, `bought: 5`, `stores: ['Lidl', 'Rewe']`; the stored lines have Bananen planned 150 / paid 180, Vollmilch 218 / 218, Nudeln 129 / null, Kaffee null / 599 with `by_other`.
- **Verlauf.** `trip_history` has two trips with the session id: Lidl (Bananen, Vollmilch) and Rewe (Butter, Nudeln, Kaffee). „Fertig“ → back on the list, no strip. Premium on, reload, `openTab(page, L, 'tabHistory')`, click `getByRole('button', { name: /Kaffee/ })` → URL `/history/trip/<same id>`.
- **Premium summary.** The page builds rows lazily: wheel down in steps and join the snapshots. It holds „4 von 14 von der Liste gekauft · 1 zusätzlich“, „6 Bananen 1,80 € · geplant 1,50 € · 0,30 € mehr“, „2 Vollmilch 2,18 € · geplant 2,18 € · wie geplant“, „Kaffee von Noah Brandt · 5,99 €“, a button „War geplant“, „Bezahlt 3,98 € Geplant 3,68 € · 0,30 € mehr · bei 2 Artikeln“ (Lidl), „Bezahlt 5,99 € 2 ohne Preis 1 ohne geplanten Preis“ (Rewe), „Insgesamt bezahlt 9,97 € Geplant 3,68 € · 0,30 € mehr · bei 2 Artikeln 2 ohne Preis 1 ohne geplanten Preis“ and the note. In English amounts read „€1.80“. For screen readers each store is `getByRole('heading', { name: 'Lidl', exact: true })` (and Rewe), the section labels headings of their own, the counts „6 Artikel“, and „War geplant“ is `getByRole('switch', { name: 'War geplant', exact: true })` (Flutter web has no `aria-pressed`), not checked.
- **War geplant.** Click the switch on Kaffee → `trip_session`'s Kaffee line has `marked_planned: true`, the switch is `[checked]`, Rewe's „Beim Start auf der Liste“ count goes from 8 to 9.
- **Einkauf löschen.** `getByRole('button', { name: 'Einkauf löschen', exact: true })`, then „Löschen“ in the dialog → back on Verlauf; `trip_history` has no trip with the session id, `trip_session` answers `trip_not_found`, the `trip_sessions` row is gone.
- **Manual start.** `openTab(page, L, 'tabMore')`, click `getByRole('button', { name: /^Einkauf starten/ })` → the list with the strip, no sheet. „Beenden“ → „0 von 10 von der Liste gekauft“; the session's `source` is `manual`. Wheel down before clicking „Fertig“.
- **Long-running.** A second browser with `page.clock.install({ time: new Date() })` before `signIn`. Tick Erbsen, „Ja, Einkauf starten“, read Erbsen's `bought_at`. `page.clock.fastForward('46:00')` → „Pause · seit HH:MM nichts abgehakt“ (HH:MM = that tick in Europe/Berlin) and a button „Beenden um HH:MM“. `fastForward('04:00:00')` → the card „Einkauf automatisch beendet“ with „… um HH:MM.“, no strip; the session's `end_reason` is `auto` and its `ended_at` equals Erbsen's `bought_at`. „Doch weiter“ → the strip again, no card. `fastForward('04:01:00')` → the card again, still one `auto` session. „Übersicht ansehen“ → „Beendet beim letzten Abhaken um HH:MM, automatisch.“.

## Gotchas

- The flag is off everywhere by default. `VH_DEFINES` is part of the build stamp, so `doctor.sh` and `drive.sh` need the same `VH_DEFINES` exported, or they report the build as stale.
- The running trip is kept per browser profile: every `openApp` context starts with no trip. A trip left running in one context does not show in the next.
- The stopwatch is drawn, not in the accessibility tree: the strip's text is „Einkauf läuft, seit weniger als einer Minute“ (minutes only). Prove the ticking with two pictures, as above.
- The strip and „Einkaufssumme“ read as one text run in the ARIA snapshot; match with `includes`, not exact text.
- Playwright's `fastForward` fires a due timer once: the rule runs on a 30 s timer, so a jump of 46 minutes runs it once, which is enough. Sign in before the jump; the access token renews by itself after it.
- The summary is a lazy list. A check for a row far down is vacuous until the page has been wheeled there; `getByRole('button', { name: 'Fertig' })` is not found until the page is at its end.
- Free: „Snacklist Premium ansehen“ shows only where Mehr lists Snacklist Premium, which the local stack doesn't (`premium_offer` off). The button is covered by widget tests.
- In the ARIA snapshot a section's rows and labels read as one `text:` run inside the page's group; they are separate nodes (each row one), Playwright only joins sibling text.
- Flag off (no sheet, no strip, no Mehr row, the Verlauf tile opens the edit sheet) is covered by widget tests only.
- The trip is not on the public API or MCP.
