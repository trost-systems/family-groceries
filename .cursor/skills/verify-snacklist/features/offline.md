# Working offline

The list keeps working without a connection. Adding, ticking, putting back, editing, moving and deleting items all happen on screen at once, wait in a queue on the device and are sent once the connection returns. The last synced list is stored on the device, so the app starts and shows it even with no network, and the queue survives reloads and restarts. A floating hex card „Offline“ tells the user they are offline and folds into a small pill in the bar, right of the household's name, after about 3 s (job 161). A few things need a connection and say so (below). Signing out drops the stored list.

## Sub-features

- `offline-queue` item changes (add, tick, put back, edit, move, delete) show at once, are queued and retried until the server confirms; the same change is applied once even when an answer was lost. Job 169b: the queue belongs to its household. Changes queued in one household are still sent after switching to another, and after a restart while another is shown, without opening the first again.
- `offline-amounts` (job 104) a line typed offline gets the same name and quantity as online: digits, German and English number words up front and after a known name, halves („zwei Gurken“, „Karotten drei“, „zwölf Eier“, „ein halbes Brot“, „two lemons“; „Drei Glocken Spaghetti“ stays whole). `node journeys/offline.mjs <dir>` adds lines offline, syncs, adds them again online and compares the rows.
- `offline-already-there` an add made offline whose item another member put on the list meanwhile (same name, brand and details) becomes that member's item when it syncs: no second row, their quantity and store stay. What was done offline to it goes to their item: a tick ticks it, an edit changes it; an offline delete removes only the own copy, so theirs stays (job 64b; `e2e/tests/already-on-list.spec.ts`).
- `offline-details` a line typed offline is split on the device into name and quantity only; when it syncs, the server splits off brand and details as for an online add, so „Windeln Pampers Größe 3“ typed offline becomes Windeln, brand Pampers, details Größe 3 (job 111; `e2e/tests/offline-start.spec.ts`). Until then the row shows the whole line as its name.
- `offline-indicator` the sync indicator (job 161): while offline, or once the server answered with errors and the automatic retry about 3 s later failed too (job 165: one error the retry gets past, such as a single 503 or a token refreshed mid-request, shows nothing), a floating hex card under the bar says so (one raised hex button, key `sync-indicator` in both forms; the card's content is `sync-card`; navy „Offline“ with „Änderungen werden synchronisiert, sobald du wieder online bist.“, honey „Sync gestört“ with „Deine Änderungen sind auf dem Gerät gespeichert.“ and a retry hex cue). After about 3 s, or at once on a scroll of the list or a swipe up on the card, the same hex folds into a small pill in the bar, right of the household chip and left of „Anpassen“, in one 360 ms morph of position, size and content (job 165; reduced motion: a 200 ms crossfade instead; the pill's content is `sync-pill`, „Offline“ / „Gestört“, EN "Offline" / "Failing"). A tap on the pill opens the card again; offline, a tap on the open card folds it. Failing, a tap on either retries: the arrows spin, and on success it leaves without any confirmation (screen readers hear „Synchronisiert“); a failed retry leaves the pill with a nudge (screen readers hear „Immer noch gestört“; it is never shown on screen). One button node at a time, named „Offline: Änderungen werden synchronisiert, sobald du wieder online bist.“ (EN "Offline: Your changes will sync once you're back online.") or „Sync gestört: Deine Änderungen sind auf dem Gerät gespeichert.“ (EN "Sync failing: Your changes are saved on this device."). Nothing shows while online, also while changes are on their way. Offline wins over failing.
- `offline-start` cold start without network (even with a lapsed login token) shows the cached list within seconds, the add field focused, the indicator at once.
- `offline-retry` while offline the app retries about every 3 s and on app resume; the indicator disappears when the queue is empty and the connection is back.
- `offline-network` (job 166) the app follows the device's network (`connectivity_plus`; on the web the browser's `online`/`offline` events, on iOS NWPathMonitor, on Android the system's network callback; not on Linux): when it goes down (flight mode, Wi-Fi off) the „Offline“ card shows at once with no tap and no failed request; when it is back and has stayed up for 1.5 s, the app retries at once (a resync and the queue) and brings the live channel back: a channel that is down is subscribed again, and one that still says up gets a new realtime socket (job 166b: its socket may have died with the network, and the heartbeat would notice only after 25 to 50 s; every channel joins again on the new socket), so the indicator goes without a tap about 3 s after the network returns on the web, and a partner's change shows at once. The state read at start is dropped once the network has reported a change (job 166b: it may answer after the change and undo it). A network that flaps shows offline once and retries once, after the last change. The error path stays: a network that is up while the server is out of reach still goes offline or „Sync gestört“ on the first failed request. `e2e/tests/connectivity.spec.ts` (job 166b: a socket that dies with the network and stays open is replaced, `routeWebSocket` swallows its frames).
- `offline-suggestions` while offline the add sheet's rows come from the cached usuals: typing offers the typed line and „Schon mal gekauft“ / "Bought before" rows matched as the server matches, typos and parts of words included ("Duschgl" offers „Duschgel, Schon mal gekauft“); the empty field still shows „Wieder fällig“ / "Due again" and „Zuletzt verwendet“ / "Recently used" rows. Enter waits up to 3 s for the server, then takes the device's first row. Details in quick-add (`qa-offline`) and usuals (`us-recent`).
- `offline-sorting` an item added offline has no category yet; it sits under „Wird einsortiert …“ (EN "Sorting …") until the server has classified it (the classifier runs in the database when the add arrives).
- `offline-needs-connection` things that refuse offline, below.
- `offline-signout` signing out removes the cached list and household from the device.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Switch the phone to airplane mode (or lose signal) with the list open; keep using „Liste“. The „Offline“ card shows under the bar at the top of the list (under the list tabs when there are several lists), and after about 3 s (or as soon as you scroll) it becomes the small „Offline“ pill right of the household's name, left of „Anpassen“.
- Cold start offline: open the app with no network; the list from last time shows.

## Driving it with Playwright

Preconditions:

- Seeded household (`demo-familie`: Bananen, Vollmilch, Apfelsaft, Reis …), signed in, on „Liste“, and the list cached (wait until the synced list is stored; the spec polls localStorage for the key matching `/\blist\./`).
- Two ways to go offline: `context.setOffline(true)` (whole browser context, fires the browser's `offline` event, which the app watches: the card shows within a second with no tap; `setOffline(false)` to come back) or `const network = await cutOff(page)` (aborts every request to Supabase and closes its WebSocket; `network.restore()` reconnects), which also lets a reload happen with the app server reachable.

- **Queue while offline.** `cutOff(page)` or `setOffline(true)`; `addItem(page,'Käse')`, `tick(page,'Apfelsaft')`. Observable: `itemRow(page,'Käse')` visible, `itemRow(page,'Apfelsaft')` count 0, `offlineIndicator(page)` count 1 within 10 s.
- **Survives a reload.** `page.reload()` while still cut off: the same rows, add field focused, `offlineIndicator(page)` within 3 s.
- **Cold start offline with a lapsed token.** Also set `session.expires_at` in the `sb-*-auth-token` localStorage entry to the past (as `expireStoredSession` in `e2e/tests/offline-start.spec.ts`), `cutOff`, reload: `itemRow(page,'Reis')` visible within 3 s.
- **Sync back.** `network.restore()`: poll the API as the user (`apiAs(inst, member(inst, 'mia'))` in a journey, `apiAs(email)` in the e2e specs, then `from('items').select('name, status')`) until it shows Apfelsaft `bought`, Käse `active`, Reis `active`; `offlineIndicator(page)` count 0 within 15 s. Another session of the same user sees the result.
- **Suggestions offline.** Needs a history, so use `launch.sh --scenario heavy-history` (items on the list are not offered). While cut off, `typeInto(addField(page), 'Duschgl')`: a row button whose name contains `Duschgel, Schon mal gekauft` shows. Clear the field: the „Wieder fällig“ rows still show.
- **Store needs a connection.** While cut off, add a store under Liste → Anpassen („Laden hinzufügen“; the dialog stays open): `getByText('Läden lassen sich nur mit Verbindung anlegen, umbenennen oder löschen.')` and no new store.
- **Pending state.** Hold item writes with `page.route('**/rest/v1/items**', ...)` for non-GET methods; `tick(...)`: no indicator shows while online (`offlineIndicator(page)` and `failingIndicator(page)` count 0).
- **Sync failing.** Answer every non-GET `**/rest/v1/**` with `route.fulfill({ status: 503 })`; `tick(...)`: `failingIndicator(page)` count 1 after about 3 s (the first 503 shows nothing; it shows once the automatic retry is refused too), the tick stays, no „Änderung abgelehnt“. `unroute`, click the indicator (or wait 3 s): it leaves and the server has the tick (`e2e/tests/list-header.spec.ts`).
- **Sign-out clears cache.** `openMore(page,'Abmelden')`; `Object.keys(localStorage).filter(k => /\b(list|household)\./.test(k)).length` is 0.
- **Expired token retried.** Fake a 401 JWT answer on item writes with `page.route`; the write retries with a fresh token and no „Änderung abgelehnt“ snackbar (test in `offline-start.spec.ts`).
- **Needs-connection example (feedback).** Open Feedback, type a note, `context.setOffline(true)`, click „Senden“: `getByText('Nicht gesendet. Prüfe die Verbindung und versuch es noch einmal.')`, then restore and „Erneut senden“.
- Specs: `scripts/verify.sh --only e2e -- tests/offline-start.spec.ts`, `tests/live-sync.spec.ts` (tick while offline), `tests/list-header.spec.ts` (icons), `tests/feedback.spec.ts`, `tests/already-on-list.spec.ts` (an offline add someone else made meanwhile), `tests/suggestions.spec.ts` (cached suggestions offline).

## Gotchas

- An item added offline has no category yet, so it shows under the heading „Wird einsortiert …“ until the connection returns; do not assert its category while offline.
- Offline matching: on web the card and the pill are one button whose text is its name, so use `offlineIndicator(page)` / `failingIndicator(page)` (`e2e/tests/helpers.ts`, `getByRole('button', { name: /^Offline:/ })`); `getByText('Offline')` finds nothing. Expect count 1. While a tapped retry is on its way the button is named „Wird erneut versucht“ (EN "Trying again"), so `failingIndicator(page)` counts 0 until it settles.
- Sign-in, sign-up, accepting an invite and password reset need the server: offline behaviour of the login screen is unverified (not read in detail).
- An offline start before the first ever sync shows nothing cached: unverified live (not exercised in specs). An add made then is kept across a restart (job 99, sync fuzz harness).
- Moving an item to another category or store in the edit sheet is an item edit (queued); reordering the stores or categories themselves needs a connection.
- Queued changes are tied to the device: a second device only sees them after sync.
