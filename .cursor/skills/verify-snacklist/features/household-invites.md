# Household and invites

One household shares one list, and a person can be in several households (job 169b; up to 20, Free and Premium alike: creating or joining a 21st, also by a join request, is refused with „Du bist schon in 20 Haushalten. Verlasse einen, bevor du einen neuen anlegst oder beitrittst.“, `household-cap`). The household's name is the chip top left of the list, and appears on the join screen, in the account deletion dialog and on the AI-assistant consent page (there the default household). Any member invites one person with an 8-character code and a link (valid 7 days, usable once), shares or copies them, and can revoke open invites; the owner and admins also make a group link (30 days, up to 50 people, QR code) and see who joined with it (job 169b W2). A person joins by typing the code or opening the link; joining adds the household and keeps the others. Right after the guided start sets up a household, its invite step makes as many invites as the user likes and marks each one live as soon as someone joins with it (job 168b). The household page under More renames it, lists its members with their roles (the owner and admins remove members, the owner names admins), opens „Leute einladen“, makes it the default for Siri, Alexa & KI, and leaves it.

## Sub-features

- `invite-create` (job 169b W2) the invite sheet „Leute einladen“ / "Invite people" opens from the person-plus button on every household row in „Deine Haushalte“ (tooltip „Leute in {name} einladen“ / "Invite people to {name}") and from „Leute einladen“ on the household page; More → „Partner einladen“ shows the same content as the page „Einladungen“. Section „Für eine Person“ / "For one person": „Ein Link für eine Person, 7 Tage gültig.“ / "A link for one person, valid for 7 days.", button „Neue Einladung“ / "New invite". In the phone apps the share sheet opens as soon as the invite exists; on the web the card appears and you share or copy from it. The guided start's invite step (`invite-step`) and Step 3 after „Neuer Haushalt“ (`/households/new/invite`) keep their own screens with the same card.
- `join-page-hint` (job 178, Legal N-112) every screen that makes or shares an invite (the sheet „Leute einladen“, „Einladungen“, the guided start's invite step, Step 3 after „Neuer Haushalt“) shows, as plain grey text above the invite button (on the sheet and „Einladungen“ under the Premium line, on the two onboarding screens under the body text), „Wer den Link hat, sieht auf der Beitrittsseite deinen Vornamen (aus deinem Anzeigenamen), den Namen und die Art des Haushalts und die Zahl der Mitglieder. Gib den Link nur an Personen weiter, die du einladen willst.“ / "Anyone with the link sees your first name (from your display name), the household's name and kind and the number of members on the join page. Only share the link with people you want to invite." (Legal's wording; key `inviteJoinPageHint`).
- `premium-shared` (job 169b W2, moment 5) a household with Premium shows „Premium gilt für alle im Haushalt.“ / "Premium covers everyone in the household." at the top of the sheet.
- `group-link` (job 169b W2) owner and admins only; members don't see the section. „Gruppenlink“ / "Group link": „Für bis zu 50 Leute, 30 Tage gültig. Zeig den QR-Code oder teil den Link.“; „Gruppenlink erstellen“ / "Create group link". Then a card with a QR code (the join link), „{n} von 50 beigetreten“ / "{n} of 50 joined", „gültig bis …“, the code, „Teilen“, „Link kopieren“, „Code kopieren“; under it „Neuen Link erstellen“ / "Create new link" (confirm „Neuen Link erstellen?“: the old link stops working) and „Link widerrufen“ / "Revoke link" (confirm „Gruppenlink widerrufen?“ / „Widerrufen“). A full link (the 50th joined; it stays listed until it expires, is revoked or a new one is made) says „Der Link ist voll: Niemand kann mehr damit beitreten.“, keeps „{n} von 50 beigetreten“ and „Wer ist beigetreten“, and offers „Neuen Link erstellen“ at once; a revoked one „Link widerrufen: Niemand kann mehr damit beitreten.“. One open group link per household.
- `joins` (job 169b W2) „Wer ist beigetreten“ / "Who joined" under the group link: name and „am 8. Nov.“ / "on Nov 8", or „Noch niemand.“ / "No one yet."; reloads every 5 seconds while the sheet or Einladungen is on screen and the app is in the front, and at once on coming back; no polls from the list tab or a hidden app (polled; `invite_uses` is not in realtime).
- `invite-card` per invite: „Code“ with the 8 characters (letters A-Z and digits 2-9, no I, O, 0, 1), „Link“ (`<app origin>/join/<code>`; on the phones https://app.snacklist.app/join/<code>, `APP_URL`; Hummlist builds use https://app.hummlist.com), „gültig bis {date}, {time} Uhr“ / "valid until {date}, {time}", buttons „Teilen“ / "Share" (only where a share sheet exists: phones, browsers with Web Share), „Link kopieren“ / "Copy link" (snackbar „Link kopiert“ / "Link copied"), „Code kopieren“ / "Copy code" (snackbar „Code kopiert“ / "Code copied"), „Widerrufen“ / "Revoke" (accessible name „Einladung {code} widerrufen“ / "Revoke invite {code}"). The cards on the guided start's invite step and on Step 3 after „Neuer Haushalt“ have no „Widerrufen“; revoking happens on „Einladungen“.
- `invite-step` the guided start's last step (`/start/invite`, feature onboarding `invite-step`): the first button, named by the household's kind („Partner·in einladen“, „Familie einladen“, „Mitbewohner einladen“, „Mitglieder einladen“ or „Jemanden einladen“), makes one invite; „Weitere Einladung“ / "Another invite" makes one more, each with its own card and code. „Später“ / "Later", after an invite „Weiter zur Liste“ / "Go to the list", opens the list.
- `invite-joined` on the invite step only: as soon as someone joins with one of its invites, that card shows „<Name> ist beigetreten“ / "<Name> joined" (their display name; „Beigetreten“ / "Joined" without one) with a check mark instead of its buttons. It updates live (realtime on the household's `invites`), and an invite accepted before the step opened shows as joined too. More's „Einladungen“ marks no person invite this way: a used one leaves its list (who joined with the group link: `joins`). In a club with join approval on, an accept is a join request instead and the card stays (`join-approval`).
- `share-text` the share sheet text follows the household's kind (job 169b W2), always with link and code: partner „Ich hab unsere Einkaufsliste in Snacklist angelegt. Komm dazu: {link} · Code: {code}“, family „Unsere Familien-Einkaufsliste „{name}“ ist jetzt in Snacklist. Komm dazu: …“, flat „Unsere WG-Einkaufsliste „{name}“ …“, club „Wir planen die Einkäufe für „{name}“ in Snacklist. Mach mit: …“, solo „Ich teile meine Einkaufsliste „{name}“ in Snacklist mit dir: …“, no kind „Komm in unseren Snacklist-Haushalt: {link} · Code: {code}“ (EN variants in app_en.arb; code only when the build has no app URL). The user sends it; Snacklist never does (N-18).
- `open-invites` the open person invites as cards under „Neue Einladung“ (since job 169b W2 no heading and no empty text). Used, revoked and expired invites are not listed. Any member can revoke any open person invite; the group link only its owner and admins (the server answers `not_admin` to a member). Old store builds read `invites` directly, which shows person invites only, so they never list a group link. A revoked card goes, without a snackbar. A create or revoke that fails (for example offline) leaves the cards as they are and shows the reason as text under „Neue Einladung“.
- `expiry-single-use` an invite is valid 7 days from creation (database default) and works once; after use, revoke or expiry the code gives „Dieser Code ist ungültig oder abgelaufen.“ / "This code is invalid or has expired." (one answer for made-up, expired, revoked and used codes).
- `join` „Mit Code beitreten“ / "Join with a code" in the household sheet (the chip top left of the list; More has no join row since job 206), onboarding „Einladung annehmen“ or an invite link. The title depends on membership, not on the way in: „Einladung annehmen“ / "Join with an invite" without a household, „Haushalt beitreten“ / "Join a household" in one (driven 2026-11-04): field „Einladungscode“ / "Invite code" (upper-cased, spaces and dashes ignored), „Weiter“ / "Continue" previews the household under „Einladung in den Haushalt“ / "Invite to the household", then „Haushalt beitreten“ / "Join household" or „Abbrechen“ / "Cancel". Opened from the household sheet (`/join`), back, Android's back and „Abbrechen“ return to the list (job 206; jobs 78 to 205 it was More's page `/more/join`, which now lands on `/join`); from onboarding, to „Willkommen bei Snacklist“; from an invite link while in a household, to the list.
- `switcher` (job 169b) the chip top left of the list shows the shown household's name and an expand icon; it opens a sheet „Deine Haushalte“ / "Your households": a row per household (name, „2 Mitglieder · 4 offen“ / "2 members · 4 open", a check on the one shown), „Neuer Haushalt“ / "New household", „Mit Code beitreten“ / "Join with a code", and „Siri, Alexa & KI nutzen: {name}“ / "Siri, Alexa & AI use: {name}", which opens „Standard für Siri, Alexa & KI“ / "Default for Siri, Alexa & AI" to pick another. Below it, behind the PostHog flag `default-list-voice-target` (job 207) and only when that household has two lists or more, „Liste: {list}“ / "List: {list}" (`household-default-list-row`) names the list Siri, Alexa and AI add to there when no list is named; a tap opens a menu of that household's lists (`default-list-<id>`, a check on the current one) to pick another. The chip shows with one household too.
- `household-page` (job 169b) More → the household's name: „Haushalt“ / "Household" with, in this order (driven 2026-11-09): „Name des Haushalts“ and „Umbenennen“ / "Rename"; „Mitglieder“ / "Members" („Mia Brandt (du) Hauptadmin“, a menu „Optionen für <Name>“ per other member); the button „Leute einladen“; „Listen“ (lists-and-claims); „Währung“ only with the prices flag on (prices.md); „Beitrittsanfragen“ with the switch „Beitritte bestätigen“ (owner and admins); „Standard für Siri, Alexa & KI“ with „Siri, Alexa und KI-Assistenten nutzen diesen Haushalt.“, or on another household „Siri, Alexa und KI-Assistenten nutzen gerade „<Haushalt>“.“ and „Als Standard nutzen“ / "Make default"; behind the PostHog flag `default-list-voice-target` (job 207) and with two lists or more, „Liste für Siri, Alexa & KI“ / "List for Siri, Alexa & AI" (`household-default-list`) with the household's list they add to when no list is named (the user's pick, else the first list; per member), a tap opens a menu of its lists to pick another (a rename shows at once, deleting the picked list falls back to the first); and „Haushalt verlassen“ / "Leave household" with a confirm dialog „„{household}“ verlassen?“ (others keep the list; the last member deletes it; with Lifetime the warning or, with the archive on, „Archivieren und verlassen“ / „Löschen und verlassen“). The dialog reads the members when it opens, not when the page did: if the others left meanwhile it says the household is deleted. Once left, the household is gone from the app even when the reload after it fails.
- `member-roles` (job 169b W2) on the household page the owner shows „Hauptadmin“ / "Owner", admins „Admin“. The owner is whoever created the household until they leave or delete their account; then the role passes to the earliest-joined admin, else the earliest-joined member (households whose creator left before job 169b got an owner the same way). The owner's menu (⋮, „Optionen für {name}“) on another member: „Zum Admin machen“ / "Make admin" or „Adminrechte entziehen“ / "Remove admin rights", and „Aus dem Haushalt entfernen“ / "Remove from household"; an admin's only on members: „Aus dem Haushalt entfernen“. Nobody removes themselves (that is leaving). The confirm „{name} entfernen?“ / „{name} sieht die Liste von „{household}“ dann nicht mehr und kommt über bisherige Einladungen oder den jetzigen Gruppenlink nicht zurück. In den nächsten 30 Tagen holt {name} nur eine neue Einladung oder ein neuer Gruppenlink von einem Admin zurück.“ / "{name} will no longer see the list of “{household}” and can't come back through earlier invites or the current group link. For the next 30 days, only a new invite or a new group link from an admin brings them back.", button „Entfernen“; the list reloads. A removed person who opens an invite made before the removal (the group link still in the chat, an unused person invite) gets „Dieser Code ist ungültig oder abgelaufen.“ like a revoked code; an invite made afterwards lets them in again only if its maker is the household's owner or an admin (a plain member's new person invite answers the same „Dieser Code ist ungültig oder abgelaufen.“). The record of the removal is deleted on that join, or 30 days after the removal; the app never shows it, only the removed person's data export has it (the removed person's notice states the 30-day rule, `removed-notice`).
- `removed-notice` (job 169b W2) the removed person's app, at its next refresh (opening the app again, the switcher sheet, signing in, also on a device that never showed the household), shows another household (or „Willkommen bei Snacklist“ without one) and, once per device for each removal of the last 30 days the server reports (`my_household_removals`), a snackbar (15 s) with Legal's wording (job 178, N-114): „Du wurdest aus dem Haushalt „{name}“ entfernt. 30 Tage lang kommst du über alte Einladungen nicht zurück. Beitreten kannst du in dieser Zeit nur über eine neue Einladung vom Hauptadmin oder einem Admin.“ / "You were removed from the household “{name}”. For 30 days, old invites won't let you back in. In that time you can only rejoin through a new invite from the owner or an admin." A household gone from the list without such a removal (left on another device, a removal over 30 days ago) gets „Du bist nicht mehr Mitglied von „{name}“.“ / "You're no longer a member of “{name}”." (8 s), never the 30 days; so does every one gone when the server can't say (offline). Leaving on this device says nothing. Both notices wait while a gate is on top (the sign-in, the analytics question asked right after it, the guided start, a join, a new household's setup) and show once the person is on the list, More, „Willkommen bei Snacklist“ or the waiting screen (`atHome` in `router.dart`); a removal counts as told on the device only once its notice has shown there, so an app closed on the analytics question shows it at the next start (job 178, review 1).
- `join-approval` (job 169b W3 M) owner and admins switch „Beitritte bestätigen“ / "Confirm new members" on the household page (hint „Neue Leute warten, bis ein Admin sie aufnimmt.“), in a section „Beitrittsanfragen“ / "Join requests" with a count badge („1 offene Anfrage“). On by default for households of kind `club` (created after W3), off for all others. With it on, any person or group link makes a request instead of a membership: the joiner sees „Anfrage gesendet: Ein Admin von „{name}“ bestätigt deinen Beitritt.“ with „Anfrage zurückziehen“ / "Withdraw request" and „Eigenen Haushalt anlegen“ (no household yet: the guided start from „Mit wem kaufst du ein?“, job 168c) or „Zur Liste“ (in one). The admins' household page lists each request live (name, „Ablehnen“ / "Decline", „Aufnehmen“ / "Let in"); „Keine offenen Anfragen.“ without any. Approved, the joiner's open app switches into the household with the snackbar „Du bist jetzt in „{name}“.“ / "You're now in “{name}”."; declined, it stays where it is and says „Deine Anfrage für „{name}“ wurde nicht angenommen.“. Both snackbars wait while a gate (sign-in, guided start, the analytics consent) is on top and show once the list, More or welcome screen is (job 178). A use of the link is counted only at the approval. „Aufnehmen“ on a request whose link has no use left (a full group link, a person invite used by someone else) lets nobody in: the row stays and says „Die Einladung zu dieser Anfrage ist voll. Schick einen neuen Link. Sobald er geöffnet ist, kannst du aufnehmen.“ / "The invite for this request is full. Send a new link. Once it's opened, you can let them in." Opening a new link moves the waiting request to it (no second request). For 24 hours after a decline the same household's links file no new request: the join screen says, in grey rather than the error colour, „Deine Anfrage für diesen Haushalt wurde vor Kurzem abgelehnt. Du kannst nach 24 Stunden wieder anfragen.“ / "Your request to join this household was declined recently. You can ask again after 24 hours." A request from someone who has become a member meanwhile (joined through a link while approval was off) isn't listed. „Aufnehmen“ for someone who has reached 20 households since asking lets nobody in: the request closes as cancelled and leaves the admins' list with no message (live: the section then says „Keine offenen Anfragen.“); the requester's open app shows the decline notice, but no 24-hour wait follows (that comes only after „Ablehnen“). The switcher shows a pending request as „Anfrage offen“.
- `paste-invite` (job 169b W2) „Einladung einfügen“ / "Paste invite" on the start screen, the login screen and „Willkommen bei Snacklist“: reads the clipboard only on the tap; takes a join link, a whole shared message or a bare code (dash optional) and opens the join flow; signed out it says „Einladung gemerkt. Melde dich an, dann trittst du bei.“ and joins after sign-in; nothing usable: „In der Zwischenablage ist kein Einladungslink und kein Code.“
- `household-cap` (job 173, 20 since job 200) a person is in at most 20 households, on Free and on Premium (`public.household_cap()`). At 20, „Haushalt beitreten“ on the join screen, „Neuer Haushalt“ (either button on the create page), a join request, restoring an archived household and an admin's „Aufnehmen“ all stop: the join and create screens say „Du bist schon in 20 Haushalten. Verlasse einen, bevor du einen neuen anlegst oder beitrittst.“ / "You are in 20 households already. Leave one before you create or join another." and nothing changes; „Aufnehmen“ closes the request (see `join-approval`). Old store builds still say 10 in that sentence. Someone already above 20 keeps every household and can add one only once below 20. After leaving one, the same invite joins. Journey: `node journeys/household-cap.mjs <dir>` (not through drive.sh: a sub-feature).
- `join-adds` (job 169b) joining while in a household no longer warns or leaves anything: the joined household is added and shown. A code or link of a household the user is already in shows that household (no join, the invite stays unused).
- `join-page` (job 169b W2) on the web `/join/<code>` is a static page (`app/web/join/`, no Flutter download), not the app: „{Inviter} lädt dich zu „{household}“ ein“ / "{Inviter} invites you to “{household}”" (the inviter's first name only once they set a name and while they are still in the household; else „Du bist zu „{household}“ eingeladen“ / "You’re invited to “{household}”": once the inviter leaves or is removed, the page no longer names them, and the invite still works), „1 Mitglied · 2 offene Einträge“ / "1 member · 2 open items", „Premium gilt für alle im Haushalt“ when it has Premium, the code as „ABCD-2345“ with „Code kopieren“ / "Copy code", and the button „Im Browser beitreten“ / "Join in the browser" (to `/?invite=<code>`). No item names, emails or ids in the page or in the answer of `preview_invite_public`. A used, revoked, expired or made-up code: „Diese Einladung gilt nicht mehr“ / "This invite no longer works" and „Code im Browser eingeben“; a full group link „Diese Einladung ist voll“; more than 30 opens an hour from one network (or 60 of one code) „Gerade zu viele Aufrufe“, joining still offered. German unless the browser's first language is English. On the phones the link opens the installed app instead (universal links, App Links).
- `link-join` the app at `/?invite=<code>` (the join page's button; `/join/<code>` in the phone apps) signed in goes to the join screen with the code previewed (address `/join/<code>`); signed out, the address becomes `/login?invite=<code>` (the sign-in, not the start screen) and the code survives sign-in, the analytics question and onboarding (kept 7 days; without a household „Willkommen bei Snacklist“ is skipped); a web sign-in by redirect (magic link, Google, Apple) comes back to `/?invite=<code>`. A guided start under way on that device puts its typed items on the joined household's list. A member opening their own household's link lands on the list.
- `rate-limit` 10 failed codes within an hour block even valid codes: „Zu viele Versuche. Bitte später noch einmal.“ / "Too many attempts. Please try again later."
- `premium-on-join` the join screen mentions Snacklist Lifetime when the target has it (since job 169b nothing is left, so no warning about the old household). Premium is not on sale yet (hold `premium-launch`), so a normal user sees none of this.
- `archive` „Archivierte Haushalte“ / "Archived households" in More and „Archivieren und verlassen“ / "Archive and leave" on the household page: off in production (see Gotchas).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Right after creating a household: the guided start's invite step (see onboarding).
- Later: tab „Mehr“ / "More", row „Partner einladen“ / "Invite your partner" (second group, one line; since job 85 no section heading and no household name), page „Einladungen“.
- Joining: „Mit Code beitreten“ in the household sheet (tap the household's name top left of the list); or an invite link; or, signed in without a household, „Einladung annehmen“ on „Willkommen bei Snacklist“.
- Household name: the chip on the list; renamed on the household page (More → the household's name). Switching: tap the chip.

## Gotchas

- `HOUSEHOLD_ARCHIVE` is not set in `app/config/auth.json` (the production web and store builds), so it is off for normal users: no „Archivierte Haushalte“ row, and the last member of a household with Lifetime leaving it sees the Lifetime-ends warning, not the archive offer. The e2e build turns it on; `household-bound.spec.ts` therefore shows behaviour users do not see. Peter's decision log says turning it on needs Legal Review first.
- Premium/Lifetime texts on the join screen are only reachable with a household that has a purchase; Premium is not on sale (hold `premium-launch`).
- Since job 169b the household page has the members list, rename and „Haushalt verlassen“; since W2 roles, removing members and naming admins. The household is deleted when its last member leaves.
- The join page allows 30 opens an hour per client (a salted hash of `X-Real-IP`) and 60 per code. Every local Playwright page is the same client, so a long run or reruns within the hour get „Gerade zu viele Aufrufe“; `invite-loop.spec.ts` empties `invite_preview_rate` (service role) before each test. Specs that only need the join flow open `/?invite=<code>` and leave the page alone.
- On the web the join screen's address is `/join/<code>`, and a reload there serves the static join page again (one more tap on „Im Browser beitreten“).
- A removed user's app learns it at its next refresh: back to the front (resume), opening „Deine Haushalte“ or a sign-in on any device; not live while it stays open in front.
- Someone made admin while their app is open gets the admin's menu on the household page only after their households are read again (reload, back to the front, „Deine Haushalte“), though their own row there already says „Admin“ (seen 2026-11-08, reported to the job lead).
- On the web the sheet's and „Einladungen“'s plain texts (the join-page hint, the section texts, the counts, „Noch niemand.“, who joined) sit in the name of one group, not in nodes of their own: look for them by `getByRole('group', { name: /2 von 50 beigetreten/ })` (`shown()` in `invite-loop.spec.ts`); headings and buttons are their own nodes. A member row on the household page is one text, the name then the role („Peter Trost (du) Hauptadmin“), or a group of that name when it has a menu.
- Siri, Alexa and AI assistants over OAuth act in the default household, not the one shown; API keys and access tokens are bound to the household shown when they were made.
- With join approval on, the requester's app hears the decision live (realtime on `join_requests`); another admin's flip of the switch shows on an open household page only after the households are read again (`households` is not in the realtime publication).
- Headless Chromium has no Web Share, so „Teilen“ is absent in Playwright (driven 2026-11-04).
- Revoking is not undoable; there is no "revoke all".
- The invite link host: on the web it is the page's own origin, on the phones `APP_URL`: https://app.snacklist.app since job 174 (Hummlist builds: https://app.hummlist.com). The share sheet text uses the German or English variant of the app's language.
- Codes are compared upper-cased with other characters ignored; they never go to analytics.
- 7-day expiry and single use are in the database (invite default `now() + interval '7 days'`, `used_at` set on join); the pending invite on a device also lasts 7 days.
