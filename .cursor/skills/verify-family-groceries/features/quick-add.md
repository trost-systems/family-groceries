# Quick add

The add bar at the bottom of the list ("Was brauchst du?") opens the add sheet ("Hinzufügen") as soon as the user types or taps it. The sheet lays a scrim over the list and offers rows for what is typed: the typed line itself ("Wie getippt") first, then what the household bought before, other packs, kinds, sizes and brands, and words from the catalog. Enter takes the first row; a tap takes any row. Two chips set the store and category for the next row taken. After an add the field clears, focus stays, and a note under the chips says what was added and where, with "Rückgängig". With the field empty the sheet offers what is due again. Escape, "Schließen" or a tap on the scrim closes it and clears the field. On desktop and web the field has the cursor when the app opens; on phones the keyboard comes up on a tap. A tap on an item's row ticks it (no snackbar since job 29b), a left swipe opens its edit sheet, whose Löschen deletes it (job 29c; the swipe used to delete), and a tap on a ticked item under "Zuletzt verwendet" (the list's own row, faded) puts it back, which is also the tick's undo.

## Sub-features

- `qa-autofocus` puts the cursor in the add field (key `add-item-field`, hint "Was brauchst du?") when the app opens on the list, on desktop and web only.
- `qa-sheet` opens the add sheet (semantics group "Hinzufügen", key `add-sheet`) on the first keystroke or a tap on the field. The tabs give way to the hint "Antippen fügt hinzu · ↵ nimmt den ersten Vorschlag" (key `add-sheet-hint`).
- `qa-rows` lists rows `suggestion-0`, `suggestion-1`, … Each is one button labelled "<qty> <name>, <source>, <brand>, <properties…>, <store>" (parts that are empty are left out). Sources: Wie getippt, Schon mal gekauft, Andere Packung, Andere Sorte, Andere Größe, Andere Marke, Aus dem Katalog, Wieder fällig. The typed row is always first; alternatives already on the list are left out.
- `qa-enter` adds `suggestion-0` on Enter (the typed line, parsed), clears the field and keeps focus in it, so the next item needs no tap. The sheet stays open.
- `qa-quantity` splits a leading number into the quantity: "2 Äpfel" is stored as Äpfel with "2", and the row reads "2× Äpfel"; "2 l Milch" reads "2 l Milch".
- `qa-tap-row` adds a tapped row with every field it shows (quantity, brand, properties, store, category) and keeps focus in the field.
- `qa-phone-web-tap` (job 28): in a phone's browser one tap on the field focuses its input during the tap, which is what iOS needs for the keyboard. After a tapped row or chip the keyboard goes away with the tap, the field keeps focus, and the next single tap on it brings the keyboard back (DECISIONS job 28, 6). `e2e/tests/iphone.spec.ts` proves it in WebKit as an iPhone by logging every focus change. fg cannot drive it (no WebKit).
- `qa-fuzzy` (job 59): "Schon mal gekauft" and "Aus dem Katalog" rows also come from a typo or part of a word: "Hafermlch" and "milch" both offer Hafermilch. Typos from 3 letters (one edit up to 5 letters, two from 6, same first letter), part of a word from 4; shorter input matches the start only. Within a source: same name, starts with, inside, typo (DECISIONS job 59).
- `qa-chips` sets the store (`chip-store`, options `store-option-<id>`, `store-option-none` "Ohne Laden", and `store-option-new` "+ Neuer Laden", which makes a store and picks it: `features/stores.md`, `st-own-picker`) or category (`chip-category`, options `category-option-<id>`) for the row taken next, whichever row that is. A pick that differs from the guess teaches the store or category memory for that name (`features/stores.md`, `features/classifier.md`).
- `qa-added` shows "✓ Hinzugefügt: <name> → <store>" (or "✓ Hinzugefügt: <name>" without a store) in `added-note` until the next keystroke; `added-undo` ("Rückgängig") removes the item again, takes back what a chip pick on that add taught the memory, and hands focus back to the field.
- `qa-duplicate` refuses an item already on the list: the note reads "<name> steht schon auf der Liste", without the "✓" of an add, and has no undo.
- `qa-dedupe-hint` (job 32b): an item whose spelling variant or synonym is open ("Karotten" with "Möhren", same `items.canonical_key`) is added anyway. Once the server confirms it, the sheet's note gains a line (key `dedupe-hint`) "Möhren steht schon auf der Liste" with "Zusammenführen" (`dedupe-merge`); with the sheet closed (or once it closes) the same is a snackbar for six seconds. Ignored, both stay. Merged, Möhren keeps its text with the quantities merged on the server (`merge_items`: "500 g" + "1 kg" = "1,5 kg", "1 Bund" + "500 g" = "1 Bund + 500 g") and Karotten goes; the note (or snackbar) `dedupe-merged` says "Zusammengeführt: 1,5 kg Möhren" with Rückgängig (`dedupe-undo`, `unmerge_items`), which brings both back exactly.
- `qa-clear` empties the field with the ✕ (`add-clear`, tooltip "Eingabe leeren") while the sheet stays open.
- `qa-close` closes the sheet with "Schließen" (`add-sheet-close`), Escape or a tap on the scrim (`add-sheet-scrim`), and clears the field and the chip picks. After a close, the field getting focus back by any route opens the sheet again, also a web click that reaches the app as a semantics tap or focus (job 52; before, such a click showed a cursor but no sheet).
- `qa-due` shows the due usuals as "Wieder fällig" rows while the field is empty: at least 2 purchases on different days, and the usual gap since the last one has passed (up to 4 times the gap; DECISIONS 8.2). After them come "Zuletzt verwendet" rows (job 59): usuals added or bought in the last 30 days, not due and not on the list, the latest first, 10 rows in all. With nothing due or recent the sheet says "Tippe, um Vorschläge zu sehen" (`add-sheet-empty`).
- `qa-offline` without the server builds the rows on the device: the typed line, then the usuals that match it as the server matches ("Schon mal gekauft"): same name, start, a word's start, inside, typo (`qa-fuzzy`).
- `qa-tick-undo` ticks an item off with a tap on its row, with no snackbar, and a tap on it under "Zuletzt verwendet" restores it in its place.
- `qa-delete` deletes an item with Löschen in the edit sheet a left swipe opens ("<name> gelöscht", undo). The swipe alone deletes nothing.
- `qa-readd` puts an item from "Zuletzt verwendet" back on the list ("<name> steht wieder auf der Liste"). Its row there is the list's row (icon, quantity, details), faded, key `used-<id>`; only a tap (or a hold) acts on it, a swipe or right click does nothing.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the app while signed in to a household. The list tab ("Liste") is the home screen; the add bar sits above the tabs.
- Type into the bar, or tap it, and press Enter or tap a row.
- Tap the store chip ("Laden: automatisch" or the store's name) or the category chip ("Kategorie: automatisch" or the category's name) in the sheet before taking a row.
- Tap "Rückgängig" in the note after an add.
- Tap the ✕ in the bar, "Schließen", the dimmed list, or press Escape.
- Tap the empty bar to see what is due.
- Tap an item's row; swipe it to the left and tap Löschen in the sheet.
- Expand "Zuletzt verwendet" at the bottom of the list and tap an entry.

## Gotchas

- Reopening the sheet starts blank, also right after a close that hasn't finished: no note, no rows (`fg press escape`, then at once `fg tap --key add-item-field`: `added-note` and `suggestion-0` count 0).
- While the sheet is open, the list under the scrim is out of reach: `fg find` and `fg wait` don't see its rows, and a tap there closes the sheet. After an add, wait for the note (`--contains "Hinzugefügt: <name>"`), not the row; close the sheet (`fg press escape`) before acting on the list.
- `fg wait --text Milch` with the sheet open can match the row's own name text inside the sheet. Match the row's label ("Milch, Wie getippt") or the note instead.
- Autofocus happens on open only, and only on desktop and web. Escape, Schließen, the scrim and a tap on the list take focus away, and `fg type` without a matcher then fails with `no_focus`. Use `--key add-item-field` (it taps the field, which also opens the sheet), and `fg restart` to prove autofocus again. After an add, a row tap or a chip pick, focus stays.
- On the web the first key after a tap on the unfocused field is kept even when it comes before Flutter's next frame (`EarlyKeys`, job 34). The proof is the web e2e `add-sheet.spec.ts` "a key typed before the next frame", which holds the frames back; the gap is in the web engine's semantics text field, so `fg` on Android cannot show it.
- Enter takes `suggestion-0`, which is always "Wie getippt". To add a suggestion, tap its row.
- The rows come from the server a moment after the last keystroke. Wait for the row's label before tapping it by index; the index of a row depends on the history and catalog.
- A chip pick applies to the row taken next, whichever it is, then resets. It also teaches memory, so later adds of that name land in that store. Clean up the memory row after the recipe. Rows taken without a pick teach nothing: every row carries the store and category the list guesses now, history rows included. Deleting the item within 10 minutes of the add (Rückgängig) takes the lesson back (`items_unlearn_delete`).
- On Linux there is no soft keyboard and no microphone button (`dictation-button` shows on phones only). "Keyboard up on tap" can only be proven on the android surface with `fg screenshot --native`.
- The UI is optimistic: an item shows before the server has it. Assert stored state with `fg db query`, and repeat the query if it lags for a second or two.
- `--text` and `--row` match the list row's exact text, which starts with the quantity: "2 l Milch", "2× Äpfel" ("×" for a bare count). A row without a quantity reads the name. With Milch both on the list and in "Zuletzt verwendet", the used row has the same text: tap it by its key `used-<id>`.
- With "Zuletzt verwendet" expanded, the ticked name shows again there, so `fg wait --text Milch --gone` never succeeds. Close it first (tap its title), or check the status with `fg db query`.
- A tap on a row ticks it; there is no checkbox. The line draws across the name and the row closes, and the tick commits 440 ms after the tap (job 57; with reduced motion the struck row holds 260 ms instead), so wait with `fg wait --text <row text> --gone` before checking the database. A screenshot right after the tap shows the struck row. To open an item, hold it: `fg marionette -- long-press --text <row text>`.
- The usuals reload 1.5 s after the list changes and on start. Backdated history shows after `fg restart`.
- A new item takes the store memory holds for its name (`features/stores.md`), so it may land under a store rather than "Ohne Laden"; the note then ends in "→ <store>".
- Button labels (Rückgängig, Schließen) do not show in `fg find`, but `fg tap --text` reaches them. Prefer the keys.
- Items from earlier runs stay in "Zuletzt verwendet", and their purchases show up as "Schon mal gekauft" rows. Filter every query by household: scenario runs on the same stack have their own Milch.
- One purchase is never due, and neither are purchases on the same day: backdate the history as above instead of ticking twice. A tick undone within 10 minutes (a tap on it under "Zuletzt verwendet") is not a purchase, and the app sends no `item_added` for it.
