# Quick add

The add bar at the bottom of the list ("Was brauchst du?") opens the add sheet ("Hinzufügen") as soon as the user types or taps it. The sheet lays a scrim over the list and offers rows for what is typed: the typed line itself ("Wie getippt") first, then what the household bought before, other packs, kinds, sizes and brands, and words from the catalog. Enter takes the first row; a tap takes any row. Two chips set the store and category for the next row taken. After an add the field clears, focus stays, and a note under the chips says what was added and where, with "Rückgängig". With the field empty the sheet offers what is due again. Escape, "Schließen" or a tap on the scrim closes it and clears the field. On desktop and web the field has the cursor when the app opens; on phones the keyboard comes up on a tap. A tap on an item's row ticks it (undo snackbar), a left swipe opens its edit sheet, whose Löschen deletes it (job 29c; the swipe used to delete), and "Zuletzt gekauft" puts ticked items back.

## Sub-features

- `qa-autofocus` puts the cursor in the add field (key `add-item-field`, hint "Was brauchst du?") when the app opens on the list, on desktop and web only.
- `qa-sheet` opens the add sheet (semantics group "Hinzufügen", key `add-sheet`) on the first keystroke or a tap on the field. The tabs give way to the hint "Antippen fügt hinzu · ↵ nimmt den ersten Vorschlag" (key `add-sheet-hint`).
- `qa-rows` lists rows `suggestion-0`, `suggestion-1`, … Each is one button labelled "<qty> <name>, <source>, <brand>, <properties…>, <store>" (parts that are empty are left out). Sources: Wie getippt, Schon mal gekauft, Andere Packung, Andere Sorte, Andere Größe, Andere Marke, Aus dem Katalog, Wieder fällig. The typed row is always first; alternatives already on the list are left out.
- `qa-enter` adds `suggestion-0` on Enter (the typed line, parsed), clears the field and keeps focus in it, so the next item needs no tap. The sheet stays open.
- `qa-quantity` splits a leading number into the quantity: "2 Äpfel" is stored as Äpfel with "2", and the row reads "2× Äpfel"; "2 l Milch" reads "2 l Milch".
- `qa-tap-row` adds a tapped row with every field it shows (quantity, brand, properties, store, category) and keeps focus in the field.
- `qa-phone-web-tap` (job 28): in a phone's browser one tap on the field focuses its input during the tap, which is what iOS needs for the keyboard. After a tapped row or chip the keyboard goes away with the tap, the field keeps focus, and the next single tap on it brings the keyboard back (DECISIONS job 28, 6). `e2e/tests/iphone.spec.ts` proves it in WebKit as an iPhone by logging every focus change. fg cannot drive it (no WebKit).
- `qa-chips` sets the store (`chip-store`, options `store-option-<id>`, `store-option-none` "Ohne Laden") or category (`chip-category`, options `category-option-<id>`) for the row taken next, whichever row that is. A pick that differs from the guess teaches the store or category memory for that name (`features/stores.md`, `features/classifier.md`).
- `qa-added` shows "✓ Hinzugefügt: <name> → <store>" (or "✓ Hinzugefügt: <name>" without a store) in `added-note` until the next keystroke; `added-undo` ("Rückgängig") removes the item again, takes back what a chip pick on that add taught the memory, and hands focus back to the field.
- `qa-duplicate` refuses an item already on the list: the note reads "✓ <name> steht schon auf der Liste" and has no undo.
- `qa-clear` empties the field with the ✕ (`add-clear`, tooltip "Eingabe leeren") while the sheet stays open.
- `qa-close` closes the sheet with "Schließen" (`add-sheet-close`), Escape or a tap on the scrim (`add-sheet-scrim`), and clears the field and the chip picks.
- `qa-due` shows the due usuals as "Wieder fällig" rows while the field is empty: at least 2 purchases on different days, and the usual gap since the last one has passed (up to 4 times the gap; DECISIONS 8.2). With nothing due the sheet says "Tippe, um Vorschläge zu sehen" (`add-sheet-empty`).
- `qa-offline` without the server builds the rows on the device: the typed line, then the usuals that start like it ("Schon mal gekauft").
- `qa-tick-undo` ticks an item off with a tap on its row, shows "<name> abgehakt" with "Rückgängig", and undo restores it in its place.
- `qa-delete` deletes an item with Löschen in the edit sheet a left swipe opens ("<name> gelöscht", undo). The swipe alone deletes nothing.
- `qa-readd` puts an item from "Zuletzt gekauft" back on the list ("<name> steht wieder auf der Liste").

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the app while signed in to a household. The list tab ("Liste") is the home screen; the add bar sits above the tabs.
- Type into the bar, or tap it, and press Enter or tap a row.
- Tap "Laden" or "Kategorie" in the sheet before taking a row.
- Tap "Rückgängig" in the note after an add.
- Tap the ✕ in the bar, "Schließen", the dimmed list, or press Escape.
- Tap the empty bar to see what is due.
- Tap an item's row; swipe it to the left and tap Löschen in the sheet.
- Expand "Zuletzt gekauft" at the bottom of the list and tap an entry or its "+" button.

## Driving it with fg

Preconditions:

- Baseline from `features/README.md`; peter signed in (`fg doctor` shows `peter.marionette ... screen list`).
- `H="(select id from households where name = 'Familie Test')"`. None of the test items are active: `fg db query "update items set status = 'bought' where status = 'active' and name in ('Milch', 'Äpfel', 'Brot', 'Brötchen', 'Butter') and household_id = $H" --write`.
- Note the stores: `fg db query "select id, name from stores where household_id = $H order by sort_order" --as peter`. `EDEKA=<Edeka's id>` below.

- **`qa-autofocus`.** Run `fg restart`. On linux, if `fg find --key login-email` returns a match, run `fg login peter` (see Gotchas). Then `fg wait --key add-item-field` and `fg screenshot --name qa-autofocus-opened`: the bar shows "Was brauchst du?" with a cursor, the sheet is closed (`fg find --key add-sheet` counts 0).
- **`qa-sheet`, `qa-rows`.** Run `fg type --input Milch` (no matcher: the focused field; it fails with `no_focus` if `qa-autofocus` is broken). `fg wait --text "Milch, Wie getippt"` succeeds: that is `suggestion-0`'s label. `fg find --type Semantics | jq -r '.elements[].label'` lists "Hinzufügen", the chips ("Laden: automatisch", "Kategorie: Kühlregal") and each row ("Milchreis, Aus dem Katalog", "Milchpulver, Aus dem Katalog, dm", …). `fg find --key add-sheet-hint` counts 1. Take `fg screenshot --name qa-sheet`.
- **`qa-enter`.** Run `fg press enter`. `fg wait --contains "Hinzugefügt: Milch"` succeeds (the note ends in "→ <store>" when memory knows one), `fg find --key add-item-field` shows no `text`, and `fg db query "select count(*) from items where status = 'active' and name = 'Milch' and household_id = $H"` returns 1.
- **`qa-quantity`, focus kept.** Run `fg type --input "2 Äpfel"` (no matcher again), `fg wait --text "2× Äpfel, Wie getippt"`, then `fg press enter` and `fg wait --contains "Hinzugefügt: Äpfel"`.
- **`qa-duplicate`.** Run `fg type --input milch --submit`. `fg wait --text "✓ Milch steht schon auf der Liste"` succeeds, `fg find --key added-undo` counts 0, and the count query above still returns 1.
- **`qa-close` with Escape, then the list.** Run `fg press escape` and `fg wait --key add-sheet --gone`. Now the list shows: `fg wait --text Milch`, `fg wait --text "2× Äpfel"`, and `fg find --row "2× Äpfel" --type Semantics` returns the label "Äpfel\n2". Escape also took focus away: `fg type --input x` fails with `no_focus`.
- **`qa-tap-row`, `qa-chips`.** Run `fg tap --key add-item-field`, `fg type --key add-item-field --input Brot` and `fg wait --contains "Brötchen, Aus dem Katalog"` (`fg find --type Semantics` names its index; `suggestion-1` below; the label ends in ", Edeka" if an earlier run left the memory). Run `fg tap --key chip-store`, `fg wait --key store-option-$EDEKA`, `fg tap --key store-option-$EDEKA` and `fg screenshot --name qa-chip-picked`. Then `fg tap --key suggestion-1`. `fg wait --text "✓ Hinzugefügt: Brötchen → Edeka"` succeeds, and `fg db query "select s.name from items i join stores s on s.id = i.store_id where i.status = 'active' and i.name = 'Brötchen' and i.household_id = $H"` returns Edeka. The pick taught memory: `fg db query "select item_key, store_id from item_store_memory where household_id = $H and item_key = 'brotch'"` returns Edeka's id. Focus stayed: `fg type --input Butter --submit` (no matcher) succeeds.
- **`qa-added` undo.** After that Butter add, `fg wait --contains "Hinzugefügt: Butter"`, then `fg tap --key added-undo` and `fg wait --key added-note --gone`. Within a few seconds `fg db query "select count(*) from items where status = 'active' and name = 'Butter' and household_id = $H"` returns 0. Focus is back in the field: `fg type --input Kaffee` (no matcher) succeeds; `fg tap --key add-clear` empties it again.
- **`qa-clear`.** Run `fg type --key add-item-field --input Kaffee`, `fg wait --text "Kaffee, Wie getippt"`, `fg tap --key add-clear`. `fg find --key add-item-field` shows no `text` and `fg find --key add-sheet` still counts 1.
- **`qa-close` with the scrim and Schließen.** Run `fg type --key add-item-field --input Kaffee`, then `fg tap --x 206 --y 100` (the dimmed list above the sheet; `fg tap --key add-sheet-scrim` aims at the scrim's center, which the sheet may cover) and `fg wait --key add-sheet --gone`. Run `fg tap --key add-item-field`: the sheet opens with an empty field (`fg find --key add-item-field` shows no `text`). Run `fg type --key add-item-field --input Tee`, `fg tap --key add-sheet-close`, `fg wait --key add-sheet --gone`; nothing was added (`fg db query "select count(*) from items where status = 'active' and name in ('Kaffee', 'Tee') and household_id = $H"` returns 0).
- **`qa-rows` from history.** Buy Milch with a quantity: `fg tap --text Milch`, `fg wait --contains "Milch abgehakt"`, `fg tap --key add-item-field`, `fg type --key add-item-field --input "2 l Milch" --submit`, `fg wait --contains "Hinzugefügt: Milch"`, `fg press escape`, `fg tap --text "2 l Milch"`, `fg wait --text "2 l Milch" --gone`. Then `fg tap --key add-item-field`, `fg type --key add-item-field --input Mi`, and `fg wait --text "2 l Milch, Schon mal gekauft"` (the row after "Mi, Wie getippt"). Take `fg screenshot --name qa-history`. `fg tap --key suggestion-1` adds it: `fg wait --contains "Hinzugefügt: Milch"`, and `fg db query "select quantity from items where status = 'active' and name = 'Milch' and household_id = $H"` returns `2 l`.
- **`qa-due`.** Give Brötchen two purchases a week apart: `fg press escape`, `fg tap --text Brötchen`, `fg wait --contains "Brötchen abgehakt"`, then `fg db query "update item_events set created_at = now() - interval '7 days' - case action when 'added' then interval '1 hour' else interval '0' end where item_name = 'Brötchen' and household_id = $H" --write` and `fg db query "insert into item_events (household_id, item_id, action, item_name, created_at) select household_id, item_id, action, item_name, created_at - interval '7 days' from item_events where item_name = 'Brötchen' and household_id = $H" --write`. Run `fg restart` so the usuals reload, `fg wait --key add-item-field`, then `fg tap --key add-item-field`. `fg wait --text "Brötchen, Wieder fällig"` succeeds; take `fg screenshot --name qa-due`. `fg tap --key suggestion-0` adds it: `fg wait --contains "Hinzugefügt: Brötchen"`. Close the sheet (`fg press escape`, `fg wait --key add-sheet --gone`) and open it again (`fg tap --key add-item-field`): Brötchen is on the list now, so it is not due, and with nothing else due `fg wait --key add-sheet-empty` succeeds ("Tippe, um Vorschläge zu sehen").
- **`qa-tick-undo`.** Run `fg press escape`, then `fg tap --text Brötchen` (a tap anywhere on the row ticks it), `fg wait --text Brötchen --gone` and `fg wait --contains "Brötchen abgehakt"`. Run `fg tap --text Rückgängig` and `fg wait --text Brötchen`: the row is back in its section.
- **`qa-delete`.** Run `fg swipe --row Brötchen --direction left` and `fg wait --key edit-sheet` (the swipe opens the sheet; Brötchen is still on the list), then `fg tap --key edit-delete` and `fg wait --text Brötchen --gone`. Within a few seconds `fg db query "select count(*) from items where name = 'Brötchen' and status = 'active' and household_id = $H"` returns 0.
- **`qa-readd`.** Tick "2 l Milch" (`fg tap --text "2 l Milch"`), then run `fg marionette -- scroll-to --text "Zuletzt gekauft"`, `fg tap --text "Zuletzt gekauft"` and `fg tap --row Milch --type IconButton`. `fg wait --contains "steht wieder auf der Liste"` matches, and Milch is back in its section.
- **`qa-offline`.** Not drivable on linux or android: fg cannot take a native client offline. Report it as skipped; the web e2e test "suggestions come from the device cache when Supabase is unreachable" (`e2e/tests/suggestions.spec.ts`) covers it.
- **Proof.** Record across the adds: `fg record start --name qa-enter` before typing and `fg record stop` after the last wait. Take `fg screenshot --name qa-after` and run a `fg db query` showing the rows and their status.
- **Cleanup.** `fg db query "update items set status = 'bought' where status = 'active' and name in ('Milch', 'Äpfel', 'Brot', 'Brötchen') and household_id = $H" --write` and `fg db query "delete from item_store_memory where household_id = $H and item_key = 'brotch'" --write`.

## Gotchas

- Reopening the sheet before it has finished closing keeps its content (the note, the rows). Wait for `fg wait --key add-sheet --gone` between a close and the next open.
- While the sheet is open, the list under the scrim is out of reach: `fg find` and `fg wait` don't see its rows, and a tap there closes the sheet. After an add, wait for the note (`--contains "Hinzugefügt: <name>"`), not the row; close the sheet (`fg press escape`) before acting on the list.
- `fg wait --text Milch` with the sheet open can match the row's own name text inside the sheet. Match the row's label ("Milch, Wie getippt") or the note instead.
- Autofocus happens on open only, and only on desktop and web. Escape, Schließen, the scrim and a tap on the list take focus away, and `fg type` without a matcher then fails with `no_focus`. Use `--key add-item-field` (it taps the field, which also opens the sheet), and `fg restart` to prove autofocus again. After an add, a row tap or a chip pick, focus stays.
- Enter takes `suggestion-0`, which is always "Wie getippt". To add a suggestion, tap its row.
- The rows come from the server a moment after the last keystroke. Wait for the row's label before tapping it by index; the index of a row depends on the history and catalog.
- A chip pick applies to the row taken next, whichever it is, then resets. It also teaches memory, so later adds of that name land in that store. Clean up the memory row after the recipe. Rows taken without a pick teach nothing: every row carries the store and category the list guesses now, history rows included. Deleting the item within 10 minutes of the add (Rückgängig) takes the lesson back (`items_unlearn_delete`).
- The added note keeps its "✓" for a refusal too ("✓ Milch steht schon auf der Liste").
- On Linux there is no soft keyboard and no microphone button (`dictation-button` shows on phones only). "Keyboard up on tap" can only be proven on the android surface with `fg screenshot --native`.
- The UI is optimistic: an item shows before the server has it. Assert stored state with `fg db query`, and repeat the query if it lags for a second or two.
- `--text` and `--row` match the list row's exact text, which starts with the quantity: "2 l Milch", "2× Äpfel" ("×" for a bare count). A row without a quantity reads the name. With Milch both on the list and in "Zuletzt gekauft", `--row Milch` targets the list row.
- With "Zuletzt gekauft" expanded, the ticked name still shows there, so `fg wait --text Milch --gone` never succeeds. Wait for the snackbar (`--contains "Milch abgehakt"`) instead.
- A tap on a row ticks it at once; there is no checkbox. To open an item, hold it: `fg marionette -- long-press --text <row text>`.
- The usuals reload 1.5 s after the list changes and on start. Backdated history shows after `fg restart`.
- A new item takes the store memory holds for its name (`features/stores.md`), so it may land under a store rather than "Ohne Laden"; the note then ends in "→ <store>".
- Button labels (Rückgängig, Schließen) do not show in `fg find`, but `fg tap --text` reaches them. Prefer the keys.
- Items from earlier runs stay in "Zuletzt gekauft", and their purchases show up as "Schon mal gekauft" rows. Filter every query by household: scenario runs on the same stack have their own Milch.
- One purchase is never due, and neither are purchases on the same day: backdate the history as above instead of ticking twice. A tick undone within 10 minutes (with "Rückgängig" or a re-add from "Zuletzt gekauft") is not a purchase.
