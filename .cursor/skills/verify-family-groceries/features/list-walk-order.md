# List and walk order

The list is one scroll of sections, one per store in the household's store order, then "Ohne Laden" for items without a store. Each store starts with a divider "<Store>, N Artikel" (the name in a hex badge, a cobalt rule) and a fold caret. Inside a store, small uppercase category dividers follow that store's walk order, and the rows under each follow the household's walk order (`sort_key`). A row is a full-width checkbox for screen readers (unchecked, checked while it leaves; job 57), with no visible check box: a tap ticks it, a hold or a right click opens the edit sheet, a swipe does nothing (job 78; it edited since job 29c and deleted before). Nothing on the list drags (job 78): an item changes store or category in the edit sheet, and the order of stores and categories lives under Liste → Anpassen (`/edit`, a store's details `/edit/<store id>` or `/edit/none`). A tap on a store divider folds it; a hold or right click on any divider does nothing (job 171). Folds and the dismissed „Erste Schritte“ card are kept per device. A partner's adds and moves show live.

## Sub-features

- `lw-sections` shows a divider per store in store order (key `store-<store id>`, label "<Store>, N Artikel"), and "Ohne Laden" last (key `store-none`). Only sections with open items show: an empty store has no divider and no "0 Artikel".
- `lw-category-dividers` shows a store's categories in its layout order as uppercase dividers (key `category-<store id or none>-<category id>`): the text drops a leading emoji and turns ß into SS ("👶 Drogerie" reads DROGERIE), the semantics label keeps the full name.
- `lw-rows` reads the quantity first ("2 l Milch", "6× Bananen", a bare "Gurke"), with "Marke · Eigenschaften" under it, an emoji at the left and no checkbox.
- `lw-walk` orders the rows of a category by `sort_key`, not by add order.
- `lw-tick` ticks an item with a tap on its row, without a snackbar (job 29b); a tap on it under "Zuletzt verwendet" puts it back in the same place.
- `lw-no-drag` a hold on a row opens the edit sheet once the hold completes (about 300 ms), while the finger is still down; moving afterwards moves nothing, and no empty store ever shows as a drop target. A finger may drift 18 px and still hold.
- `lw-divider-tap` a tap on a store divider only folds or unfolds it (`lw-fold`). A hold or a right click on a store divider ("Ohne Laden" included) or a category divider does nothing (job 171; before, it pushed the store's details): no route, no fold, no menu, nothing moves. A screen reader's store divider has a tap action and no long-press action, no up/down moves; a category divider has no action. A store's details open only from Liste → Anpassen.
- `lw-fold` folds a store with a tap on its divider (the caret turns from ˅ to ›); the fold survives a restart.
- `lw-hint` shows the „Erste Schritte“ card (key `list-hint`, job 168b; "Tippen hakt ab · Halten bearbeitet" is its „Einmal einkaufen“ line) until its X (`list-hint-dismiss`, „Erste Schritte ausblenden“); it stays gone after a restart.
- `lw-empty` shows the empty state (key `list-empty`) when the list has no items: a honey pointy hex with the cart icon (`list-empty-mark`, job 57) over "Die Liste ist leer. Tippe unten ein, was fehlt."
- `lw-partner` tints a partner's new row (key `new-<id>`, label ends in "Neu") with "N neu von <name>" under the title (`new-items-subtitle`) and no initial; the tint clears after 3 s on screen.
- `lw-partner-move` shows a partner's move (edit sheet) on the other client without a reload.
- `lw-top-bar` (job 29a, floating since job 148): the bar at the top of the list (key `list-header`) is 68 px under the top inset in every state: 52 px high, 8 px below the inset, with the raised hex button "Anpassen" (`list-edit`, 48 px, no frame around it since job 152) at the top right and, only while there is something to show, a small hex capsule (`list-status`, 48 px, the tab pill's sticker shadow) to its left holding `new-items-subtitle`. While offline or failing, the sync indicator's pill (`sync-pill`, job 161) sits at the bar's left end and its card (`sync-indicator`) floats under the bar; neither moves Anpassen or the list. It is the list's pinned header: the rows scroll on under it, and a row brought into view (focus, a screen reader, a `/stores/<id>` link) stops below it. Rows beside the button and the capsule stay tappable. No household name, no item count; the list never moves when the indicator comes or goes.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open the app while signed in to a household. The list sits above the add bar.
- Tap a row to tick it; to undo, open "Zuletzt verwendet" at the bottom of the list and tap the item there.
- Press and hold a row (or right-click it) to edit it; its store and category chips move it.
- Tap a store divider to fold or unfold it. Holding or right-clicking a store or category divider does nothing.
- Tap the X („Erste Schritte ausblenden“) on the card above the list.

## Driving it with fg

Preconditions:

- `fg up --clients peter,lea`.
- Seed the walk: `fg seed h-list-walk` (household "Familie Berg": the stores Drogerie, Supermarkt and Wochenmarkt in that order, plus "Ohne Laden", every item with brand, properties and `walkOrder`). Note the run id (`R`). The seed leaves running clients signed in as peter and lea, so run `fg logout --client peter` and `fg logout --client lea` first (`fg login` otherwise fails with `not_on_login`), then `fg login $R:hanna --client peter` and `fg login $R:jonas --client lea`. Use `--as $R:hanna` on every query, so it reads only this household.
- The ids for keys: `fg db query "select name, id from stores order by sort_order" --as $R:hanna`, and the divider keys from `fg find --contains store-` and `fg find --contains category-`.
- The order query used below: `Q="select s.name as store, c.name as category, i.name, i.sort_key from items i join categories c on c.id = i.category_id left join stores s on s.id = i.store_id where i.status = 'active' order by s.sort_order nulls last, i.sort_key"`.

- **Sections and dividers.** Run `fg find --contains "Artikel"`: the labels read "Drogerie, 3 Artikel", "Supermarkt, 7 Artikel" (and "Wochenmarkt, 2 Artikel" further down). The store order follows `select name, sort_order from stores` (the scenario's: Drogerie, Supermarkt, Wochenmarkt). `fg find --type Text` lists DROGERIE then HAUSHALT under the Drogerie, OBST & GEMÜSE, KÜHLREGAL, GETRÄNKE under the Supermarkt. `fg find --contains "👶"` shows the Semantics label "👶 Drogerie" on the divider. `fg marionette -- scroll-to --key store-none` brings "Ohne Laden, 2 Artikel" with Brot and Eier into view. Take `fg screenshot --name lw-sections`.
- **Rows.** `fg find --type Text` lists "1 kg Äpfel" then "Elstar", "6× Bananen" then "Bio", "Gurke", "2 l Milch" then "Milbona · 1,5 %". `fg find --type Checkbox` counts 0.
- **Walk order.** Under OBST & GEMÜSE the rows read Äpfel, Bananen, Gurke (`sort_key` 1024, 2048, 3072), although Bananen was added first. `fg db query "$Q" --as $R:hanna` shows the same order.
- **Tick and put back in place.** Run `fg tap --text "6× Bananen"` and `fg wait --text "6× Bananen" --gone`; no snackbar shows (`fg find --contains abgehakt` counts 0). `fg db query "select status from items where name = 'Bananen'" --as $R:hanna` returns bought. Run `fg marionette -- scroll-to --text "Zuletzt verwendet"`, `fg tap --text "Zuletzt verwendet"`, then tap the row there by its key: `fg find --contains Bananen` lists it as `used-<id>` (label "Bananen\nBio\n6\nWieder auf die Liste"), and `fg tap --key used-<id>` puts it back. It looks like the list's row, icon and quantity included, faded, but `fg tap --text "6× Bananen"` finds nothing there. `fg wait --text "6× Bananen"` succeeds and no snackbar shows (`fg find --contains "wieder auf der Liste"` counts 0): the texts read "1 kg Äpfel", "6× Bananen", "Gurke" again, and the status is active.
- **Move in the edit sheet.** Run `fg marionette -- long-press --text Gurke`, `fg wait --key edit-sheet`, `fg tap --key chip-category`, `fg tap --key category-option-<Kühlregal id>`, `fg tap --key edit-done`. `$Q` shows Gurke under Supermarkt, Kühlregal. The same with `chip-store` and `store-option-<Drogerie id>` moves it to the Drogerie, and `fg db query "select s.name from item_store_memory m join stores s on s.id = m.store_id where m.item_key = item_key('Gurke')" --as $R:hanna` returns Drogerie.
- **Divider tap.** Run `fg marionette -- long-press --key store-<Supermarkt id>` and `fg marionette -- secondary-tap --key store-<Supermarkt id>`: `fg find --key store-details` counts 0 and `fg find --text "2 l Milch"` still counts 1 (not folded). The same on `category-<Supermarkt id>-<Getränke id>` and on `store-none`. `fg db query "select name, sort_order from stores order by sort_order" --as $R:hanna` is unchanged: a hold moves nothing. Then `fg tap --key store-<Supermarkt id>`: `fg wait --text "2 l Milch" --gone`, and `fg find --key store-details` still counts 0; tap again to unfold.
- **Hold edit.** Run `fg marionette -- long-press --text "2 l Milch"` and `fg wait --key edit-sheet`, then `fg tap --key edit-cancel`. `fg marionette -- secondary-tap --text Gurke` opens it the same way (a right click).
- **Fold persists.** Run `fg tap --key store-<Drogerie id>`, `fg wait --text Windeln --gone`, `fg restart` and `fg wait --key add-item-field`. `fg find --text Windeln` counts 0, and the Drogerie divider is still there. Tap it again to unfold.

With the partner's add from **Partner add** on screen, `fg find --key new-items-subtitle` sits inside the same bar and the bar's height is unchanged. fg cannot take a Linux client offline (`features/live-sync.md`), so the sync states are proven by the widget test group "the bar above the list" (`app/test/list/list_screen_test.dart`: bar height, Anpassen's place and the list's first entry equal while synced, pending, offline, reconnecting, synced again, with a partner add) and by `e2e/tests/list-header.spec.ts` (held write, cut network; the first divider's y never changes; screenshots `topbar-01-synced`, `topbar-02-pending`, `topbar-03-offline`).

- **Partner move.** Start `fg record start --name lw-partner-move --client peter`; on lea run `fg marionette --client lea -- long-press --text Haferflocken`, `fg wait --key edit-sheet --client lea`, `fg tap --key chip-store --client lea`, `fg tap --key store-option-<Supermarkt id> --client lea`, `fg tap --key edit-done --client lea`; then on peter `fg marionette -- scroll-to --text Haferflocken`: its `y` lies between the Supermarkt and the Wochenmarkt divider. `fg db query "select s.name from items i join stores s on s.id = i.store_id where i.name = 'Haferflocken'" --as $R:hanna` returns Supermarkt. `fg record stop`.

## Gotchas

- `--from-text` and `--to-text` take the first widget that shows that exact text. Category dividers show capitals ("KÜHLREGAL"), and several sections can show the same one; use `--to-key category-<section>-<category id>` when two are on screen.
- A divider's hold (`fg marionette -- long-press`) or `secondary-tap` does nothing, "Ohne Laden" included: no route, the section stays as it was. Reach a store's details with `fg tap --key list-edit`, then its `store-row-<id>` (`features/stores.md`).
- The store divider's key sits on its padding: the dividers after the first are 20 px taller than the line you see. Taps at the key's centre still hit the divider.
- `fg up` starts the clients signed out, which clears the folds and the dismissed card. `fg restart` keeps them.
- `fg back` on the list screen closes the Linux app (there is no route below it). Use it only on a pushed screen; `fg doctor` then shows `peter.process: not running`, and `fg up` relaunches it.
- A category change in the edit sheet teaches the household (`category_memory`). Use the h-list-walk run, which `fg teardown` removes, not the baseline household.
- The partner tint lasts 3 s on screen; take its screenshot in the same chain as the wait.
