# Onboarding

Signed out, the app opens on the start screen (job 168b): „Los geht's“ starts the guided start,
„Schon ein Konto? Anmelden“ opens the sign-in. The guided start asks, one step each: the
usage-data question (builds with a PostHog key, once per install), "Mit wem kaufst du ein?",
the household name, the stores, "Was fehlt gerade?", and only then the account. The answers are
kept on the device until the household exists. Once signed in, the app creates the household
from them (name, stores, `households.kind`, the items) and shows the invite step: several
invites, each marked live when someone joins. A signed-in user without a household lands on
"Willkommen bei Hummlist" (job 22): "Haushalt erstellen" runs the same steps from "Mit wem"
(no usage step, no account step), "Einladung annehmen" opens the join screen. An invite link
skips all of it. A user who already has a household goes straight to the list.

## Sub-features

- `ob-order` signed out: the start screen (`/start`), the steps (`/start/<step>`), the account, the setup, the invite step, the list. Signed in without a household: the analytics consent (builds with a key, if not answered yet), then `/welcome`, or the draft's step while a guided start is under way. The sign-in (`/login`) comes first after a sign-out, an account deletion, for an invite link and for a `next` link. Routes: `/start`, `/start/usage|who|name|stores|need|account|setup|invite`, `/welcome`, `/join`, `/join/<code>`; in a household the switcher's `/households/new` and `/households/new/invite`. The only gate is `authRedirect` in `app/lib/app/router.dart`; a step's screen moves on by changing the draft, never by going to a path.
- `ob-start` (`start-screen`): `start-go` („Los geht's“) starts the draft; `start-log-in` („Schon ein Konto? Anmelden“) opens the sign-in, where `login-new` („Neu bei Hummlist? Los geht's“, not with an invite or a `next`) leads back.
- `ob-steps` each step's list is `onboarding-<step>`; `onboarding-next` („Weiter“), `onboarding-skip` („Überspringen“, on „Mit wem“ and the stores), the X `onboarding-exit` (every step but the invite step), the back arrow („Zurück“) on name, stores, need and account. Above the content „Schritt N von 7“ and a bar.
- `ob-who` chips `onboarding-kind-partner|family|flat|club|solo|other`, any number; `other` adds `onboarding-kind-other-text`; two or more show `onboarding-kinds-several` („Wir starten mit „<first>“. Die anderen merken wir uns für später.“). `onboarding-next` is off until one is picked. The first pick becomes `households.kind`; the others are kept per user on the device (`onboarding.later.<user>`) for job 169.
- `ob-name` `household-name`, prefilled from the first kind: "Unsere Familie", "Unsere WG", "Unser Verein", "Meine Liste" (solo), the „Andere“ words, else "Unser Haushalt". 1 to 80 characters.
- `ob-stores` chips `onboarding-store-<name>` for EDEKA, REWE, Lidl, Aldi Süd, Aldi Nord, Kaufland, Netto, Penny, dm, Rossmann and Baumarkt, none picked; `onboarding-store-own` opens `store-new-field` (up to 40 characters; `store-new-confirm` or Enter adds, `store-new-cancel` or Escape closes); a new name joins the chips picked (`onboarding-store-Wochenmarkt`); a typed name of an offered chain in any case or spacing ("edeka") ticks that chip instead; `stores-disclaimer` is the store-name note. `onboarding-skip` goes on without stores. The household gets them in the order picked (EDEKA, Lidl and dm become those chains).
- `ob-need` chips `onboarding-staple-<name>` (Milch, Brot, Eier, Butter, Käse, Bananen, Äpfel, Tomaten, Nudeln, Kaffee, Joghurt, Klopapier), the field `onboarding-need-field` with `onboarding-need-add` or Enter, each item a chip `onboarding-need-item-<text>` with an X. An item is kept once, whatever its case. `onboarding-next` reads "Weiter", "Weiter mit 1 Artikel" or "Weiter mit N Artikeln"; signed in it goes to the setup, signed out to the account step.
- `ob-account` (`onboarding-account`, signed out only): the sign-in panel (`login-email`, `login-password`, "Registrieren", "Anmelden", the sign-in link) under the progress and "Fast geschafft. …".
- `ob-setup` (`onboarding-setup`): creates the household with the name, stores and kind (a club starts with join approval on, job 168c), adds the items under the ids the draft gave them (the server splits "2 Gurken" and picks the category). A failure shows `onboarding-setup-problem` and `onboarding-setup-retry`. A retry or an app kill in between makes one household and adds each item once.
- `ob-invite` (`onboarding-invite`): `invite-partner` (its label by kind: "Partner·in einladen", "Familie einladen", "Mitbewohner einladen", "Mitglieder einladen", "Jemanden einladen") makes the first invite, `invite-another` ("Weitere Einladung") each next one, every one its own card (`invite-<code>`) with the code, `invite-copy-link` (only with an `APP_URL`), `invite-copy-code` and `invite-share` where a share sheet exists; no `invite-revoke` here. iOS and Android open the share sheet at once with "Komm in unseren Hummlist-Haushalt: <link> · Code: <code>". With items, `invite-step-waiting` ("N Artikel warten schon auf der Liste."). `invite-later` ("Später", after an invite "Weiter zur Liste") ends the guided start on the list. Solo as the only kind skips this step.
- `ob-joined` on the invite step, a card whose invite someone accepts shows `invite-joined` ("<Name> ist beigetreten", or "Beigetreten" without a display name) instead of its buttons. Realtime on `invites` plus a read at subscribe, so an earlier accept shows too. For a club (join approval on, every „Verein oder Team“ household from the guided start), an accept is a join request: the card stays as it is, and the request shows on the household page (Mehr → the household) until an owner or admin lets the person in (job 168c).
- `ob-exit` `onboarding-exit` opens `onboarding-exit-dialog`: `onboarding-exit-discard` („Fortschritt verwerfen“, then `onboarding-discard-dialog` with `onboarding-discard-confirm`), signed out `onboarding-exit-sign-in` („Stattdessen anmelden“, the draft stays), signed in `onboarding-exit-sign-out` („Abmelden“), and `onboarding-exit-stay` („Weiter einrichten“).
- `ob-draft` the draft is one JSON value in `DeviceCache` (`onboarding.draft`), saved on every change, one per device (outside `forget()`). A restart resumes at the step left; signed out past the questions, at the account. A draft that cannot be read starts over. Once its household exists it belongs to that user and household; a sign-out, a deletion or another household drops it.
- `ob-existing` a sign-in to an account that has a household (the account step, or „Stattdessen anmelden“) puts the draft's items on that list and drops the draft: no create, no kind, no invite step. A join by invite with a draft under way does the same in the joined household. An account without a household continues the questions without the account step.
- `ob-own-household` (job 168c) the join-request wait's „Eigenen Haushalt anlegen“ (`join-request-go-on`) starts a draft marked as that user's own household (`own_household_for` in `onboarding.draft`): if the request is approved during the questions, they go on, and the setup makes a new household beside the joined one with the draft's name, stores, kind and items (the joined list gets nothing). The same user signing in again at the account step gets it too; anyone else signing in there gets `ob-existing`. Every draft keeps a create request id (`request_id`), so a setup run again after an app kill gets the same household.
- `ob-welcome` (`welcome`): `onboarding-create` starts the draft at "Mit wem" (`/start/who`); `onboarding-join` opens the join screen; `account-delete`; the logout icon.
- `ob-join` (`join-screen`): `invite-code`, then `invite-check` ("Weiter") shows `invite-household` with the name, then `join-confirm` ("Haushalt beitreten"). `join-cancel` goes back to "Willkommen" and drops the pending link. In a household it is the same screen (job 169b): joining adds the household beside the others and shows it; nothing is left.
- `ob-new-household` the switcher's "Neuer Haushalt" (`features/household.md`, job 169b) is not the guided start: `/households/new` is the create step (`create-household-screen`, `household-name` empty with a hint, the store chips, `create-household` or `skip-stores`), then `/households/new/invite` (`invite-partner-screen`): `invite-partner` makes one invite, `invite-later` ("Später", then "Weiter zur Liste") goes to the list.
- `ob-pending` keeps a link's code on the device (`DeviceCache` key `invite.pending`, 7 days) from the moment `/join/<code>` or `?invite=` arrives. It survives sign-in (also the apps' `com.hummlist.app://login-callback` return), an app kill, the consent and the steps. It goes after a join, Cancel, an invalid code or a sign-out.
- `ob-errors`: one "Dieser Code ist ungültig oder abgelaufen." for made-up, expired, revoked and used codes; "Zu viele Versuche. Bitte später noch einmal." after 10 failures in an hour; the offline line on the join screen, the setup and the invite step. The join field always keeps the code.
- `ob-events` (builds with a key, after a yes): `household_created` (`store_count`), `invite_sent` (`channel` share_sheet/copy_link/copy_code, `context` onboarding/settings), `invite_accepted` (`method` link/code), `onboarding_completed` (`kind` or `skipped`, `kind_count`, `item_count`, `store_count`, `invited`) when the invite step is left or, for solo, when the setup ends. A yes on the usage step sends `first_open` at the sign-in. Never the code, the link, a name or an item.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Start the app signed out: the start screen. „Los geht's“, the steps, the account, the invite step.
- Sign up or sign in with an account without a household: "Willkommen bei Hummlist", "Haushalt erstellen", the steps from "Mit wem", the invite step.
- Or open `/join/<code>` signed out: sign in by any method; the join screen for that code shows.

## Driving it with fg

Preconditions: the baseline from `features/README.md`. fg builds have no PostHog key, so there is no usage step and no consent screen.

- **A fresh user on Willkommen.** `fg logout --client lea`, `fg wait --key login-email --client lea` (after a sign-out the sign-in shows; if the client shows `start-screen` instead, `fg tap --key start-log-in --client lea` first), `fg type --key login-email --input ob-$(date +%s)@fg.test --client lea`, `fg type --key login-password --input test-password-123 --client lea`, `fg tap --text Registrieren --client lea`, `fg wait --key welcome --client lea`.
- **Create from Willkommen.** `fg tap --key onboarding-create --client lea`, `fg wait --key onboarding-who --client lea`, `fg tap --key onboarding-kind-family --client lea`, `fg tap --key onboarding-next --client lea`, `fg wait --key household-name --client lea`, `fg type --key household-name --input "WG $(date +%s)" --client lea`, `fg tap --key onboarding-next --client lea`, `fg wait --key onboarding-stores --client lea`, `fg tap --key onboarding-store-REWE --client lea`, `fg tap --key onboarding-store-dm --client lea`, `fg tap --key onboarding-next --client lea`, `fg wait --key onboarding-need --client lea`, `fg tap --key onboarding-staple-Milch --client lea`, `fg type --key onboarding-need-field --input "2 Gurken" --submit --client lea`, `fg wait --key "onboarding-need-item-2 Gurken" --client lea`, `fg tap --key onboarding-next --client lea`, `fg wait --key invite-partner --client lea`. Then `fg db query "select h.kind, s.name, s.seed_key from households h join stores s on s.household_id = h.id where h.name = '<name>' order by s.sort_order"` returns family with REWE (null) and dm (dm), and `fg db query "select i.name, i.quantity from items i join households h on h.id = i.household_id where h.name = '<name>' order by i.name"` returns Gurken (2) and Milch.
- **Create without anything.** As `journeys/account-deletion.sh` does: `onboarding-create`, wait `onboarding-who`, `onboarding-skip`, wait `household-name`, type the name, `onboarding-next`, wait `onboarding-stores`, `onboarding-skip`, wait `onboarding-need`, `onboarding-next`, wait `invite-later`. The stores query returns nothing, `kind` is null, categories 11.
- **Signed-out guided start.** On a signed-out client showing `start-screen` (after `fg logout`, `fg tap --key login-new` gets there from the sign-in): `fg tap --key start-go`, then the steps as above from `onboarding-who`; on `onboarding-account` type a fresh email into `login-email` and the password into `login-password`, `fg tap --text Registrieren`, `fg wait --key invite-partner`. The household and items queries above prove the setup.
- **Resume after a kill.** On `onboarding-stores` with a chip picked, `fg restart --client lea`. The app opens on `onboarding-stores` with the chip still picked.
- **Invite (Linux: no share sheet).** On the invite step, `fg tap --key invite-partner --client lea`, `fg wait --key invite-copy-code --client lea`. Read the code as in `features/household.md`. `fg tap --key invite-another --client lea` adds a second card. `fg tap --key invite-copy-code --client lea` (the first one) shows "Code kopiert". `fg tap --key invite-later --client lea`, `fg wait --key add-item-field --client lea`.
- **Joined, live.** With lea on the invite step and one invite made, a second fresh user joins with its code: on peter's client `fg logout --client peter`, a fresh sign-up there, `fg tap --key onboarding-join --client peter`, type the code into `invite-code`, `fg tap --key invite-check`, `fg tap --key join-confirm`. On lea's client, without touching it: `fg wait --key invite-joined --client lea` and `fg wait --contains "ist beigetreten" --client lea`. Afterwards `fg login peter --client peter`.
- **Existing account.** On a signed-out client in the guided start, add an item on `onboarding-need` (for example `Xylophon-OB`), go on to `onboarding-account` and sign in there as peter (`login-email` `peter@fg.test`, `login-password` `test-password-123`, `fg press enter`). `fg wait --key add-item-field`; `fg db query "select count(*) from items where name = 'Xylophon-OB'" --as peter` returns 1, and no new household exists.
- **Second client joins by code, then live.** Use `fg sync` (two clients, `docs/TEST_FIXTURES.md`) or the **Joined, live** sign-up on peter's client: `fg tap --key onboarding-join`, type the code into `invite-code`, `fg tap --key invite-check`, `fg wait --text "<household name>"`, `fg tap --key join-confirm`, `fg wait --key add-item-field`. Then add an item on one client and `fg wait --text <item>` on the other (as in `features/live-sync.md`).
- **Proof.** Screenshots of the start screen, each step, the invite step with two cards and the joined mark, the join preview and the list on both clients; the `households`, `stores`, `items` and `household_members` query output.

## Gotchas

- fg builds have no PostHog key, so the usage step and the consent screen never come and nothing is sent.
- `fg logout` counts the start screen as signed out (`alreadySignedOut`), and `fg login` taps `start-log-in` first when the client shows it. A recipe that types into `login-email` itself after a fresh start needs that tap too.
- The draft lives in the client's `DeviceCache`, per client, not per user. A guided start left half done resumes on that client after the next sign-out or start; discard it with `onboarding-exit`, `onboarding-exit-discard`, `onboarding-discard-confirm`.
- Signing in as peter or lea with a draft under way puts its items on "Familie Test". Mark them bought afterwards.
- The fg debug build has no `APP_URL`: the invite card shows the code without a link, and the share text names the code only.
- Invites are single use and expire after 7 days; seed a fresh one per join (`fg seed pending-invite` prints an open code).
- After 10 wrong codes in an hour, the user is rate-limited for an hour: `fg db query "delete from invite_attempts" --write` resets it.
- The Mac runner has no Docker, so no local stack and no fg there. Run fg on the box, or prove the web flows with `scripts/ci-e2e.sh --spec tests/guided-start.spec.ts` (or `tests/onboarding.spec.ts`).
