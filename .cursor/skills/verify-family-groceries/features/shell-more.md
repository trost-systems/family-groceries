# Shell and More

The signed-in app has two tabs: "Liste" (the shopping list with the add bar) and "Mehr". On phones they sit in a bar at the bottom, which slides away while the add sheet is open; from 840 px wide they become a rail on the left. Since job 85 (design "M-P2") More is a list of inset groups: each row is a hex icon and its name, with no second line. Top to bottom: [Konto, Hummlist Premium (only while it may be offered or the household has it)], [Partner einladen, Anderem Haushalt beitreten, Archivierte Haushalte], [Zugänge], [Einstellungen, Nutzungsdaten (only in builds with a PostHog key)], [Feedback senden, Hilfe & Kontakt], the one group label "Rechtliches" above [Impressum, Datenschutzerklärung, Nutzungsbedingungen (EULA), Lizenzen], [Abmelden, red, alone], then the app version. The legal rows sit on More itself, so the imprint is two taps from the list (Mehr, Impressum; DDG §5). Nutzungsdaten (`/more/usage-data`) is a page of its own; the former Rechtliches page is gone and `/more/legal` lands on More. Categories and stores moved to the list header's "Anpassen" in job 78 (`features/stores.md`); theme and language are on Einstellungen (`features/settings.md`).

## Sub-features

- `sm-tabs` switches between `tab-list` ("Liste") and `tab-more` ("Mehr"). Each tab reopens the page it last showed; a tap on the current tab goes back to its first page. Leaving the list tab closes the add sheet.
- `sm-household` opens "Partner einladen" (`more-invite`, shown with a household only) on the Einladungen page (`invites-page`, route `/more/invites`: "Neue Einladung" `invite-new` and the open invites, `features/household.md`), "Anderem Haushalt beitreten" (`more-join`) on the join screen (`join-screen`, route `/join`, outside the tabs, pushed: back and "Abbrechen" return to More, job 78).
- `sm-stores` (job 78: in the List tab) the list header's "Anpassen" (`list-edit`) opens `stores-screen` inside the List tab, so the tabs stay and back returns to the list. A row `store-row-<id>` opens the store's details; a drag on "<Store> verschieben" reorders the stores; each row's trash deletes it, "Laden hinzufügen" adds one, and "Ohne Laden" (`store-row-none`) opens the route order (`features/stores.md`, `features/categories.md`).
- `sm-update` an update card (`more-update-card`, lemon, "Neu starten" / "Aktualisieren" and a close button) can sit on top of the list, but no build has an update check yet (`NoAppUpdates`), so it never shows (job 85 stub; widget tests drive it with a fake).
- `sm-lifetime` "Hummlist Premium" (`more-lifetime`, route `/more/lifetime`, below Konto) opens the Premium page: `features/purchases.md`. It is listed while the household could be offered Premium or has it; the row has no second line (job 85: no "Freigeschaltet").
- `sm-archived` "Archivierte Haushalte" (`more-archived`, after "Anderem Haushalt beitreten") shows only in builds with `HOUSEHOLD_ARCHIVE` (the e2e config); fg builds don't have it.
- `sm-access` opens the Zugänge page (`more-access` → `access-screen`, route `/more/access`: MCP-Server, Zugriffstoken, Verbundene Apps; `features/mcp-access.md`).
- `sm-account` opens the Konto page (`menu-account` → `account-screen`, route `/more/account`: the signed-in email, export, deletion; `features/account.md`). Zugänge and Konto are two pages (job 29d); each has a back arrow to More.
- `sm-old-links` the routes of the one page they were before keep working: `/settings` redirects to `/more/access`, `/settings?section=account` to `/more/account`.
- `sm-sign-out` signs out (`more-sign-out`, "Abmelden" in red, its own group below the legal rows, no chevron) and shows the login screen (`features/auth.md`).
- `sm-feedback` opens the feedback form (`more-feedback`, "Feedback senden", first in the group above Abmelden): `features/feedback.md`.
- `sm-help` "Hilfe & Kontakt" (`more-help`, below Feedback senden, an external-link icon instead of the chevron) opens the help page on hummlist.com (`/support`, English `/en/support`) in the system browser, in every build since Peter approved the page on 2026-10-30: `features/legal.md`.
- `sm-settings` opens Einstellungen (`more-settings` → `settings-screen`, route `/more/settings`): the theme and the language list (job 29e; `features/settings.md`). The language is no longer on the More list itself.
- `sm-usage-data` "Nutzungsdaten" (`more-usage-data`, below Einstellungen; only in builds with a PostHog key) opens the Nutzungsdaten page (`more-usage-data-page`, route `/more/usage-data`, app bar "Nutzungsdaten"): only the switch (`sm-analytics`), no policy link. Back returns to More. The consent text's path "Mehr → Nutzungsdaten" names exactly this row.
- `sm-legal` the label "Rechtliches" (English "Legal", a heading) above its own group of direct rows, below Hilfe & Kontakt and above Abmelden: "Impressum" (`more-imprint`, external: https://hummlist.com/impressum, English "Imprint", /en/imprint), "Datenschutzerklärung" (`more-privacy-policy`, external: https://hummlist.com/datenschutz, English "Privacy policy", /en/privacy), "Nutzungsbedingungen (EULA)" (`more-terms`, English "Terms of Use (EULA)", external: Apple's standard EULA on iOS, the support page /support or /en/support on Android, web and desktop as a placeholder) and "Lizenzen" (`sm-licenses`). The external rows carry an external-link icon and leave the app on More. `/more/legal` (the former Rechtliches page) redirects to `/more`.
- `sm-imprint` the imprint is two taps from the list: `tab-more`, then `more-imprint` (scrolled into view on a phone).
- `sm-licenses` "Lizenzen" (`more-licenses`, last legal row, a chevron) opens Flutter's license page for "Hummlist" with the version; back returns to More.
- `sm-version` shows "Version <version> (<build>)" at the bottom (`more-version`). A long press marks the device internal for analytics (`features/analytics.md`, `an-internal`); then "Interne Installation" (`more-internal`) and "Testfehler senden" (`more-test-error`) show below it.
- `sm-analytics` the switch "Nutzungsdaten und Fehlerberichte senden" (`more-analytics`) on the Nutzungsdaten page, only in builds with a PostHog key (fg builds only with `FG_POSTHOG_HOST`; without one More has no Nutzungsdaten row): `features/analytics.md`, `an-switch`.
- `sm-wide` from 840 px: a NavigationRail instead of the bar, the add sheet as wide as the list column, and More in two panes (the list on the left, Zugänge or the picked page on the right, without a back arrow).

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Tap "Mehr" in the tab bar (or the rail on a wide screen). Tap "Liste" to go back.
- Scroll down the More list for Einstellungen, Nutzungsdaten, Feedback senden, Hilfe & Kontakt, the Rechtliches rows (Impressum, Datenschutzerklärung, Nutzungsbedingungen (EULA), Lizenzen) and Abmelden.
- Nutzungsdaten → the usage-data switch. Impressum, Datenschutzerklärung and Nutzungsbedingungen open hummlist.com (or Apple's EULA on iOS) in the browser.

## Driving it with fg

Preconditions:

- Baseline from `features/README.md` with the chain stores dm, Lidl and Edeka, added as in `features/README.md` (Baseline); peter signed in on the list.
- `H="(select id from households where name = 'Familie Test')"`; the store ids from `fg db query "select id, name from stores where household_id = $H order by sort_order" --as peter`.

- **`sm-tabs`.** Run `fg tap --key tab-more` and `fg wait --key more-screen` (if it times out, More reopened its last page: tap `tab-more` again); `fg screenshot --name sm-more`. `fg find --key add-item-field` counts 0. `fg tap --key tab-list` and `fg wait --key add-item-field` return to the list. Leaving closes the sheet: `fg tap --key add-item-field`, `fg wait --key add-sheet`, `fg press escape`; with the sheet open the tabs are out of reach (`fg tap --key tab-more` fails with `not_found`, and `fg find --key add-sheet-hint` shows the hint in their place), so close it first.
- **`sm-household`.** On More, `fg marionette -- scroll-to --key more-invite` (the list keeps its scroll position), `fg tap --key more-invite`, `fg wait --key invites-page`; the page shows no code until `fg tap --key invite-new` (`features/household.md`, Invite). `fg back` returns to More. `fg tap --key more-join`, `fg wait --key join-screen` (`/more/join`, inside the More tab since job 78), then `fg back` and `fg wait --key more-screen`: back returns to More. `fg find --key more-categories` and `fg find --key more-stores` count 0 (job 78: Liste → Anpassen).
- **`sm-stores`.** `fg tap --key tab-list`, `fg tap --key list-edit`, `fg wait --key stores-screen`. `fg find --contains verschieben` lists "dm verschieben", "Lidl verschieben", "Edeka verschieben". `fg tap --key store-row-<dm id>` and `fg wait --key store-details`; `fg back` twice returns to the list with the List tab selected. Reordering: `features/stores.md` (Stores screen).
- **`sm-access`.** `fg tap --key more-access`, `fg wait --text "Verbundene Apps"`, `fg screenshot --name sm-access`. Run `fg tap --key tab-list`, then `fg tap --key tab-more`: Zugänge shows again (`fg wait --text "Verbundene Apps"`), the tab kept its page. A second `fg tap --key tab-more` goes to the More list (`fg wait --key more-screen`). `fg tap --key more-access` again, then `fg back` also returns to the More list; `fg find --key account-delete` on Zugänge counts 0.
- **`sm-account`.** `fg marionette -- scroll-to --key menu-account`, `fg tap --key menu-account`, `fg wait --key account-screen`; `fg find --key account-email` shows `peter@fg.test`, `fg find --key account-export` and `fg find --key account-delete` count 1, `fg find --text MCP-Server` counts 0. `fg screenshot --name sm-account`. `fg back` (or `fg tap --key tab-more`) returns to the list of entries.
- **`sm-old-links`.** Not drivable with fg (no URL bar on the desktop client); the widget tests in `app/test/more/more_screen_test.dart` ("the link /settings… opens …") and the web e2e test "old /settings links open the right page; back from a deep link goes to More" (`e2e/tests/shell-more.spec.ts`) cover it. Report it as skipped.
- **`sm-usage-data`.** In an fg build without `FG_POSTHOG_HOST`, `fg find --key more-usage-data` counts 0 (no row); report the page as skipped or rebuild with `FG_POSTHOG_HOST` (`features/analytics.md`). With it: `fg marionette -- scroll-to --key more-usage-data`, `fg tap --key more-usage-data`, `fg wait --key more-usage-data-page`; `fg find --key more-analytics` counts 1 and `fg find --key more-privacy-policy` 0. `fg screenshot --name sm-usage-data`. `fg back`, `fg wait --key more-screen`.
- **`sm-legal`, `sm-imprint`.** From the list: `fg tap --key tab-more`, `fg marionette -- scroll-to --key more-imprint`, `fg screenshot --name sm-legal` (the label "Rechtliches" and its four rows above `more-sign-out`), `fg tap --key more-imprint`. The app hands the URL to the desktop: on the build box `pgrep -af hummlist.com/impressum` shows `google-chrome-stable https://hummlist.com/impressum`. Stop that browser with `kill <pid>` from that line; the app stays on More (`fg find --key more-screen`). `more-privacy-policy` opens https://hummlist.com/datenschutz and `more-terms` https://hummlist.com/support the same way (Linux is not iOS, so the placeholder). On a host without a browser (the Hetzner runner) see Gotchas for reading the URL. With the language on English (`features/settings.md`) the imprint, privacy policy and help entries open `/en/imprint`, `/en/privacy` and `/en/support`.
- **`sm-licenses`, `sm-version`.** On More, `fg marionette -- scroll-to --key more-licenses`, `fg tap --key more-licenses`, `fg wait --text Hummlist`, and `fg find --contains 0.1.0` returns "0.1.0 (1)"; `fg screenshot --name sm-licenses`. `fg back` returns to More (`fg wait --key more-screen`). `fg marionette -- scroll-to --key more-version`, then `fg find --key more-version` shows "Version 0.1.0 (1)" (the checkout's `pubspec.yaml` version).
- **`sm-settings`.** `fg marionette -- scroll-to --key more-settings`, `fg tap --key more-settings`, `fg wait --key settings-screen`; `fg back` returns to More (`fg wait --key more-screen`). The theme and language steps are in `features/settings.md`.
- **`sm-sign-out`.** `fg tap --key tab-more`, `fg marionette -- scroll-to --key more-sign-out`, `fg tap --key more-sign-out`, `fg wait --key login-email`. `fg logout` does the same. `fg login peter` signs in again.
- **`sm-wide`.** Not drivable on linux: the client's Xvfb screen is fixed at 412×892, below the 840 px breakpoint. Report it as skipped; the web e2e test "wide screens: a rail, a sheet as wide as the list column, More in two panes" (`e2e/tests/shell-more.spec.ts`) covers it.
- **Proof.** Screenshots `sm-more`, `sm-access`, `sm-account`, `sm-usage-data` (with a PostHog key), `sm-legal`, `sm-licenses`; the theme and language proofs are in `features/settings.md`.

## Gotchas

- More is a lazy ListView. On a 412×892 client, the rows from `more-settings` down (`more-usage-data`, `more-feedback`, `more-help`, `more-imprint`, `more-privacy-policy`, `more-terms`, `more-licenses`, `more-sign-out`) may not be built until you scroll: `fg tap --key more-imprint` fails with `not_found` before `fg marionette -- scroll-to --key more-imprint`.
- `fg back` on the More list itself closes the Linux app ("Back button pressed, no route to pop (app may exit)"; `fg doctor` then shows `peter.process: not running`, `fg up` relaunches it). Since job 29d Zugänge and Konto are routes below `/more`, so `fg back` (and Android's back button) there returns to the More list; so do Einladungen, Einstellungen and Nutzungsdaten. `fg back` is also safe on the licenses, on Liste → Anpassen (the stores screen and a store's details) and in dialogs, and since job 78 on the join screen from `more-join` (`/more/join`, a page of the More tab).
- After a language switch (`features/settings.md`) every text match changes language: `fg logout` and `fg login` use keys and work in both, but recipes that match German text need `language-system` (or `language-de`) back.
- The open add sheet hides the tabs: tap `add-sheet-close` or `fg press escape` before `fg tap --key tab-more`. `fg logout` does that for you.
- `more-invite` shows only while the user is in a household.
- On a host without a browser the tap opens nothing visible. To read the URL, give the client a URL handler in its own XDG directories (a relaunch by `fg up` empties them, so add it again after one): make an executable script that appends its arguments to `/tmp/opened-urls.log`, point a desktop entry at it in `~/.cache/fg/<prefix>/clients/<client>/data/applications/url-log.desktop` (`Type=Application`, `Exec=<script> %u`, `MimeType=x-scheme-handler/http;x-scheme-handler/https;`), set it as the default in `~/.cache/fg/<prefix>/clients/<client>/config/mimeapps.list` (`[Default Applications]`, `x-scheme-handler/https=url-log.desktop`), then tap and read `/tmp/opened-urls.log`.
- The More list keeps its scroll position while the app runs. Scroll to an entry at the top (`more-invite`) as well as to one at the bottom.
- When `fg up` rebuilds the app for another client, the bundle on disk changes under a client that keeps running: icons render as boxes and `more-version` is empty. `fg restart` that client (or `fg up`, which relaunches stale ones).
