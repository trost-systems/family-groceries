## Peter's round-2 feedback (the spec for round 3)
- E (e-satz-zeilen) is his favourite layout overall. Build ONE new variant `h-satz-final` based on E.
- When a store is collapsed, do NOT show the item icons/emoji strip in the store heading. The "X Artikel" count is enough.
- Categories must NOT be sticky, and stores must NOT be sticky either. Both are plain inline dividers in the scrolling flow, in the style of E's category divider (store divider can be a bit stronger than the category one, but no sticky/pinned headers). No category tags as in F.
- MOST IMPORTANT: items are listed in the order they are placed in the store; you shop by walking through it sequentially. Categories are mainly guides for organizing and adding, so keep them visually light (thin, quiet dividers; they must not dominate the walk-through order).
- EDIT uses SEPARATE FIELDS: Bezeichnung (name), Marke (brand), Eigenschaften (details), Menge (quantity), plus the store and category fold-out chips. You usually change only one or a few things, so each field is directly editable, prefilled; no retyping the whole item. Save must never silently apply a guessed suggestion. Delete stays available.
- ADD stays as it is: type, then tap a suggestion; every suggestion is tappable; Enter takes the first; fold-out store/category chips apply to the tapped suggestion.
- SHEET SIZE: the sheet takes only as much space as it needs. It opens at about 50% of the screen height, grows as more suggestions come in (smoothly, no jumps of the input), and scrolls its suggestion list only once it reaches full height. The input stays sticky at the bottom (same on-screen position as the closed bar), the sheet still grows upwards around it and hides the tabs. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. The edit sheet likewise sizes to its content (no dead area).
- He liked the temporary explainer card at the top of the list (the gesture hint). Keep a simple version of it in H. Onboarding (that card vs. overlays with spotlights) comes LATER: do not build an onboarding system now.
- KEEP from round 2 everything he didn't object to: collapsible stores (tap the store divider; count when collapsed; sessionStorage persistence) and reorderable stores (drag), drag and drop of items between categories and stores (also onto a collapsed store), category reorder, full-width rows with small leading emoji icons, no check circles, long names wrap in full, one tap checks off with Undo, long-press or right-click opens edit, DE text by default with a complete EN toggle, List/More tabs, mock data only, PhoneFrame 390x844 on desktop.
