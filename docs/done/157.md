## Behaviour

One state machine per phone (`next()` in `v2.js`). Its phases are hidden, open (expanded), rest (collapsed) and busy (retrying).

- Offline or failing opens the indicator. S3 and S2 with C1 or C3 collapse after 3 s. S2 with C2 or C3 collapses on a scroll or a swipe up on the card. S1 doesn't collapse.
- Offline: a tap on the collapsed form opens it, and a tap on the open card closes it. There is no retry.
- Failing: a tap on either form retries. The state icon turns into spinning sync arrows for 1.2 s. On success the indicator leaves, and the polite live region says "Synchronisiert". If the retry fails ("Retry fails" toggle), it settles collapsed with a short nudge.
- Online shows nothing. There is no "back online" flash and no confirmation snackbar.
- Motion uses the app's tokens (180/260/360 ms, the standard curve in, the exit curve out, bounce only on the small collapsed badge). Reduced motion turns every change into a 200 ms crossfade: S3's compact and expanded layers carry their own fill then, so nothing grows.

## Recommendation

1. **S2-C3F3.** The full sentence once, then the state moves into the top bar where today's cloud sits. No new object and no list shift. It is the same on every platform, and the bar is already tall enough for a 44 pt target.
2. **S3, if Peter wants the island feel.** Use a1 on island iPhones and d1 on Pixel-style centred holes, and the hex capsule (x3) everywhere else. The port needs a switch on the cutout (`MediaQuery.displayFeatures` on Android; on iOS only the top inset tells island from notch).
3. **S1-x1 (navy/yellow) as the quick win.** It is AA in both themes, with light icons on navy and dark icons on yellow. It costs a row while offline.
