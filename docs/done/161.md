# Job 161: the sync indicator S2-C3F2 in the app

PR: https://github.com/trost-systems/family-groceries/pull/311

## What was built

- `app/lib/list/sync_indicator.dart`: the indicator. A pure reducer (`reduceSync`) moves between four phases: hidden, open, collapsed and busy. Offline or failing opens a floating hex card under the list's top bar. After 3 s, or at once on a list scroll or a swipe up on the card, it collapses into a hex pill at the bar's top left. Tapping the pill opens the card again. Offline, tapping the open card collapses it. Failing, a tap retries. Success hides the indicator and announces "Synchronisiert". A failed retry leaves the pill, nudges it and announces "Immer noch gestört". Online shows nothing, even while changes are pending. Reduced motion is a 200 ms crossfade.
- `list_repository.dart`: `ServerTrouble` separates "the server answered with an error worth retrying" (5xx, PGRST0xx/3xx, transient SQLSTATE classes, function 5xx) from "no answer" (offline). Writes that fail this way no longer turn into a refusal that drops the change.
- `list_cubit.dart`: `ListState.failing`, `trouble` (offline wins), `_goFailing` and `retry()`. `_flush` returns the run in flight.
- `list_screen.dart`: the cloud icon is gone. The `list-status` capsule holds only the partner's new adds. Anpassen is unchanged.
- e2e: `offlineIndicator` / `failingIndicator` helpers (a role button: on web the card's text is its name). `list-header.spec.ts` covers pending (shows nothing), card → pill, a tap re-expands, the list and Anpassen never move, and failing through a 503 with a retry.
- Feature map: verify-hummlist `offline.md`, `live-sync.md`, FEATURES-FOR-WRITERS "Working offline", and verify-family-groceries `live-sync.md`, `list-walk-order.md`, `household.md` and SKILL.md.
