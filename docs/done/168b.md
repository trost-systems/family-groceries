# Job 168b: guided start (O2) and trigger hints (H2)

Peter's picks from job 168's plan (2026-11-08): O2 with his changes and H2 with his texts, in the
real app, German and English. Plan and decisions: `docs/onboarding/PLAN.md` („Phase 2: what was
built“), `docs/DECISIONS.md` („Job 168b: guided start (O2)“, „Job 168b: hints“).

## What was built

- **Welcome screen** (`/start`): hex mark, „Hummlist“, tagline, welcome line, „Los geht's“,
  „Schon ein Konto? Anmelden“. Signed out, `/` opens it; the sign-in got „Neu bei Hummlist? Los
  geht's“ back.
- **Questions before the account**: usage data (only if unanswered), „Mit wem kaufst du ein?“
  (multi-select, „Andere“ with a field), name, stores, „Was fehlt gerade?“ (12 staples and a
  field), then the account (every sign-in method), then the setup and the invite step.
  „Schritt N von 7“ and an X (log in instead, discard after a confirmation) on every step.
- **Kept on the device** (`OnboardingDraft` in `DeviceCache`, `onboarding.draft`) across reloads
  and app kills, resumed at its step; at the sign-up an idempotent setup makes the household
  (`create_household`, `households.kind`, the items through `put_item` with the draft's ids).
  An existing account gets the items on its list.
- **Invite step**: several invites, each marked „<Name> ist beigetreten“ as soon as it is
  accepted (realtime on `invites`).
- **Multiple households**: job 169b has not landed; the first „Mit wem“ answer becomes the
  household, the others are kept per user (`onboarding.later.<user>`).
- **Hints (H2)**: `app/lib/tips/` registry, `UsageLog`, pure `nextHint`, flat card and callout,
  „Alle Tipps unter Mehr → Tipps.“ for 3 s after X, More → Tipps, the „Erste Schritte“ checklist.
  `own-household` and `second-household` wait for `HintHost.addHousehold` (169b), `siri` for the
  native-siri hold.
- **Server**: migration `20261108204546_household_kind_invite_realtime.sql`, pgTAP
  `household_kind.test.sql`.
- **Analytics**: `onboarding_completed`, `hint_*`, `checklist_*` (consented installs only);
  `first_open` after a yes before sign-in goes at the sign-in.
- **Tooling and docs**: feature map (onboarding, sign-in, privacy-consent, household-invites,
  app-links, tips, FEATURES-FOR-WRITERS), fg recipes and `fg login` through the start screen,
  `journeys/onboarding.mjs`, e2e helpers and `e2e/tests/guided-start.spec.ts`.
