# Job 32a: spelling variants and synonyms in the classifier (Spec #8 Part A)

PR: https://github.com/trost-systems/family-groceries/pull/115

In a beta build "Möhren" went to Obst & Gemüse but "Karrotten" to Sonstiges. Now misspellings,
regional words and English words land in the category of the word they mean, on every add path
(app, `add_item`/`add_items` for MCP, Siri and voice, and the coming Alexa skill), because all of
them go through the one database classifier. The text stays exactly as typed. No paid API, no
LLM, no UI change (the duplicate hint is job 32b). Decisions: [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md),
"Job 32a".

## What changed for users

- "Karrotten", "Karotten", "Karotte", "Möhre", "Möhren", "Mohrrüben", "Gelbe Rüben" and "carrots"
  all land in Obst & Gemüse; "Paradeiser", "Karfiol", "Semmeln", "Topfen", "Schlagobers",
  "toilet paper" and about 150 more regional and English words with their German word; typos
  like "Schokolde", "Brokoli", "Zuchini", "Mlich", "Kartofeln", "Hänchenbrust", "Erdnusbutter",
  "Feuchttüscher" with the word they mean.
- Moving one spelling to another category moves its variants with it, in that household only:
  after "Karrotten" goes to Getränke, "Möhren" and "Karotten" go there too.
- A store remembered for "Mohrrüben" is guessed for "Karotten".
- Short words are never guessed (Reis/Eis, Brot/Rot, Milch/Mehl stay apart); a word the
  classifier is not sure about stays in Sonstiges rather than landing somewhere wrong.

## For job 32b

Match on **`items.canonical_key`** (hidden; set on every insert and rename, and backfilled for
existing rows by the migrations). `public.canonical_key(name)` gives the key for a typed
name; "Karotten", "Karrotten", "Möhren", "Mohrrüben", "Gelbe Rüben" and "carrots" all give `mor`.
The analytics events `dedupe_hint_shown`, `dedupe_hint_merged` and `dedupe_hint_ignored` are in
`eventCatalog` with optional `actor_role` and `household_size`.

## How to verify

- `supabase test db` (CI's db gate): `classifier_variants.test.sql` plus the whole suite.
- `supabase/classifier/wordlist.sh` and `latency.sh` on a running stack (the db gate runs them).
- `cd supabase/classifier && deno test --allow-read`; `cd app && flutter test`.
- e2e: `scripts/ci-e2e.sh --spec "tests/classifier.spec.ts"`.

## Results

See the PR for CI's numbers on the merged head. Local run (Postgres 17 without Docker, the same
migrations): word list words 210/210, variants 47/47, misspellings 130/138 (94.2%, target ≥ 90%),
false matches 2/421 (0.48%, target < 1%; both from the job-2 dictionary's compound rule:
Fahrradschlauch, Nagel); latency p50 0.13 ms vs 0.57 ms before, p95 0.21 ms vs 0.71 ms. All 1,202
dictionary terms, all brand products and the 345-item sweep classify as before.

## Missing or blocked

Nothing. Out of scope by the spec: the duplicate hint (32b), paid APIs or LLMs, auto-correcting
