# Job 136: bottom nav prototypes (comparison preview)

PR: https://github.com/trost-systems/family-groceries/pull/277 (draft, NOT FOR MERGE)

## What was built

A static comparison page at `/internal/bottom-nav` in the hummlist-site project.
The files are `site/public/internal/bottom-nav.html`, `site/public/internal/bottom-nav/nav.css` and `nav.js`.
The page shows five phones (390 × 844 pt, 34 pt home-indicator inset) with the same list, a floating add field and the nav:

| Frame | Nav | Nav + add field | Rows visible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current (Material NavigationBar 62 + 34) | 96 pt | 160 pt | 7 |
| V1 Compact (edge to edge, 40 pt, icon and label inline, 20 pt bottom pad) | 60 pt | 124 pt | 8 |
| V2 Floating pill (raised hex capsule, 52 pt, 24 pt above the bottom edge) | 76 pt | 140 pt | 7 |
| V3 Island (dark capsule, 44 pt, icons only, the active tab shows its label) | 68 pt | 132 pt | 7 |
| Extra: one row (no tab bar, "More" is a hex button beside the add field) | 0 pt | 76 pt | 9 |

The page measures the numbers from the DOM.
The toggles switch DE/EN, light/dark, the add field floating (job 135) or docked (today), and fit or 1:1 size.
Tapping a tab switches that phone to a mock More page.
The colours come from the 6b tokens (`/tokens.css` values) and the icons are the shell's Material glyphs.
