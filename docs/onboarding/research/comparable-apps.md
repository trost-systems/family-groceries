# Onboarding of comparable apps (desk research, 2026-11-07)

Method: web search plus page fetches only. No app was installed or driven. Public onboarding detail for grocery apps is thin. Most "teardowns" are short third-party recordings or student/consultancy exercises. Anything not backed by a URL is marked **unverified**.

## Bring!
- **Steps before value:** Account creation is mandatory at the very start; the teardown flags it as a possible drop-off for users who want to try first. Source: https://screensdesign.com/showcase/bring-grocery-shopping-list . Sign-in options: Google or email (secondary source, https://screensdesign.com/apps/bring-grocery-shopping-list/ via search snippet). Questions asked (name, household size, diet): none described in the teardown (unverified that none exist).
- **First list:** Flow guides to create the first list "in under 30 seconds"; then "You probably need" suggestions let users fill the list with staples in a few taps. Task-oriented, not a feature tour. Source: https://screensdesign.com/showcase/bring-grocery-shopping-list
- **Invite:** The app encourages inviting others to a list during onboarding (about 28 s into the recording), presented as the core growth mechanic. Same source. Invites go by email address (developer comment, https://www.iphone-ticker.de/einkaufsliste-bring-jetzt-mit-push-mitteilungen-52574/ , via search snippet). Exact invite copy and link mechanics: unverified (no official help page found).
- **Permissions:** None described in the teardown. Push notifications exist as a feature (iphone-ticker link above); timing unverified.
- **Paywall:** None shown in onboarding ("No Paywall" in app info). Source: screensdesign link above. Monetisation is ads / brand partnerships: https://getbring.com/magazin/shopping-app-bring-continues-its-course-for-growth-in-2021 (company says sales up ~70 % in 2020; partner page claims 3.6 M active users DACH, unverified).
- **Discovery after onboarding:** templates and recipes were added over time (https://www.mobiflip.de/bring-einkaufsliste-bekommt-vorlagen-und-rezepte/); no tip system found.
- **Numbers:** 20 M+ users worldwide, 12 M downloads in early 2021 (company, https://getbring.com/en/about-us and the 2021 post above). 2018: 1.5 M downloads, over 0.5 M MAU (https://tech.eu/2018/02/28/bring-funding/). No published share of users who invite, or sharing-driven growth figure: not found. "Sharing drives growth" is the teardown's inference, not a company metric.

## AnyList
- **Steps before value:** Account prompt right after download ("free and only takes a few moments"); then a pre-made list "My Grocery List". Source: https://help.anylist.com/articles/getting-started . No guest mode found (unverified).
- **Invite:** Not in onboarding. Step 4 of the getting-started guide: open list, scroll to bottom, Share List, enter email. Existing account: shared at once. New person: email with app link, and the list is shared once they register with that same email. Source: https://help.anylist.com/articles/share-list/ (via search snippet) and getting-started page. Household subscription member must already have an account, added on iOS/Android only: https://help.anylist.com/articles/add-household-user
- **First list:** Non-blank: one default list, items auto-sorted by category.
- **Tips:** Text guide only: autocomplete, hide finished items, favorites and recent items, paste a list from a message. https://help.anylist.com/articles/getting-started
- **Paywall:** Core list and sharing free. AnyList Complete US$9.99/year individual, US$14.99/year household; nothing found about a paywall in first run (same page).
- **Permissions, metrics:** not found.

## OurGroceries
- **Steps before value:** No account needed to start; without an email, lists are stored anonymously and cannot be recovered on reinstall or new phone. Sharing needs the same email on each device. Source: https://ourgroceries.com/user-guide
- **Invite:** Not in onboarding. Share by using one household email address ("Choose a single email address for the household"); invite from Settings, or sign in with the shared email and password. Same source. The App Store text says "enter the same email address into each copy" (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id325851015). A reviewer says the app emails the account owner asking permission (secondary, unverified).
- **First list:** Opens to a list of lists; guide suggests one list per store. User guide above.
- **Paywall:** Ads in free tier; upgrade removes ads for everyone you share with (same guide). No onboarding paywall found.
- **Permissions, metrics:** not found.

## Todoist
- **Steps before value:** Account required. After the 2021 redesign: sign up, "Almost there", pick a theme, welcome step that avoids a blank page, dashboard. Two of three post-signup steps were removed. Source: https://speakerdeck.com/fmerian/how-todoist-redesigned-its-user-onboarding (the deck reports no numbers).
- **Invite:** Not found as part of the first run (unverified). Team sharing is project-level.
- **First list:** Todoist offers an official "Todoist onboarding" template project to learn the basics: https://www.todoist.com/templates/todoist-onboarding . Whether it is auto-created for new users: unverified.
- **Activation metric:** No Todoist-published number found. A GrowthX consultancy project proposes "task creation within the first 5 minutes of signup" (a proposal, not Todoist data): https://growthx.club/proof-of-work/consultancy/todoist/onboarding-project-----todoist|67436d5e3bc9d41985ac7ef5 . The Notion teardown (https://guptapiyush.notion.site/Todoist-Onboarding-Teardown-66020a27e8564fee89f0bfca18064534) would not load; content unverified.
- **Permissions, paywall:** not verified.

## Splitwise
- **Steps (inferred, not observed):** sign up, in-app features walkthrough, contact sync, first expense; contacts is the only named permission. Source: https://growthx.club/proof-of-work/fintech/splitwise/splitwise---onboarding-analysis%7C65b49eb02d5b877e2b4f738b (a community analysis; the steps are inferred from its metrics list).
- **Invite:** Friends are added by email or via synced contacts; the core loop needs a second person with an account (help: https://splitwise.uservoice.com/knowledgebase/articles/1088920-how-do-i-use-splitwise , third-party guides). Invite copy: not found.
- **Proposed (not published) activation metrics:** 10 expenses in 7 days, 3 groups, 15 friends, 2 settlements in 30 days. These are the GrowthX author's guesses, not Splitwise numbers.

## Gaps

## Patterns worth copying / avoiding for Snacklist
1. **Copy: first list in seconds (Bring!).** Show the list screen with suggested staples on the first run; no questionnaire. (screensdesign Bring! link)
2. **Copy: never land on a blank screen (Todoist welcome step).** Offer a few tappable suggestions or an example, and remove them after use.
3. **Avoid: mandatory account before any value (Bring!, AnyList).** Flagged as drop-off risk. Consider value-first (add items), then sign-up. Weigh against RLS and household design, which need a user.
4. **Copy: one household identity (OurGroceries advice to share one email).** Snacklist's invite-by-link household is the better version; say "one household" in the copy.
5. **Copy: ask to invite right after the first items exist (Bring! at ~28 s).** The invite makes sense once there is something to share; offer a link, not an email field.
6. **Avoid: invite buried in a list footer (AnyList: scroll to the bottom).** Use a visible empty-state CTA and a persistent entry.
7. **Avoid: invite flows needing the invitee's existing account or same email (AnyList, OurGroceries, Listonic by email).** The link should land the invitee in the household in a few steps; show the inviter's name.
8. **Copy: keep first-run permissions out (none in Bring!).** Ask for notifications only after a reason shows up, such as a partner adding an item. Contacts (Splitwise) is not needed with links.
9. **Copy: no onboarding paywall (Bring!, AnyList, OurGroceries all free-first).** Show the per-household premium when a premium feature is used.
10. **Copy: contextual tips from use, not a tour.** AnyList documents autocomplete and paste-a-list as text only. Snacklist can surface the paste-in, Siri/Alexa and store-order hints after a few items.
11. **Measure our own activation.** No benchmark exists; define it ourselves (e.g. 5 items plus a second member added within 7 days) and test it with the PostHog gate insights.
12. **Avoid copying Splitwise's "needs a second person" cliff.** A list has value alone; the invite should enhance, not block.
