# Consent text version 2026-11-11

The analytics consent screen as Snacklist showed it from job 178 until [2026-11-14](2026-11-14.md) (Legal N-146: the text did not name the Siri events, so the server stopped counting its yes). The text of
[2026-11-10](2026-11-10.md) with one change: the usage data get examples, „(etwa zu Einträgen,
Haushalten und der Einrichtung und welche Tipps du siehst)“ / "(for example about items, households
and setup, and which tips you see)", since the app now sends household and setup events and the ids
of the tips it shows (Legal Review job 178, N-125: the policy promises a new ask when the notice
covers new data). The wording of the addition was approved by Peter on 11 November 2026 with PR #363. Stored with every decision as
`consent_text_version = '2026-11-11'` (`app/lib/analytics/consent_version.dart`,
`analytics_consents.consent_text_version`). The app asks everyone again: a yes under 2026-11-10 or
older counts as no decision on the device. The server counts a yes for its own events (Alexa,
voice, purchases, integration lock) only under 2026-11-11 as well (`CURRENT_CONSENT_TEXT_VERSIONS`,
`supabase/functions/_shared/consent_version.ts`): the policy promises to send nothing after a notice
that names new data until the user answers again, so a yes under 2026-11-10 or Hummlist's
2026-11-01 no longer counts there either. `app/test/analytics/consent_version_test.dart`
checks that the app's strings are word for word this text.

## DE

Title: Darf Snacklist Nutzungsdaten erfassen?

Text (`analyticsConsentBody`, then a space, the link and a full stop):

> Snacklist (Anbieter: Peter Trost) möchte verstehen, welche Funktionen genutzt werden, Fehler finden, messen, ob sich Snacklist trägt, und neue Funktionen schrittweise freischalten. Dafür speichert Snacklist eine zufällige Kennung auf diesem Gerät und sendet damit pseudonyme Nutzungsdaten (etwa zu Einträgen, Haushalten und der Einrichtung und welche Tipps du siehst), Fehlerberichte, die Kennung deines Haushalts und technische Angaben zu Gerät oder Browser an unseren Dienstleister PostHog (Server in der EU). Listeninhalte, Namen und E-Mail-Adressen werden nie gesendet. Du kannst jederzeit unter Mehr → Nutzungsdaten widerrufen. Mehr in der [Datenschutzerklärung](https://snacklist.app/datenschutz).

Buttons, equal weight, none pre-selected: „Nicht erlauben“ · „Erlauben“

## EN

Title: May Snacklist collect usage data?

Text:

> Snacklist (run by Peter Trost) wants to see which features are used, find bugs, measure whether Snacklist pays for itself and switch new features on step by step. For that, Snacklist stores a random ID on this device and uses it to send pseudonymous usage data (for example about items, households and setup, and which tips you see), error reports, your household's ID and technical details of your device or browser to our service provider PostHog (servers in the EU). List contents, names and e-mail addresses are never sent. You can withdraw any time under More → Usage data. More in the [privacy policy](https://snacklist.app/en/privacy).

Buttons, equal weight, none pre-selected: "Don't allow" · "Allow"
