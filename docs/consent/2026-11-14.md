# Consent text version 2026-11-14 (current)

The analytics consent screen as Snacklist shows it from the Legal Review N-146 change on. The text
of [2026-11-11](2026-11-11.md) with one change: the examples of the usage data also name Siri,
„(etwa zu Einträgen, Haushalten und der Einrichtung, welche Tipps du siehst und wie du Siri
nutzt)“ / "(for example about items, households and setup, which tips you see and how you use
Siri)", since the server sends the events `siri_add` and `siri_read` (privacy policy §9, N-146),
which 2026-11-11 did not name. The wording is drafted by Engineering; Legal gave its GO on 2026-11-14
with the N-145..N-155 privacy PR. Stored with every decision as `consent_text_version = '2026-11-14'`
(`app/lib/analytics/consent_version.dart`, `analytics_consents.consent_text_version`). The app asks
everyone again: a yes under 2026-11-11 or older counts as no decision on the device. The server
counts a yes for its own events (Alexa, voice, purchases, integration lock) only under 2026-11-14
as well (`CURRENT_CONSENT_TEXT_VERSIONS`, `supabase/functions/_shared/consent_version.ts`).
`app/test/analytics/consent_version_test.dart` checks that the app's strings are word for word this
text.

## DE

Title: Darf Snacklist Nutzungsdaten erfassen?

Text (`analyticsConsentBody`, then a space, the link and a full stop):

> Snacklist (Anbieter: Peter Trost) möchte verstehen, welche Funktionen genutzt werden, Fehler finden, messen, ob sich Snacklist trägt, und neue Funktionen schrittweise freischalten. Dafür speichert Snacklist eine zufällige Kennung auf diesem Gerät und sendet damit pseudonyme Nutzungsdaten (etwa zu Einträgen, Haushalten und der Einrichtung, welche Tipps du siehst und wie du Siri nutzt), Fehlerberichte, die Kennung deines Haushalts und technische Angaben zu Gerät oder Browser an unseren Dienstleister PostHog (Server in der EU). Listeninhalte, Namen und E-Mail-Adressen werden nie gesendet. Du kannst jederzeit unter Mehr → Nutzungsdaten widerrufen. Mehr in der [Datenschutzerklärung](https://snacklist.app/datenschutz).

Buttons, equal weight, none pre-selected: „Nicht erlauben“ · „Erlauben“

## EN

Title: May Snacklist collect usage data?

Text:

> Snacklist (run by Peter Trost) wants to see which features are used, find bugs, measure whether Snacklist pays for itself and switch new features on step by step. For that, Snacklist stores a random ID on this device and uses it to send pseudonymous usage data (for example about items, households and setup, which tips you see and how you use Siri), error reports, your household's ID and technical details of your device or browser to our service provider PostHog (servers in the EU). List contents, names and e-mail addresses are never sent. You can withdraw any time under More → Usage data. More in the [privacy policy](https://snacklist.app/en/privacy).

Buttons, equal weight, none pre-selected: "Don't allow" · "Allow"
